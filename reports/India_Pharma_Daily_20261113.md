## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-12】NITI Aayog releases PLI impact assessment across 14 sectors; pharmaceuticals rated "notable progress"** — The report, prepared with IIM Ahmedabad, grades all 14 notified PLI sectors into four buckets — on track, partial impact, insignificant evidence, data unavailable. Pharmaceuticals, along with mobile manufacturing (LSEM), food processing, telecom, white goods, automobiles and drones, is cited for notable progress in investment, output and capacity creation; about 70% of targeted segments are on track. It recommends deeper MSME participation and harmonised data systems. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2335594)【Source: PIB PRID 2335594, 2026-11-12; NITI Aayog report, 2026-11】

**【2026-11-10】Medical devices PLI extended to 2035-36 with ₹2,700 crore outlay reopened for applications, focus shifts to critical components** — The scheme is extended and fresh applications invited, with about ₹2,700 crore (≈USD 288 million) available. The minimum sales threshold has been discontinued and the minimum investment requirement trimmed; applicants get three years to set up. Among the four target segments, critical components (TS2) carry a tapering incentive averaging about 9%. Since 2020-21 the scheme has localised more than 60 high-end devices, with production valued above ₹16,000 crore. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2334770)【Source: PIB PRID 2334770, 2026-11-10 (Department of Pharmaceuticals)】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-13】Supreme Court reserves verdict on 30% trade-margin cap for all medicines, putting DPCO 2013 framework under strain** — A special bench reserved judgment after hearings closed on 10-27. The court questioned why 82%–90% of formulations sit outside NLEM ceiling prices and are bound only by the 10% annual MRP increase, called NPPA ineffective against outsized mark-ups, and declined to refer the matter to an expert committee; the Centre argued on rural availability and cold-chain costs. The ruling will decide whether a uniform 30% trade-margin cap applies across all medicines, affecting the margin structure of 1.24 million retail chemists. — Source: [Pharmabiz](https://pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191880&sid=1)【Source: hearing of 2026-10-27, Kishan Chand Jain v. EMRB (with Sanjay Kulshrestha v. Union of India)】

**【2026-11-12】India's antibiotic import dependence on China in FY2025-26: erythromycin 99%, chloramphenicol 95%** — Calculated at HS six-digit level, China supplied 81% of India's penicillin imports, 86% of rifampicin, 95% of chloramphenicol, 99% of erythromycin, 76% of cefadroxil and 85% of streptomycin. Scholars note the dependence is concentrated in KSMs and intermediates rather than finished APIs, a layer that trade data does not separate; India has supported APIs and KSMs through PLI since 2020. — Source: [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/economy/building-at-home-yet-buying-more-from-china-indias-manufacturing-push-meets-an-import-paradox/3096139/)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-11-12】ALKEM Q2 FY27 revenue up 13.8%; Mandva API plant clears FDA inspection with zero observations** — Revenue from operations ₹4,551.99 crore (≈USD 485 million), up 13.8% YoY; EBITDA ₹1,025.2 crore at a 22.5% margin (down 50 bps); net profit ₹675.3 crore, down 11.7% on a higher effective tax rate. Indications cover neovascular (wet) age-related macular degeneration, macular oedema after retinal vein occlusion, diabetic macular oedema, proliferative diabetic retinopathy and choroidal neovascularisation. — Source: [Lupin press release](https://www.lupin.com/media/press-releases/lupin-announces-european-launch-of-biosimilar-ranibizumab)【Source: company announcement 2026-11-12】
