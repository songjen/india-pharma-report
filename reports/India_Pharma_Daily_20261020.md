## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-19】India–New Zealand FTA enters into force on 20 October; zero duty for pharma and medical device exports (updated)** — The Commerce Ministry announced that the India–New Zealand free trade agreement takes effect on 20 October; New Zealand has eliminated customs duties on all Indian export tariff lines (previously up to 10%), with chemicals and pharmaceuticals among the beneficiaries, while pharma and medical devices also gain simplified and streamlined regulatory procedures. New Zealand committed to facilitating USD 20 billion of investment in India. — Source: [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2325286&lang=1&reg=3)【Source: PIB PRID 2325286, 2026-10-19】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-19】Q2 FY27 preview: input cost inflation and US competition to squeeze margins; Aurobindo's 6-APA/Pen-G ramp is the key monitorable** — Brokerage previews point to diverging September-quarter results: Sun, Aurobindo, Torrent and Divi's lead, while Dr. Reddy's, Lupin and Cipla are weighed down by a high base in high-margin US products and intensifying competition; raw material and R&D costs are set to compress margins slightly. For Aurobindo, the key monitorables are the Lannett integration and the ramp-up of its 6-APA/Pen-G facility. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/financial-performance/pharma-q2-outlook-pricing-pressure-us-competition-to-weigh-on-select-players/135037972)【Source: ET Intelligence Group earnings preview, 2026-10-19】

**【2026-10-19】Bajaj Healthcare Q2 revenue up 18.9%, API exports up 28.5%; first Indian firm cleared for Cenobamate API** — Quarterly revenue was ₹175.9 crore with net profit of ₹14.1 crore (+26.9%); API exports of ₹62.6 crore (+28.5%) were the main driver, while domestic API sales fell year on year to ₹71.6 crore. The company is the first in India to receive DCGI approval to manufacture and market Cenobamate API and tablets in six strengths, and plans a 250 kg/year peptide plant by March 2027 and an oncology API plant in Q2 FY28, targeting ₹900–1,000 crore revenue by FY29. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/bajaj-healthcare-q2-profit-jumps-269-as-revenue-climbs-189-22184)【Source: company release 2026-10-17】

**【2026-10-19】NATCO launches generic carboplatin injection in the US, a market worth about USD 27.8 million** — Through its US subsidiary and in partnership with Teyro Labs, NATCO launched a generic version of Paraplatin injection to help ease US shortages; industry sales data put the US market for the product at about USD 27.8 million for the 12 months to June 2026. — Source: [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/amp/news/industry/pharma/natco-pharma-launches-generic-carboplatin-injection-in-us-181634)【Source: company release 2026-10-19】
