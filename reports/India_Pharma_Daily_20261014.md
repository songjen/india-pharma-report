## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-13】Supreme Court orders Centre to form expert committee within two weeks on a legal framework for pharma marketing regulation** — In a public-interest case on unethical pharmaceutical marketing, the Supreme Court directed the Union government to constitute an expert committee **within two weeks** to examine an appropriate regulatory or statutory framework to control unethical pharma marketing; the committee must consult petitioners, intervenors and other stakeholders and submit its recommendations **within two months of its first meeting**. The case was filed in 2021; the government had maintained existing rules sufficed, but this time conceded that the current mechanism is inadequate to deter companies from influencing prescribing through inducements. The Uniform Code for Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices (UCPMP) 2024 requires ethics officers and marketing-expenditure disclosure, but was issued by administrative direction and lacks statutory investigative and penalty powers. — Source: [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/amp/health/story/supreme-court-pharma-marketing-rules-centre-stronger-legal-curbs-expert-committee-3015694-2026-10-13)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-13】Piramal Pharma says China raw-material exposure cut to single digits** — Chairperson Nandini Piramal said at the Bloomberg New Economy Forum in New Delhi that the company had worked hard to limit its exposure to China and has "gotten it down to single digits". Over half of revenue comes from CDMO operations and North America accounts for 40%; the company is also affected by US Section 232 tariffs of 100% on many patented drugs and associated ingredients. She said West Asia turmoil has raised raw-material costs and that India's manufacturing talent and capacity "is different from China", positioning it as a trusted supplier to the US and Europe. Former UK Prime Minister Rishi Sunak said on the same panel that single-country dependence on key starting materials and APIs constitutes a new supply-chain risk. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/piramal-pharma-cuts-exposure-to-chinese-raw-materials-to-reduce-supply-risk-126101300747_1.html)【Source: Bloomberg New Economy Forum remarks 2026-10-13】

**【2026-10-13】Indian specialty-chem startup NuSynth raises ₹36.5 crore seed to build AI chemical synthesis platform** — Bengaluru-based NuSynth Technologies raised ₹36.5 crore (about USD 4.3 mn) in a seed round co-led by Avaana Capital and Shastra VC. Its AI platform converts experimental data into a proprietary knowledge base to accelerate molecule selection, experimentation and commercial scale-up; proceeds will fund development of its first proprietary processes, scale-up capabilities and team. Management said India has built strength in manufacturing established chemistry and that the next chapter will be defined by innovating on the underlying chemistry itself. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/start-ups/nusynth-tech-raises-36-cr-in-seed-round-to-build-ai-driven-chemical-synthesis-platform/134916133) · [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/nusynth-to-build-chemical-process-innovation-platform-with-new-capital/)【Source: company funding announcement 2026-10-13】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-13】ZYDUS LIFESCIENCES' Keytruda biosimilar application accepted for review by US FDA** — Zydus and Formycon AG announced that the US FDA has accepted the Biologics License Application (BLA) for FYB206, a proposed biosimilar to Keytruda (pembrolizumab) — according to the companies, the **first publicly announced FDA acceptance of a Keytruda biosimilar application**. The BLA was submitted in Q3 2026 and a decision is expected in **Q3 2027**; a dossier has also been filed with Health Canada, and Zydus holds exclusive commercialisation rights for the US and Canada. Keytruda recorded more than USD 31 bn in global sales in 2025. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/zydus-keytruda-biosimilar-application-accepted-for-review-by-fda/134915388) · [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/regulatory/fda-accepts-zydus-and-formycons-first-of-its-kind-keytruda-biosimilar-for-review-22136)【Source: company announcement 2026-10-13】
