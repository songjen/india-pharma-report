## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-08-22】Health Ministry bans chlorpheniramine + phenylephrine FDCs for children under 4 under Section 26A**
On August 22, the Union Health Ministry issued a notification restricting *all* fixed-dose combinations (FDCs) containing Chlorpheniramine Maleate and Phenylephrine Hydrochloride: they are now prohibited for use in children below four years of age, and manufacturers must display the warning "Fixed Dose Combination shall not be used in children below four years of age" on labels, package inserts and promotional material. An earlier notification (April 15, 2025) restricted only certain combinations; the latest order extends this to every formulation containing both ingredients. The decision follows recommendations of an expert committee and the Drugs Technical Advisory Board (DTAB), was issued under Section 26A of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, and is effective from the date of Gazette publication. It is part of the Supreme Court-directed FDC review (16 irrational FDCs were already banned in June). The move forces label/packaging/promotional overhauls across a wide range of paediatric cold-and-congestion OTC products (including cough syrups). — Sources: [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/pharmaceuticals/government-restricts-fixed-dose-combinations-of-chlorpheniramine-maleate-and-phenylephrine-hydrochloride-for-children-under-four/articleshow/133424446.cms) · [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/govt-restricts-2-compound-fixed-drug-combinations-for-children-under-4-126082200196_1.html)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-08-22】Reactor blast at Hazelo Lab (Telangana) API/intermediate plant kills 2, injures 9**
At around 10:27 pm on August 21, a reactor exploded and triggered a major fire at Hazelo Lab Pvt Ltd, an API/bulk-drug-intermediate manufacturing unit at Dotigudem village, Pochampally mandal, Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri district, Telangana (~50 km from Hyderabad), during a night-shift changeover. Two workers died (from Nalgonda and Khammam districts) and 9–11 others were injured, some seriously (including workers from neighbouring Andhra Pradesh).
