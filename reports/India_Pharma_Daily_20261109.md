## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-04】DGTR extends questionnaire deadline to 22 Nov in 6-APA and Penicillin G anti-dumping cases; no PCN methodology adopted** — The two investigations were initiated on 25 September and 24 September respectively. As no interested party filed comments on the scope of the product under consideration (PUC) or proposed a PCN methodology by the extended date of 23 October, DGTR confirmed that the PUC scope remains as notified at initiation and that no PCN methodology is adopted; the deadline for questionnaire responses was extended from 7 November to 22 November, with no further extension to be granted. — Source: [DGTR 6-APA case](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-6-amino-penicillanic-acid-6-apa) · [DGTR Penicillin G case](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-penicillin-g-and-its-salts-peoples)【Source: DGTR F.No. 6/58/2026-DGTR and 6/56/2026-DGTR, 2026-11-04】

**【2026-11-03】Health Ministry proposes to expand authorised drug import gateways by 10 inland ports, an SEZ and an international airport** — A proposed amendment to Rule 43A of the Drugs Rules, 1945 would add inland container depots at Sonipat, Piyala and Harsaru (Haryana), Dadri (Uttar Pradesh), Gandhidham/Mundra (Gujarat), Sanath Nagar and Thimmapur (Hyderabad), Whitefield (Bengaluru), the Bommasandra SEZ in Karnataka, and Noida International Airport for air imports, shortening inland transport distances and clearance time for imported drugs and devices. — Source: [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2331879)【Source: PIB PRID 2331879, 2026-11-03】

**【2026-11-03】Industry seeks CDSCO clarity on whether batch-wise NOC for high-risk solvents applies to export-oriented units** — A CDSCO circular dated 27 October requires manufacturers, vendors and suppliers of ten pharma-grade solvents — including glycerine, propylene glycol, sorbitol, maltitol, polyethylene glycols, polysorbates and ethyl alcohol — to register on ONDLS and upload batch CoAs and distribution trails, with no batch to be distributed before a system-generated NOC is issued; a companion directive requires batch-wise testing of all raw materials including APIs and excipients plus stronger vendor qualification. Industry says the order does not distinguish domestic from export use and could disrupt solvent supply, and has asked for clarification; CDSCO has not responded. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/news/pharma-industry-seeks-clarity-on-cdsco-solvent-order-s-export-ambit-126110301630_1.html)【Source: CDSCO circulars dated 2026-10-27】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-07】SHILPA MEDICARE posts record quarterly API sales at about 47% of revenue** — Revenue rose 43% to ₹529.12 crore and net profit 80% to ₹79.2 crore; EBITDA was ₹142 crore at a 26.8% margin (29.3% a year earlier). **【2026-11-07】SUPRIYA LIFESCIENCE Q2 net profit falls 42% as API revenue grows only 3.2%** — Revenue was ₹206.29 crore and net profit ₹29.25 crore; total expenses rose 27% on higher material, power and operating costs, compressing the EBITDA margin to 22% from about 36%. The company launched two inhalation anaesthetic products from its Ambernath site during the quarter. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/supriya-lifescience-q2-fy27-pat-drops-42-to-rs-292-cr-22346)【Source: company results announcement 2026-11-07】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-11-07】DIVI'S Q2 net profit up 64% to ₹1,127 crore; exports about 90% of sales** — Revenue rose 35.4% to ₹3,676 crore and profit before tax 65%. On a standalone basis exports were about 90% of sales, with Europe and North America together contributing about 75%; custom synthesis and generic APIs both grew at double digits, three dedicated projects are near commercialisation, a new 3,000-litre solid-phase peptide synthesis line has been added, and capex is guided at about ₹2,000 crore a year over the next two to three years. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/stocks/earnings/divis-labs-q2-results-pat-surges-64-yoy-to-rs-1127-crore-revenue-jumps-35/articleshow/135632058.cms)【Source: company announcement 2026-11-07 (board approval)】

**【2026-11-06】GLAND PHARMA Q2 net profit up 58%, CDMO up 31% at 45% of revenue** — Revenue rose 23% to ₹1,833.6 crore, net profit was ₹290.8 crore and EBITDA ₹480.4 crore, up 53%. The US grew 42% and accounted for 64% of revenue, Europe grew 30%; the company filed 11 ANDAs and received 10 approvals in the quarter, taking cumulative filings to 399. The VSEZ site closed its FDA inspection with zero observations, while the Dundigal site concluded an inspection in October 2026 with four procedural observations. — Source: [Gland Pharma](https://glandpharma.com/images/Press_Release_Q2_FY27.pdf)【Source: company results announcement 2026-11-06 (board approval)】
