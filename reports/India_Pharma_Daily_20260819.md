## 1. Government Pharma Policy

### 【2026-08-12】CDSCO orders nationwide ban on unapproved infertility drug enclomiphene; states must report enforcement action (ET Pharma/Ingredients South Asia)
The Drugs Controller General of India (DCGI) has written to state drug regulators flagging that certain manufacturers are **producing and marketing enclomiphene (a selective estrogen receptor modulator that stimulates natural testosterone and sperm production) and its combinations without mandatory approval from the Central Licensing Authority (CLA/CDSCO)**. Under Rules 2(1)(w), 19 and 80 of the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, enclomiphene is classified as a "New Drug" requiring central clearance in Form CT-21 before commercial manufacture or sale; **state licensing authorities have no legal mandate to grant such permissions**, making any ongoing trade illegal under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940. The DCGI directed states to immediately identify manufacturers, marketers, distributors and retailers, take strict enforcement action including suspension/cancellation of licenses, and submit Action Taken Reports (ATRs) promptly. In mid-August, the All India Organisation of Chemists and Druggists (AIOCD) further issued an urgent compliance circular (Ref: APH/AIOCD/511/2026) instructing manufacturers, distributors and stockists to **halt distribution and immediately recall non-compliant enclomiphene stocks**; products without central clearance will be returned by retailers for full credit. This is CDSCO's second crackdown on unauthorized products following its Aug 10 circular (full review required for new drugs not yet approved globally), signalling a marked escalation in enforcement against **unapproved / out-of-scope medicines**.
