## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-25】DGTR initiates anti-dumping investigation into 6-APA from China PR and the European Union** — Acting on an application by domestic producer Qule Pharma (a subsidiary of Aurobindo's antibiotics arm, operating a 6-APA plant in Kakinada SEZ), the Directorate General of Trade Remedies has initiated an anti-dumping investigation into 6-Amino Penicillanic Acid (a direct derivative of Penicillin-G and a key intermediate for β-lactam antibiotics such as amoxicillin and ampicillin), alleging that low-priced imports from China PR and the EU cause material retardation of the establishment of the domestic industry. Period of investigation: 2025-04-01 to 2026-03-31; customs classification 2941 10 50. — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-6-amino-penicillanic-acid-6-apa)【Source: DGTR File No. 6/58/2026-DGTR · SETU AD/OI/059/2026, initiated 2026-09-25】

**【2026-09-24】DGTR initiates investigations into Amoxicillin Trihydrate and Montelukast Sodium from China PR on the same day** — The Amoxicillin Trihydrate case was filed by Apitoria Pharma (Aurobindo's API subsidiary), with customs classification 2941 10 30 and a period of investigation of 2025-04-01 to 2026-03-31; a separate anti-dumping investigation into Montelukast Sodium originating in or exported from China PR was initiated the same day. Both cases are at an early stage and interested parties must file submissions within the prescribed timelines. — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-amoxycillin-trihydrate-originating)【Source: DGTR File No. 6/59/2026-DGTR · SETU AD/OI/060/2026, initiated 2026-09-24】

**【2026-09-25】PIB reports five-year PLI outcomes: five bulk drug projects commissioned, yet China dependence rises to 73.7%** — Under the Bulk Drugs PLI, investment reached ₹5,210.74 crore with 39 projects covering 28 APIs/KSMs commissioned, including Lyfius Pharma's 15,000 MTPA Penicillin-G plant (₹2,270 crore investment, 2,353 direct jobs), Kinvan's 400 MTPA potassium clavulanate facility and Meghmani's 13,500 MTPA para-aminophenol plant; the Pharmaceuticals PLI has attracted ₹46,744 crore in investment and ₹2.57 lakh crore in exports. However, India's imports of APIs, KSMs and drug intermediates from China rose from ₹18,646 crore in FY21 to ₹27,032 crore in FY25, with China's share climbing from 68.2% to 73.7%. — Source: [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2314891) · [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/healthcare/pharma-pli-attracts-over-rs-46700-crore-investments-but-china-still-dominate-api-imports/4347513)【Source: PIB PRID 2314891, released 2026-09-25】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-25】Pharmexcil Chairman calls dependence on Chinese APIs "unsustainable", import pricing erodes local viability** — Speaking at the ETPharma Future-Ready Supply Chain Summit, Pharmexcil Chairman Namit Joshi said India's reliance on imported APIs, key starting materials and intermediates remains unsustainable; although dependence has eased from the levels seen around 2020, aggressive pricing of imported inputs continues to undermine the commercial viability of domestic manufacturing. He called for identifying import "blind spots" and stronger data integration, and warned against over-concentration of exports in the US and Europe, advocating diversification into Latin America, Africa and Southeast Asia. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/supply-chain-and-logistics/indias-pharma-medtech-resilience-hinges-on-critical-localisation-not-complete-self-reliance-experts/134479742)【Source: ETPharma Future-Ready Supply Chain Summit 2026, 2026-09-25】

**【2026-09-26】"Virtual API" firm HRV Pharma prepares IPO, targeting over ₹800 crore** — The company owns no plants and aggregates capacity across 54 partner manufacturing sites (including Metrochem, Arene Lifesciences and Shodhana). It guides to ₹600 crore revenue and ₹80 crore net profit this fiscal year, with a FY28 revenue target of ₹1,000 crore; it has recently committed ₹150 crore to peptide and high-potency oncology API capacity and is exploring acquisitions to secure European market access and finished-dosage filings. Industry observers note India's pharma capacity utilisation is only 45–50%, leaving room for asset-light models. — Source: [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/ipos/fpos/virtual-pharma-api-firm-hrv-preparing-for-ipo-drhp-likely-in-h1-of-2027-sources/amp_articleshow/134491851.cms)

**【2026-09-25】NGL Fine Chem starts Phase II production at Tarapur** — The company announced that Phase II of its greenfield Tarapur facility has commenced commercial production (Phase I has been operating since 11 March 2025), expanding API and intermediate capacity. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news)
