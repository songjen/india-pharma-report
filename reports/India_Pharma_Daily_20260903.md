## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-02】India plans to amend the Drugs Rules, 1945 to force a legal distinction between biologics and synthetics — aimed squarely at dual-route molecules like semaglutide** — Citing two government officials and documents reviewed by Mint, the government plans to add a statutory definition of "biological products" (aligned with WHO standards) to the Drugs Rules, 1945 and the New Drugs and Clinical Trials (NDCT) Rules, 2019, and to mandate that manufacturers declare on licence applications and packaging whether an active ingredient is of "synthetic origin," "r-DNA origin," "human origin" or "animal origin." The move would unify licensing governance across Form 28 (synthetic) and Form 28D (biological) — today multiple firms hold both licences for the same molecule at the same facility — and would update Schedules C and C1 to cover biological-origin products across parenteral and non-parenteral forms. The intervention targets molecules such as semaglutide that can be produced by either chemical synthesis or recombinant DNA technology. India's biologics market is estimated at **$13.3 billion** (roughly 22% of a ~$60 billion pharma market); CareEdge projects Indian semaglutide demand to reach **$347.5 million** by 2035. Following the IPC's late-August move to draft a semaglutide monograph, this is the second escalation of "GLP-1 dual-route regulation"; it directly affects registration-file classification and labelling compliance for API/peptide suppliers exporting to India — track the draft notification window. — Source: [Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/news/world/how-one-man-s-prediction-fueled-fears-of-a-2027-taiwan-invasion-11773027368982.html)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-08-31】Cipla secures exclusive licence for Sino Biopharma's HER2 bispecific ADC (TQB2102) across seven emerging markets; deal value up to $123 million** — Sino Biopharma announced that its subsidiary Chia Tai Tianqing (CTTQ) has entered an exclusive licence-and-supply agreement with Cipla for TQB2102 (rolditamig deuderuxtecan), a next-generation HER2-targeted bispecific antibody-drug conjugate. Cipla gets exclusive development and commercialisation rights in India, South Africa and five other emerging markets (7 countries); CTTQ retains manufacturing and supply, and Sino Biopharma is entitled to an upfront payment plus development, regulatory and sales milestones of up to **$123 million**, plus double-digit royalties on annual net sales. TQB2102 holds three breakthrough-therapy designations from China's CDE and is in multiple Phase III studies (HER2-low/positive breast cancer, colorectal and biliary tract cancer). This is the second regional out-licence for TQB2102 — the two deals have brought in roughly **$30 million** in upfront and milestone payments combined. The "regional out-licensing with retained manufacturing" structure offers a reference model for Chinese API/formulation players assessing India partnerships. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/biopharma/cipla-inks-exclusive-licence-deal-with-sino-biopharma-for-her2-targeted-adc-21651)

**【2026-09-01】Gland Pharma clears USFDA routine GMP inspection at Visakhapatnam sterile oncology + API facilities with zero Form 483 observations** — Gland Pharma disclosed post-market on September 1 that the USFDA concluded its routine GMP inspection of the VSEZ Sterile Oncology Formulations facility and the API facility in Visakhapatnam, conducted August 24 – September 1, 2026, with **zero Form 483 observations** — the best possible outcome — removing regulatory uncertainty for exports from both sites. Against a backdrop of frequent India compliance events (Eugia warning letter, Cipla Pithampur 7 observations, etc.), the clean audit provides a positive benchmark for India's complex-injectables/CDMO quality capability. — Source: [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/pharma-stocks-today-caplin-point-gland-pharma-nephrocare-health-services-share-price-usfda-gmp-acquisition-impact-19981919.htm)

**【2026-09-02】Delhi High Court orders Sun Pharma unit to hand semaglutide tablet samples to Novo Nordisk for testing; next hearing November 4** — In Novo Nordisk's patent suit against Sun Pharma Laboratories over oral semaglutide tablets, the Delhi High Court directed Sun to provide tablet samples so Novo can test whether they infringe its patent (Sun argues its SNAC absorption-enhancer levels fall outside Novo's patented range). The court questioned Sun's credibility — it had earlier said it was "very far away" from launch, then filed a commercial-manufacture application within three weeks, without disclosing a February application — and listed the case for hearing on **November 4**. Following the August 11 pre-launch-injunction order (court approval required before any launch), this procedural step further delays Indian generic oral GLP-1 entry; the SNAC-patent-design-around case is closely watched by peptide/GLP-1 API supply chains. — Source: [Informist](https://informistmedia.com/MoneyWire/58908/Semaglutide-Patent-Row-Give-sample-semaglutide-tablets-to-Novo-for-testing-HC-to-Sun-Pharma-arm)
