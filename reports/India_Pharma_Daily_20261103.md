## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-02】NPPA expands revised-MRP list for anti-cancer drugs to 1,066 brands; 876 cut (update)** — The data cut-off moved from 23 October to 2 November and the number of entries rose from 643 to 1,066; 876 were reduced, 190 unchanged and none raised, with an average cut of 50.86% among reduced entries and 41.80% across the full sample, still effective 9 November. Most generic cuts exceed 80% (abiraterone 250 mg from ₹2,718.75 to ₹477), while originator products from Eli Lilly, Servier and Astellas all held their prices. — Source: [NPPA](https://nppa.gov.in/en)【Source: NPPA revised MRP report 2026-11-02, 77 pages, data as of 2026-11-02, effective 2026-11-09】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-02】WOCKHARDT Q2 revenue up 24% to ₹939 crore as pre-launch Zaynich spend weighs on profit** — Revenue rose from ₹782 crore to ₹939 crore, EBITDA was ₹165 crore and net profit fell from ₹82 crore to ₹73 crore; excluding pre-commercialisation spending on Zaynich (zidebactam-cefepime), EBITDA rose 25% to ₹201 crore and net profit 19% to ₹109 crore. Emerging markets contributed ₹310 crore with H1 up 53% to ₹574 crore, and biosimilars rose 68% to ₹252 crore. In the quarter Miqnaf won DCGI clearance without the medical-college-only supply condition, and Foviscu completed Phase 3 with an India NDA filed. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/financial-performance/wockhardt-revenue-rises-24-to-939-cr-in-q2-pre-launch-zaynich-spending-dents-profit/135464201)【Source: company results release 2026-11-02】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-11-02】DR. REDDY'S wins FDA tentative approval for long-acting lenacapavir across 120 low- and middle-income countries** — Indicated for HIV-1 pre-exposure prophylaxis as a twice-yearly injection for adults and adolescents weighing at least 35 kg, approved under PEPFAR; following a October 2024 voluntary licence from Gilead, development was accelerated from September 2025 with Unitaid, Wits RHI and CHAI at US$40 per person per year. First supplies are expected by end-2026, with broader rollout in 2027. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/dr-reddys-bags-tentative-usfda-nod-for-twice-yearly-hiv-injectable-lenacapavir-across-120-nations-22303)【Source: company announcement 2026-11-02】
