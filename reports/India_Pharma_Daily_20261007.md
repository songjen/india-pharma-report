## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-30】DGTR initiates mid-term review of anti-dumping duty on Sodium Cyanide from China and the EU** — India's Directorate General of Trade Remedies has launched a mid-term review (MTR) of the existing anti-dumping duty on Sodium Cyanide (NaCN) originating in or exported from China PR and the European Union (notification AD/MTR/006/2026). Sodium cyanide is used for gold and silver extraction and metal heat treatment, and is also an upstream input for insecticides, dyes, pigments, bulk drugs, agrochemicals, nylon and pharmaceutical intermediates. The review will examine whether the duty remains adequate to offset injury. — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-ongoing-investigation)【Source: DGTR MTR notification AD/MTR/006/2026, 2026-09-30】

**【2026-10-06】GST Council meeting rescheduled to 8 October; relief proposed for pharma exports and rare-disease drugs** — The 57th GST Council meeting has been moved to 8 October. Proposals on the table include treating "work on goods" performed by Indian pharma units for overseas clients — covering clinical trials, testing and analysis, and contract manufacturing — as exports taxed at nil GST instead of 18%; allowing refunds of input tax credit on related inputs, with 90% released automatically after a risk check; and exempting imported medicines and specialised foods for seven rare diseases from GST. — Source: [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/gst-council-likely-to-allow-itc-on-group-insurance-ease-rates-on-rare-disease-drugs-and-pharma-services-14045831.html)·[Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/gst-council-may-give-export-status-to-clinical-trials-for-overseas-sponsors-126100500886_1.html)【Source: GST Council Secretariat meeting notice (2026-10-05); 57th meeting on 2026-10-08】

**【2026-10-06】Centre to seek legal opinion on central licensing of all sterile drugs** — The Drugs Consultative Committee (DCC) discussed a proposal to bring all sterile drug products under the Central Licence Approving Authority (CLAA) scheme and recommended forming a sub-committee to examine feasibility and obtain a legal opinion first. The proposal would move small-volume parenterals, injectables and ophthalmic preparations currently licensed by states into the central framework. It follows the Supreme Court declining to interfere with a Himachal Pradesh High Court ruling that CDSCO drugs inspectors lack independent enforcement powers under Chapter IV of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/pharmaceuticals/centre-may-seek-legal-view-on-licensing-of-sterile-drugs/articleshow/134715676.cms)【Source: DCC meeting record; reported 2026-10-06】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-06】Pharma accounts for 88.8% of Indian healthcare M&A value in September 2026** — Equirus data show pharmaceuticals accounted for 88.8% of healthcare deal value in September 2026, while hospitals and healthtech led private-equity activity. **【2026-10-06】Aragen commissions integrated high-potency API platform OneHP** — The company began operations at OneHP, a high-potency development and manufacturing platform at its Nacharam campus in Hyderabad. Spanning about 15,000 sq ft at OEB-6, the single line is designed for 500–600 kg of API a year, covering milligram-scale discovery through multi-kilogram cGMP manufacturing, and can handle cytotoxic oncology payloads, laying the ground for future antibody-drug conjugate and peptide-drug conjugate expansion. The firm has spent more than ₹1,000 crore of capex in the last three years. — Source: [Aragen](https://www.aragen.com/news/aragen-life-sciences-to-begin-operations-at-onehp-an-integrated-high-potency-platform-in-hyderabad/)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-06】

**【2026-10-06】One killed as drones hit Indian pharma plant in Ukraine** — A pharmaceutical plant of India's Kusum Healthcare in Ukraine was hit by Russian drones, killing one person. The incident highlights the geopolitical exposure of Indian pharma's overseas manufacturing assets. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/1-dead-as-russian-drones-hit-kusum-pharma-plant-in-ukraine/134720987)【Source: ET Pharma report 2026-10-06】

**【2026-10-06】Piramal's Lexington sterile injectable site opens new microbiology laboratory** — The new microbiology laboratory and sterility testing suite at the Kentucky site were released for routine GMP use on 11 September, among the first elements of an US$85 million expansion to come online. ## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-06】LUPIN launches world's first triple fixed-dose combination inhaler for COPD in India** — Lupin announced the India launch of Formoflo-G® MDI, described as the world's first fixed-dose triple-combination metered-dose inhaler for the long-term management of moderate to severe COPD. It combines glycopyrronium bromide, formoterol fumarate and fluticasone propionate as a twice-daily single-strength dose, following DCGI approval. Lupin says more than 55 million people live with COPD in India and that it ranks second in India's respiratory therapy segment (IQVIA MAT March 2026). — Source: [Lupin](https://www.lupin.com/media/press-releases/lupin-launches-formoflo-g-mdi-world-s-first-fixed-dose-triple-combination-drug-for-copd-management)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-06】
