## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-03】NPPA revised anti-cancer MRP list expands a third time to 1,084 brands, 895 cut (update)** — Data cut-off moved from 2 November to 3 November; entries rose to 1,084 (from 1,066), with 895 reduced, 189 unchanged and still none increased. Average cut among reduced brands is 50.83% and 41.97% across the full sample; effective 9 November. Originators hold prices (Eli Lilly's Ramiven stays at ₹21,429 across strengths) while generics fall sharply (abiraterone 250 mg from ₹2,718.75 to ₹477). — Source: [NPPA](https://nppa.gov.in/storage/uploads/tender/revised-mrp-report-03112026-03-11-2026.pdf)【Source: NPPA revised MRP report 2026-11-03, 81 pages, data to 2026-11-03, effective 2026-11-09】

**【2026-11-03】Health Ministry proposes three new categories of authorised ports of entry for drugs and devices** — Inland container depots, special economic zones and international airports would be added as authorised points of entry for drugs, medical devices and cosmetics: Noida International Airport and SEZ Bommasandra in Karnataka, plus ICDs at Whitefield (Bengaluru), Sonipat/Piyala/Harsaru (Haryana), Dadri (Uttar Pradesh), Gandhidham/Mundra (Gujarat) and Sanath Nagar/Thimmapur (Hyderabad), to allow clearance closer to plants and warehouses. — Source: [Sina Finance](https://wap.cj.sina.cn/pc/7x24/5189791)【Source: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare statement 2026-11-03, draft amendment, gazette number not yet available】
