## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-30】DGFT extends minimum import price on ATS-8 and Sulfadiazine to 30 November** — The Directorate General of Foreign Trade extended the minimum import price on two APIs through Notification Nos. 39/2026-27 and 40/2026-27: ATS-8 (a key intermediate for atorvastatin calcium) stays at **USD 111/kg (CIF)** and Sulfadiazine API at **₹1,774/kg**; export-oriented units and supplies to SEZs remain exempt. Industry reports Chinese material still entering at around ₹9,500/kg through "cross-subsidisation", leaving domestic producers with inventory overhang and soft order flows. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/centre-extends-mip-cover-on-two-apis-till-nov/134608390)【Source: DGFT Notification No. 39/2026-27 and 40/2026-27, 2026-09-30】

**【2026-10-01】Karnataka drug regulator flags mark-ups of up to 52x on high-cost drugs; next phase targets antivirals and critical antibiotics** — The state Food Safety and Drug Administration said a 25–26 September verification drive covering 189 high-cost drugs and 768 consumables identified extreme pricing anomalies in 253 drugs: companies supply hospitals at institutional discounts while patients are billed at or near MRP, producing mark-ups of 10x to 52x over procurement cost. The state has written to the Union Health Ministry seeking amendments to the Drugs Price Control Order 2013, expansion of the essential medicines list and mandatory disclosure of landing cost alongside MRP on bills; the next round will focus on antiretrovirals, newer-generation and critical antibiotics, and medical devices. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulatory-update/karnataka-flags-10-to-52-times-markup-on-cancer-drugs-consumables-seeks-centres-intervention/134626908)【Source: Karnataka FDA press note, 2026-10-01】

**【2026-09-30】DGTR initiates anti-dumping investigation into Sodium Cyanide from China PR** — The Directorate General of Trade Remedies opened an investigation into Sodium Cyanide (NaCN) originating in or exported from China PR, the European Union, Japan and Korea RP; NaCN is the upstream feedstock for cyanoacetic acid and other cyano-based pharmaceutical and agrochemical intermediates. On the same day the authority also initiated probes into Toluene Diisocyanate (China PR, Japan, Korea RP), Persulphates (China) and Counterbalance Forklifts (China PR, Japan). — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/)【Source: DGTR initiation notifications, 2026-09-30】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-28】CRISIL sees 11–13% revenue growth for Indian pharma in FY27, with margin pressure** — CRISIL expects Indian pharmaceutical revenue to grow 11–13% in FY27, led by export momentum, but sees input and compliance cost inflation trimming margins by 150–200 basis points, with widening divergence across companies. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news)【Source: CRISIL report, 2026-09-28】

**【2026-09-30】Symbiotec unit Knovea signs US commercialisation agreement for dual-chamber vial products** — Knovea Pharmaceutical, a wholly owned subsidiary of steroid API maker Symbiotec Pharmalab, signed a US commercialisation agreement with a leading global pharmaceutical company on 30 September for dual-chamber vial (DCV) products. The company filed its first DCV ANDA in September and plans a second in early Q4 FY27; its complex injectables CDMO segment remains early-stage at under 5% of revenue. — Source: [Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/symbiotec-usfda-eir-vai-pithampur-cmuo8zxav000p34mk9ajwoz46)【Source: company disclosure, 2026-09-30】
