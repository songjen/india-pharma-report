## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-09】Revised anti-cancer MRPs take effect: 85 firms file 1,364 formulations, ₹3,540 crore annual savings (update)** — The 30% trade-margin cap on 137 anti-cancer drugs entered its implementation day. As of 8 November, 85 companies had reported revised MRPs for 121 drugs covering 1,364 formulations, with 679 brands cutting prices by more than 50% and the steepest cut at 96.45%; estimated annual savings are ₹3,540.38 crore, and no chemist or hospital pharmacy may charge above the revised MRP. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2334268)【Source: PIB PRID 2334268, 2026-11-09; NPPA under S.O. 5690(E)】

**【2026-11-09】Department of Pharmaceuticals drafts ₹7,000 crore bulk-drug scheme, shifting from product-specific to platform-based incentives** — About ₹5,000 crore would fund production incentives and ₹2,000 crore R&D and common infrastructure; the framework defines 52 molecule platforms spanning more than 300 intermediates and downstream APIs, split into intermediates, advanced intermediates and APIs, and would admit brownfield plants, apply a flat incentive rate, run an eight-year expenditure schedule and allow two zero-production years. The existing PLI has drawn ₹5,210.74 crore of investment but only ₹3,270 crore of output and ₹87.5 crore of incentive disbursal, 1.2% of the approved outlay. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/7-000-crore-bulk-drug-scheme-in-the-works-to-boost-value-chain-126110901594_1.html)

**【2026-11-09】Industry body seeks review of automatic licence-suspension clause (G.S.R. 956(E))** — The Federation of Pharma Entrepreneurs argues that the Drugs and Cosmetics Act already empowers investigation and suspension, and that automatic suspension on an NSQ report lacks procedural safeguards; it asks for a right to be heard, proportionality, and case-by-case action. The draft is open for comment for 30 days. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/fope-seeks-reconsideration-of-proposed-automatic-suspension-of-drug-manufacturing)
