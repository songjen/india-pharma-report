## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-12】DGTR issues final findings in the sunset review on Arylides (Aceto Acetyl Derivatives) from China PR** — India's Directorate General of Trade Remedies issued final findings in the anti-dumping sunset review on Arylides (Aceto Acetyl Derivatives, used in dyes, pigments and some pharma intermediates) from China PR; File No. 6/28/2020-DGTR and 7/04/2026-DGTR, SETU Case ID AD/SSR/002/2026, applicant Laxmi Organics, SSR initiated on 2026-03-19, period of investigation 2024-10-01 to 2025-09-30. — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-aceto-acetyl-derivatives-also)【Source: DGTR Final Findings 2026-10-12 (SETU AD/SSR/002/2026; SSR initiation notification 2026-03-19)】

**【2026-10-12】Supreme Court revisits drug trade margins: Centre confirms 30% cap on non-scheduled cancer drugs, court seeks extension to all essential medicines** — The Centre told the Supreme Court it has decided to cap trade margins on non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs at 30%, expected to cover 110 products (including about 35 patented drugs), with the list to be drawn up by a DGHS expert committee and notified by NPPA after price examination; the court questioned why the measure is limited to cancer drugs, sought extension of the 16% retail margin under DPCO 2013 to other essential medicines and medical devices, and gave the government until October 27 to work out the modalities. — Source: [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/india/centre-plans-30-cap-on-cancer-drug-trade-margins-sc-calls-for-broader-scrutiny-14050230.html)【Source: Supreme Court hearing 2026-10-12 (Kishan Chand Jain & Ors), next hearing 2026-10-27】

**【2026-10-11】DCGI writes to IPC over unapproved drugs listed in National Formulary of India 2026** — The Drug Controller General of India has asked the Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission to explain why the 7th edition of the National Formulary of India (NFI 2026, released 2026-09-21, containing 653 monographs) lists at least five drugs that have not been approved by CDSCO; the DCGI said the discrepancy "may have international implications and therefore requires an urgent response", and termed the unavailability of the NFI 2026 online version a "serious concern". — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/national-formulary-of-india-lists-drugs-not-approved-by-cdsco-dcgi-seeks-explanation-from-ipc/134864175)【Source: DCGI note to IPC (2026-08); NFI 7th edition released 2026-09-21】

**【2026-10-12】TDB-DST signs ₹86 crore agreement with Peptris to accelerate AI-discovered drugs into the clinic** — The Technology Development Board (TDB) under the Department of Science & Technology signed an agreement with Bengaluru-based Peptris Technologies for ₹86 crore (about USD 9 million) of RDI Fund support for the VIGOR programme, to advance four AI-discovered drug candidates from TRL-4 to TRL-6 through preclinical development; total project cost is ₹172 crore. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2322077)【Source: PIB PRID 2322077 (2026-10-12)】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-12】After CPHI Milan: Indian firms cite a "missing middle" holding back China+1, specialised molecules still dominated by China** — Indian pharma and chemical companies at the expo said the China+1 opportunity is real, but gaps in financing, scale, technology and regulatory capability prevent MSMEs from capturing it; Deccan Nutraceuticals said it remains 20%–30% dependent on China for raw materials and chemicals, Sun Moon said some molecules are manufactured "only in China", and is turning to Egypt, Indonesia, Brazil, Malaysia and Turkey, while meeting European requirements can cost almost twice its existing expense base. — Source: [The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/small-biz/trade/exports/insights/no-love-for-the-middle-cphi-milan-exposes-indias-pharma-scaling-challenge/articleshow/134883651.cms)【Source: on-site interviews at CPHI Worldwide Milan, 2026-10-06 to 10-08】

**【2026-10-07】PTI: Indian SME pharma firms say some drug-intermediate orders are shifting from China to India** — Small and mid-sized Indian firms at CPHI Milan said some drug-intermediate orders are moving from China to India as Western buyers diversify supply chains; about 40 SMEs exhibited under the Pharmexcil India pavilion, with Clarion Organics saying it has won US orders that previously went to China over the past year and AllChem saying it has replaced seven to eight intermediates formerly sourced from China; others cautioned that China remains hard to replace on raw materials and price in the near term. — Source: [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/small-biz/trade/exports/insights/indian-sme-pharma-firms-see-shift-of-orders-from-china-cost-quality-concerns-remain/articleshow/134754153.cms)【Source: PTI report 2026-10-07 (CPHI Worldwide Milan)】

**【2026-10-10】Shilpa Medicare receives EU GMP certification from AGES Austria; OERIS ondansetron injection cleared by SEC** — Shilpa Medicare's Unit 4 at Jadcherla, Telangana (sterile injectables and non-sterile tablets and capsules) received European GMP certification following an inspection by Austria's AGES from 27 to 30 July 2026, allowing continued supply to Europe and other markets; separately, its extended-release ondansetron injection OERIS received final approval from the CDSCO Subject Expert Committee for the Indian market, protected by a patent in India until 2039. — Source: [AskFuzz](https://askfuzz.ai/discover/news/companies/shilpa-medicare-gets-gmp-certification-oeris-approval)【Source: company regulatory filing 2026-10-10】

**【2026-10-12】Rubicon Research receives 1 USFDA observation at Satara plant after unannounced inspection** — Rubicon Research said its Satara manufacturing facility in Maharashtra was inspected by the USFDA without prior notice from 5 to 9 October 2026 and received a Form 483 with one observation, which the company described as procedural and unrelated to data integrity; it was the company's third successful unannounced USFDA inspection in FY27. — Source: [EquityPandit](https://www.equitypandit.com/kec-international-bags-rs-1014-cr-td-orders-in-india)【Source: company announcement 2026-10-12 (Form 483)】
