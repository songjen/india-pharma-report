## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-10】Pharma Secretary: steep margins breed malpractice; 30% trade-margin cap on cancer drugs is the first lever** — Pharmaceuticals Secretary Manoj Joshi said excessive trade margins push costlier medicines into preference and create a nexus among doctors, retailers and smaller manufacturers; the government will target "malpractice" rather than margins in general, noting that large firms, being dependent on US and EU markets, violate the Uniform Code for Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices (UCPMP) less often, with violations concentrated among small firms with turnover of ₹100–150 crore. The remarks came immediately after the government imposed a 30% trade-margin cap on cancer drugs. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/steep-margins-allow-room-for-malpractice-pharmaceuticals-secy/134846052)【Source: Pharmaceuticals Secretary Manoj Joshi, public remarks 2026-10-09】

**【2026-10-06】CDSCO directs Teneligliptin makers to add "Bullous Pemphigoid" as an adverse reaction in labelling** — The office of the Drugs Controller General of India has written to State and UT licensing authorities asking them to direct Teneligliptin formulators in their jurisdictions to add "Bullous Pemphigoid" as an adverse drug reaction in package inserts and promotional literature, and to report action taken. The decision follows a pharmacovigilance signal assessment by PvPI and review by the Subject Expert Committee on Endocrinology and Metabolism. — Source: [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/news/industry/pharma/cdsco-seeks-to-list-bullous-pemphigoid-as-teneligliptin-adverse-reaction-180705)【Source: CDSCO/DCGI notice 2026-10-06】

**【2026-10-04】Department of Pharmaceuticals sanctions ₹1,600 crore for 41 projects under PRIP** — The Department of Pharmaceuticals approved 41 projects with ₹1,600 crore of financial assistance under the first phase of the Promotion of Research and Innovation in Pharma-MedTech (PRIP) scheme: 27 new medicines, nine novel medical devices and five complex generics and biosimilars. With industry co-funding, total R&D investment across these projects is about ₹4,620 crore. Selected applicants include Wockhardt, Biocon, Bharat Biotech, Sun Pharma, Zydus and Mankind, covering multidrug-resistant Gram-negative infections, AMR priority pathogens and in-vivo CAR-T. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/pharma-dept-sanctions-1600-cr-for-41-projects-under-prip/134675288)【Source: DoP PRIP Phase-1 approvals 2026-10-04】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-07】DoP asks expert committee to submit cancer-drug margin list by October 14** — The Department of Pharmaceuticals has written to the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority to take forward the 30% trade-margin cap, and has asked the Health Ministry's DGHS expert committee to submit by October 14 the list of non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs to which the cap will apply. — Source: [PharmaBiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/)【Source: DoP letter to NPPA 2026-10-07】

**【2026-10-06】Pharma dominates Indian healthcare M&A, with three large deals in the month** — Healthcare M&A in India totalled about ₹19 billion during the month, led by three major deals in pharmaceuticals, while hospitals and healthtech dominated private-equity activity. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news)

**【2026-10-06】PCG Symposium 2026 spotlights India's complex-therapeutics ecosystem** — The two-day Mumbai event featured 75+ speakers and 30+ panel discussions spanning peptides, complex generics, oligonucleotides, regulatory pathways and AI in drug development. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/biopharma)

**【2026-10-04】Commentary: over 70% of APIs used by Indian generics still sourced from China** — A commentary noted that more than 70% of the active ingredients used by India's generic drug industry continue to be procured from China, and that four years of localisation tools such as PLI and tariffs have not changed the upstream picture; India's imports of APIs, KSMs and drug intermediates from China reached ₹27,032 crore in FY25, about 73.7% of the total. — Source: [Continental Bulletin](https://continentalbulletin.com/indias-record-112-billion-trade-deficit-with-china-underscores-persistent-import-dependence)
