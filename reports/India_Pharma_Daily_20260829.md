## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-08-28】Dept. of Pharmaceuticals opens second call under PRIP Scheme (₹5,000 cr ≈ $5.23 bn total outlay)** — The Department of Pharmaceuticals (DoP, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilisers) on Aug 28 announced the second call for applications under the PRIP (Promotion of Research and Innovation in Pharma & MedTech) Scheme, with a ₹5,000 cr (≈$5.23 bn) outlay. Two tracks: (i) Preparatory phase — startups/MSMEs progressing projects at TRL 1-3 to not beyond TRL 5, via a two-stage process (concept note, then detailed application), with up to ₹5 cr (≈$523K) per project; (ii) Subsequent phase — industry/startups/MSMEs advancing projects at TRL 4-6, with up to ₹100 cr (≈$10.5M) per project, capped at 35% of approved project cost with co-funding from the applicant. Priority areas cover NCE/NBE/phyto-pharmaceuticals, complex generics and biosimilars, and novel medical devices (AI/ML devices, robotic surgery, genetic diagnostics, etc.). Submission window on the PRIP portal (prip.pharma-dept.gov.in) opens by mid-September; Round 1 applicants may not resubmit the same project. — Source: [BioSpectrum India](https://www.biospectrumindia.com/news/93/28395/prip-scheme-opens-up-new-opportunities-for-startups-msmes-and-industry-in-pharma-and-medtech-sectors.html) · [Business AajKal](https://www.businessaajkal.com/business/centre-invites-applications-for-rs-5000-crore-prip-scheme-to-boost-pharma-medtech-innovation-32365)

**【2026-08-27】IPC launches first-ever 7 phytochemical + 5 botanical reference standards (first conclave in Northeast)** — The Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission (IPC) on Aug 27 held a Scientific Conclave on "IP 2026: Advancing Quality, Safety and Innovation in Drugs, Food and Phytopharmaceuticals" at NIPER Guwahati — its first such event in the Northeast region. Highlights: launch of seven Indian Pharmacopoeia Phytochemical Reference Standards (IPPRS: Andrographolide, Capsaicin, Embelin, Eugenol, Marmelosin/Imperatorin, Scopoletin, Umbelliferone) and five Indian Pharmacopoeia Botanical Reference Standards (IPBRS: Terminalia arjuna, Bacopa monnieri, Tinospora cordifolia, Centella asiatica, Ocimum sanctum), establishing material baselines for standardisation, quality assurance and regulatory evaluation of plant-based medicines. This follows IPC's Aug 25 release of seven nitrosamine impurity reference standards, further advancing reference-standard localisation. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2304237)

**【2026-08-27】Union Home Minister Amit Shah: cumulative pharma investments ₹42,700 cr ≈ $4.47 bn; drug exports ₹3.64 lakh cr ≈ $38.1 bn** — Speaking at the PharmaInnova Awards-2026 ceremony (organised by Troika Pharmaceuticals) in Ahmedabad on Aug 27, Amit Shah said government initiatives — PLI, Bulk Drug Parks and Medical Device Parks — have attracted cumulative investment of over ₹42,700 cr (≈$4.47 bn) with India's cumulative drug exports exceeding ₹3.64 lakh crore (≈$38.1 bn). He said the PRIP scheme will support pharma & medtech R&D with multi-thousand-crore funding, and that all medical devices will be manufactured locally in India before 2028, with PLI and Bulk Drug Parks expected to break China's dominance in the API supply chain. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2303992) · [Nation Press](https://www.nationpress.com/national/shah-india-4th-largest-economy-2047-goal-in-sight)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-08-28】Maharashtra FDA cancels Cipla's Pune C&F drug sale licence (Reactin Plus violations + incomplete recall compliance)** — The Maharashtra Food & Drug Administration on Aug 28 announced cancellation, effective Aug 27, of the drug sale licences of Cipla Pharma & Life Sciences' Carry & Forwarding unit at Wadki, Pune. Origin: a June inspection found the Schedule H prescription medicine Reactin Plus tablets carrying an unauthorised "analgesic and antipyretic" claim on packaging (a prohibited advertisement), leading to seizure of stock valued at ~₹11.19 lakh (≈$11.7K) and a market recall order; a follow-up inspection found the company had not fully complied with recall directions, and identified additional violations including medicines stored directly on the floor, inadequate pallet/rack arrangements, discrepancies between computerised and physical stock, and deficiencies in expired-medicine handling records. FDA Commissioner Tukaram Mundhe said there would be no leniency on drug safety and public health issues, with no exception for large entities. — Source: [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/maharashtra/fda-cancels-drug-sale-licences-of-cipla-pharma-life-sciences-pune-carry-and-forwarding-unit/article71400652.ece) · [Mid-Day](https://www.mid-day.com/amp/news/india-news/article/maharashtra-fda-raids-maharashtra-fda-cancels-cipla-facilitys-drug-sale-licences-in-pune-over-violations-seizes-rs-11-11-lakh-stock-23647216)
