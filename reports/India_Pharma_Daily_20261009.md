## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-08】NPPA approves 30% trade margin cap on non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs** — At its 151st meeting, the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority approved in principle, under Paragraph 19 of the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013, a 30% trade margin cap on non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs, covering branded and generic, domestic and imported, patented and non-patented medicines; MRPs are expected to fall 20%–70%, saving about ₹2,500 crore (approx. US$258 million) a year. Non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs carry an average trade mark-up of about 170%, reaching 700% in some cases. The Department of Pharmaceuticals has asked NPPA to implement the measure and requested the Health Ministry to constitute a DGHS expert committee to submit the drug list by 14 October. — Source: [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/amp/news/industry/pharma/cancer-drug-mrps-could-fall-20-70-under-nppas-proposed-trade-margin-cap-180844)·[ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/centre-caps-cancer-drugs-trade-margins-at-30-dghs-panel-to-prescribe-final-list/134802360)【Source: Minutes of the 151st (283rd overall) NPPA Authority meeting, 2026-10-08】

**【2026-10-08】DCGI launches focused inspections of cough syrup manufacturers this month** — Speaking at IPA's Advanced GMP Workshop, the Drugs Controller General of India said central and state drug authorities will begin focused audits of cough syrup manufacturing units nationwide this month, with licence cancellation for non-compliant facilities. More than 1,000 CAPA notices issued over the past four years have gone unaddressed; the agency will set a response deadline for CAPA notices, failing which manufacturing licences will be suspended. Since December 2022, 1,100 cough syrup facilities — about 90% of the national total — have been inspected. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulatory-update/drug-regulator-to-launch-focused-inspection-drive-for-cough-syrup-mfg-units-this-month/134791859)【Source: DCGI remarks at IPA online Advanced GMP Workshop, 2026-10-08】

**【2026-10-08】Supreme Court orders expert committee on statutory backing for pharma marketing rules** — In Federation of Medical & Sales Representatives Association of India v. Union of India, the Supreme Court directed the Centre to constitute an expert committee within two weeks to comprehensively examine the adequacy and enforcement of the current pharma marketing framework, including the Uniform Code for Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices, 2024, with recommendations due two months after its first meeting. The government must file a compliance affidavit within two weeks of the committee completing its work; the matter is listed for 29 January 2027. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/supreme-court-seeks-committee-to-curb-pharma-firms-unethical-marketing-practices/134788596)·[The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/sc-directs-centre-to-form-expert-panel-to-assess-statutory-backing-for-pharma-marketing-regulation/article71558619.ece)【Source: Supreme Court order, 2026-10-08 (FMRAI v. Union of India)】

**【2026-10-06】Pharmexcil invites expressions of interest for Netherlands pharma delegation** — The Pharmaceuticals Export Promotion Council of India issued Circular No. 15935, inviting member companies to register their interest in participating in an Indian pharmaceutical business delegation to the Netherlands. — Source: [Pharmexcil](https://pharmexcil.com/circulars/expression-of-interest-for-participation-in-indian-pharmaceutical-business-delegation-to-the-netherlands/15935/c3145e53be99766f8a27453a0ca890fe.html)【Source: Pharmexcil Circular 15935, 2026-10-06】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-08】CPHI Milan: Indian firms shift from generic exports to licensing, CDMO and higher-value plays** — The expo showed Indian drugmakers moving beyond low-cost generic exports towards R&D collaboration, licensing, contract manufacturing and differentiated products. Hetero Group held close to 700 customer meetings in three days; Cipla entered an exclusive US licensing and supply partnership with China's Qilu Pharmaceutical for QL2107, a pembrolizumab biosimilar. Mankind, Cadila and Akums are also pursuing in-licensing and out-licensing as well as injectables and oral solid dose CDMO capabilities. — Source: [The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/small-biz/trade/exports/insights/cphi-milan-beyond-generics-how-indian-pharma-is-rewriting-its-global-playbook/amp_articleshow/134788066.cms)【Source: on-site interviews at CPHI Worldwide Milan, 2026-10-06 to 10-08】

**【2026-10-05】Aptar expands Mumbai pharma site with localised inhalation device production** — Aptar Pharma completed an expansion of its Mumbai site, adding local production and moulding capability for pressurised metered dose inhaler (pMDI) valves, breath-actuated systems and multi-dose nasal spray systems. The footprint rose to 11,300 sq m, serving customers across India and Southeast Asia; an inauguration ceremony was held on 5 October. — Source: [Aptar](https://www.aptar.com/news-events/aptar-expands-pharma-operations-in-mumbai-reinforcing-india-as-a-strategic-growth-market)【Source: Company announcement, 2026-10-05】
