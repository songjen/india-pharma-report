## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-27】DGTR issues PUC/PCN methodology in the glycine anti-dumping case against China PR; all grades, including injectable pharmaceutical grade, remain in scope** — The Directorate General of Trade Remedies (applicant: Avid Organics) confirmed that the product under consideration covers crude, industrial, technical, feed, food, EP/USP and pharmaceutical (injectable) grade glycine, with grades differing only in application-specific additional testing; as no interested party commented on product scope, the scope notified on 22 September is retained with no PCN methodology adopted, and the comment window closed on 9 October. — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/initiation-anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-glycine-originating-or)【Source: DGTR notification 2026-10-27 (F. No. 6/41/2026-DGTR; SETU AD/OI/045/2026)】

**【2026-10-27】India finalises list of 100 critical imported inputs for domestic substitution, including APIs and speciality chemicals** — A government official said 100 critical inputs have been shortlisted from a wider basket worth USD 51 billion in imports, with measures planned to promote local manufacturing, cut reliance on China and narrow the trade deficit; official analysis sees about USD 398 billion of imports as substitutable locally; GTRI notes China's share of India's industrial goods imports rose from 21% to 30% over 15 years, with APIs and speciality chemicals among the key categories. — Source: [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/foreign-trade/india-considers-measures-to-boost-local-production-of-100-critical-inputs-to-cut-import-reliance-government-official-says/printarticle/135236274.cms)

**【2026-10-27】Supreme Court questions NPPA's inaction on trade margins, reserves order and gives two weeks for written submissions** — The bench asked why NPPA had not capped trade margins on essential medicines for years and why the 30% ceiling on 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs came only after court intervention; the government argued for a "calibrated" approach and against full control; the court also cited NPPA's ₹10,029.8 crore (about USD 1.05 billion) of overcharging dues as of end-March, of which only ₹1,582.4 crore had been recovered. — Source: [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/sc-says-economic-policy-cannot-take-precedence-over-lives-as-centre-backs-calibrated-drug-price-controls/article71631892.ece)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-26】ORCHID PHARMA signs nine-country Latin America licence for Exblifep with Laboratorio Libra, potential USD 80 million over 10 years** — The product is a cefepime/enmetazobactam combination (carbapenem-sparing, for complicated urinary tract infections and hospital-acquired pneumonia); Libra obtains exclusive rights to register, import and market it in Chile, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Panama, Paraguay and Uruguay, with Orchid manufacturing and supplying the finished dosage form; Orchid operates one of only three USFDA-approved sterile cephalosporin API sites globally, with APIs accounting for about 70% of its revenue. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/orchid-pharma-inks-80-million-latin-america-deal-to-expand-antibiotic-exblifep-across-nine-markets-22242)

**【2026-10-27】ACUTAAS CHEMICALS Q2 revenue up 42.7% on pharmaceutical intermediates, FY27 guidance raised** — Revenue reached ₹436.9 crore (about USD 45.9 million), up 42.7% YoY, with gross margin at 62.1%, EBITDA margin 40.3% and net profit ₹123.1 crore (about USD 12.9 million), up 71.4%; growth was led by pharmaceutical intermediates (advanced intermediates for regulated-market and generic APIs, plus NCE intermediates) while speciality chemicals softened; the company raised FY27 revenue growth guidance from 25% to about 30%. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/laurus-labs-rallies-4-for-2nd-straight-day-stock-hits-new-high-126102700817_1.html)

## 4. Risk Alerts

- **Glycine case covers all grades** — Impact on SINOBRIGHT: pharmaceutical grade is not excluded, so quotes for similar amino acids and glycine-salt intermediates to India must price in anti-dumping duty headroom.
- **Localisation of 100 critical inputs** — Impact on SINOBRIGHT: if penem or steroid intermediates are listed, clients gain policy incentives to buy locally and long-term volume share faces diversion.
- **Cipla's API segment down 59%** — Impact on SINOBRIGHT: the client's captive and outsourced API structure is contracting in tandem, so intermediate enquiries will soften near term.
- **Risk of wider margin caps** — Impact on SINOBRIGHT: after Supreme Court pressure, margin control may extend beyond cancer drugs, and formulation margin compression will pass upstream as price-cut demands.
