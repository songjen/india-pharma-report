**【2026-09-03】India Customs issues Circular No. 40/2026: mandatory pre-clearance compliance checklists for drugs (APIs & finished formulations) among 7 product categories**
— The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) issued Circular No. 40/2026-Customs: aligned with CDSCO's ease-of-doing-business push, customs officers must verify compliance documents against seven checklists before granting out-of-charge for facilitated Bills of Entry. Checklist II covers Drugs (API and finished formulations), Checklist III covers drugs for personal use and small quantities of new drugs imported by government hospitals, and Checklist IV covers drugs imported for examination, test or analysis; uploading documents on e-SANCHIT expedites clearance, while integration with SWIFT 2.0 is under process. Importers of APIs/formulations (and their Chinese/exporting counterparts) should keep registration certificates and import licences ready for customs verification. — Source: [CBIC (Circular 40/2026)](https://www.smta.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Circular-No-40-2026.pdf)
