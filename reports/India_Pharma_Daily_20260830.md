## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-08-29】Bombay High Court raps Maharashtra FDA for acting "overboard, high-handed" in Cipla Pune C&F case; regulator withdraws licence cancellation and restarts proceedings** — The Maharashtra FDA on Saturday (Aug 29) withdrew its order cancelling the drug-sale licences of Cipla Pharma & Life Sciences Ltd's Carry and Forwarding (C&F) facility at Wadki, Pune (effective Aug 27), after a Bombay High Court division bench (Acting Chief Justice Ravindra Ghuge and Justice Gautam Ankhad) criticised the regulator for going "overboard" and behaving in a "high-handed" manner, in violation of the principles of natural justice. Background: following a June inspection that found an unauthorised "analgesic and antipyretic" claim on the packaging of Schedule H prescription medicine Reactin Plus Tablets, the FDA issued a show-cause notice on Aug 13, received the company's reply on Aug 25, then emailed Cipla on Aug 26 (a state-declared public holiday) requiring attendance at a hearing the same day — Cipla sent only an administrative employee to file a written adjournment request, yet the FDA passed the cancellation order that same day (after seizing stock worth ~₹11.19 lakh ≈ $11,700 and ordering a recall). The bench questioned how a department that itself refuses to work on holidays could pass an order on one, stressed that "justice must not only be done, but must appear to be done," and recorded the FDA's statement that it will withdraw the order forthwith, issue a fresh show-cause notice (by Sep 4) and pass a reasoned order after a proper hearing. The Pune C&F hub serves ~180 customers and distributes over 430,000 units of medicines monthly. This reverses the "Cipla licence cancellation" covered in the Aug 28 daily and signals tighter judicial scrutiny of procedural fairness in Indian drug-regulator enforcement. — Sources: [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/maharashtra-fda-withdraws-cipla-licence-cancellation-after-bombay-hc-rap-2982521-2026-08-29) · [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/cipla-pharma-license-cancellation-fda-withdraws-order-after-bombay-hc-raps-it-for-going-overboard-19979592.htm) · [Bar and Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/bombay-high-court-pulls-up-fda-for-high-handed-cancellation-of-cipla-drug-licences)

**【2026-08-28】Jubilant Generics gets show-cause notice demanding ₹105.5 cr ≈ $11.04M from Uttarakhand GST authority (alleged double-availment of IGST refund + Advance Authorisation benefits)** — Jubilant Pharmova disclosed on Aug 28 (exchange filing) that its wholly owned subsidiary Jubilant Generics received a show-cause notice on Aug 27 from the Joint Commissioner, CGST Division, Roorkee, Uttarakhand, alleging that the company availed an IGST refund under Rule 96 and benefits under the Advance Authorisation Scheme (duty-free import of inputs for export products) simultaneously for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22, in violation of Rule 96(10) of the CGST Rules, 2017. The notice proposes GST (tax) of ₹52.75 cr (≈ $5.52M) plus an equal penalty of ₹52.75 cr, totalling ₹105.5 cr (≈ $11.04M), with interest unquantified. Jubilant Generics said the notice is "factually and legally incorrect, bad in law" and issued without proper verification of facts; it will file a detailed reply on merits and expects no financial or operational impact. The case is a compliance warning for export-oriented pharma companies: IGST export refunds and AA duty-free import benefits are mutually exclusive channels, and tax authorities are actively targeting "double-availment." — Sources: [ET Manufacturing](https://manufacturing.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/life-sciences/jubilant-generics-receives-show-cause-notice-demanding-over-105-cr/133589144) · [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/stocks/jubilant-pharmova-share-price-in-focus-after-subsidiary-receives-52-75-crore-gst-notice)

Companies operating or warehousing in India (including downstream customers of Chinese API exporters) should treat hearing rights seriously in state-level enforcement responses.
