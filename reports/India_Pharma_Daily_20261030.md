## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-27】CDSCO mandates batch-wise testing of raw materials and excipients; ten high-risk solvents must be logged on ONDLS** — Two directives issued this week: manufacturers must test every batch or lot of raw materials and excipients (propylene glycol, glycerol and sorbitol in particular) before release, keep records under Schedule U, and put in place a robust vendor-qualification system; ten high-risk solvents (including glycerin, propylene glycol, ethyl alcohol and diethylene glycol) must be reported on the ONDLS portal with quantities manufactured, CoA, quantities supplied and distribution trail. Background: 24 child deaths in Madhya Pradesh after diethylene glycol contamination in the Coldrif syrup; CDSCO has inspected 1,100 cough-syrup facilities since December 2022. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulatory-update/centre-tightens-oversight-of-high-risk-solvents-mandates-excipient-testing-to-strengthen-cough-syrup-quality/135297074) 【Source: DCGI notices 2026-10-27】

**【2026-10-28】Minimum residual shelf life for imported drugs raised to one year; biologicals and radiopharmaceuticals at 60% of labelled life** — The Health Ministry amended the second proviso to Rule 31 of the Drugs Rules, effective the same day: the licensing authority may not permit import of a drug with less than one year of residual shelf life at the date of import; biologicals and radiopharmaceuticals are governed by a separate provision allowing 60% of labelled shelf life. The draft, G.S.R. 505(E), was issued on 22 June and finalised after consultation with the Drugs Technical Advisory Board. — Source: [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/centre-mandates-minimum-one-year-shelf-life-for-imported-drugs-enn26102906725) 【Source: Drugs (Thirteenth Amendment) Rules, 2026, 2026-10-28】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-29】PHARMEXCIL: H1 FY27 pharma exports at USD 16.14 bn, up 4.9%; bulk drugs and intermediates up 12.07% (updated)** — H1 exports USD 16.14 bn against USD 15.38 bn a year earlier; Q2 USD 8.04 bn, up 3.05%, with September the strongest month at USD 2.744 bn, up 4.68%. Europe USD 3.29 bn, up 9.66% and 20.38% of the total (Germany +21.57%, Netherlands +15.93%, France +15.02%), while NAFTA fell 2.05% to USD 5.3 bn. Formulations and biologicals were 73.84% of shipments, up 3.81%; bulk drugs and intermediates exceeded USD 2.75 bn, up 12.07%; surgical products rose 9.53%. — Source: [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/business/Industry/pharma-h1-exports-up-5-to-1614-bn-as-europe-offsets-us-slowdown/article71641947.ece) 【Source: Pharmexcil data release 2026-10-29】

**【2026-10-29】Indian Patent Office rejects TB Alliance's BPaL combination patent** — The application covered a unitary dosage form of bedaquiline, pretomanid and linezolid, optionally with pyrazinamide. Three opponents filed pre-grant opposition; the applicant did not appear for oral submissions and the office held the application failed the requirements of the Patents Act. BPaL is the WHO-recommended six-month all-oral MDR-TB regimen; Macleods and Lupin already manufacture components in India, and 15,000 patients had been initiated under NTEP until March. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/indian-denies-patent-to-tballiances-bpal-combo/135277397) 【Source: Indian Patent Office order 2026-10-28】

Cumulative ANDA approvals reached 247 (227 final, 20 tentative). — Source: [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/news/industry/pharma/alembic-pharma-gets-usfda-final-approval-for-generic-valganciclovir-oral-solution-182169) 【Source: company announcement 2026-10-28】
