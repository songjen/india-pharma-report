## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-08-29】Argentina commits to upgrade India from Annex II to Annex I under its pharma regulatory framework, lowering market-entry barriers for Indian drugs** — India's Ministry of Commerce and Industry said on Saturday (Aug 29) that Argentina has committed to work towards upgrading India from Annex II to Annex I under its pharmaceutical regulatory framework and reducing barriers to entry for Indian pharmaceutical companies, creating greater opportunities for Indian drugmakers and improving access to quality, affordable healthcare in Argentina. The commitment emerged at the 4th India-Argentina Joint Trade Committee (JTC) meeting held on August 24 in Buenos Aires, led by Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agarwal. Background: the 2019 MoU between Argentina's ANMAT and India's CDSCO underpins bilateral regulatory cooperation, with both sides also committing to joint Ayurveda and Yoga initiatives. Bilateral trade crossed USD 6.5 billion in 2025 (annual growth over 17%), making India Argentina's fifth-largest trading partner. The JTC was followed on Aug 25 by India's largest-ever business delegation to Argentina (25+ business leaders, including UPL, Kirloskar, Aditya Birla Group, spanning pharma/healthcare/agriculture). Annex I status implies smoother registration recognition and market access — a significant breakthrough for Indian pharma "going global" in South America and a potential boost to Indian generics and API exports to Latin America. — Sources: [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/argentina-committed-to-reducing-barriers-to-facilitate-entry-of-indian-pharma-sector-government/article71403791.ece) · [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/amp/business/india-argentina-step-up-efforts-for-pharma-and-farm-product-access-articleshow-8r21fv2) · [India Business & Trade](https://www.indiabusinesstrade.in/argentina-opens-doors-to-pharma.htm)

**【2026-08-30】Commerce Ministry to meet industry & exporters on India-US trade on Sep 1, with pharma on the agenda, ahead of Goyal's late-September US visit** — PTI reported on Saturday (Aug 30) that India's Commerce Ministry will meet industry associations and export promotion council representatives on September 1 to discuss India-US trade performance and recent bilateral trade developments. The meeting will be chaired by Additional Secretary Darpan Jain, the country's chief negotiator for the India-US bilateral trade agreement (BTA). Context: Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal will visit the US in late September for the G-20 Trade Ministerial in Milwaukee (beginning Sep 30) and hold bilateral talks with USTR Jamieson Greer. Sectors expected at the meeting include **pharmaceuticals**, engineering, textiles, agriculture, chemicals, marine products, leather, autos and auto components; chambers CII, FICCI, Assocham and FIEO are also likely to participate. Data backdrop: India's exports to the US rose marginally to USD 87.31 billion in FY26 from USD 86.51 billion in FY25; the US imposed an additional 10% tariff on India and other countries from July 24. India is seeking a tariff advantage over competitors (Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia) in the US market. Generic medicines are among India's key exports to the US. — Source: [News18 (PTI)](https://www.news18.com/agency-feeds/commerce-min-meeting-industry-exporters-on-india-us-trade-on-sep-1-10300880.html)
