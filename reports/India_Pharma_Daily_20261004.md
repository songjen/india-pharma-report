## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-30】DGTR initiates anti-dumping investigation into Clavulanic Acid (Potassium Clavulanate and its Amine Salt) from China PR** — The Directorate General of Trade Remedies has initiated an anti-dumping investigation into Clavulanic Acid and its amine salt originating in or exported from China PR and the European Union. Clavulanic acid is a core raw material for beta-lactamase inhibitors; the case advances alongside the Penicillin-G, 6-APA and Amoxycillin Trihydrate cases, with exporters and interested parties required to file questionnaire responses within the prescribed deadline. — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-ongoing-investigation)【Source: DGTR Initiation Notification 2026-09-30】

**【2026-10-02】CDSCO shelves plan to make drug test reports public** — The central drug regulator has dropped the plan to build a digital platform for public disclosure of batch test reports generated under routine quality surveillance. Minutes of the 70th Drugs Consultative Committee (DCC) meeting held that merely uploading a manufacturer-supplied document would not establish its authenticity or assure batch quality, and that any disclosure mechanism must form part of a broader verifiable regulatory system. Currently only batch number, manufacturer name and test result of Not-of-Standard-Quality or spurious batches are published monthly, without the extent of deviation or the regulatory action taken. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/centre-shelves-plan-to-make-drug-test-reports-public/134631046)【Source: Minutes of the 70th DCC meeting】

**【2026-09-30】Supreme Court seeks a 16% retail margin cap, flags huge cancer drug markups** — A Supreme Court bench questioned the logic of distinguishing essential from non-essential medicines under the Drugs (Prices Control) Order and proposed capping the maximum retail price at no more than 16% above the retailer price for all medicines, citing a cancer drug sold to retailers at ₹3,520 and to patients at ₹22,427. The Centre said it would seek an equitable solution; the matter is posted for further hearing on 12 October 2026. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/sc-seeks-16-mrp-cap-on-medicines-flags-huge-cancer-drug-markups/134577318)【Source: Supreme Court hearing 2026-09-29; next hearing 2026-10-12】

**【2026-10-02】Department of Pharmaceuticals launches Special Campaign 6 on e-waste disposal and pendency liquidation** — The Department of Pharmaceuticals, together with the NPPA, PMBI, NIPERs and public sector undertakings, launched the sixth edition of the special campaign on Gandhi Jayanti, running to 31 October: 12,208 cleanliness drives, liquidation of 4 MP references and 72 public grievances, review of 2,716 physical files, and compliant disposal of 1,011 obsolete equipment items (including 602 IT and telecom units) through authorised recyclers. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2318074)【Source: PIB PRID 2318074, 2026-10-02】
