## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-30】Department of Pharmaceuticals announces 41 PRIP first-round approvals worth about ₹1,600 crore, adds a "New Discovery" track** — The Department of Pharmaceuticals announced the first-call results under PRIP: 41 projects approved for about ₹1,600 crore (≈US$167 mn), expected to catalyse ₹3,020 crore of private investment; the portfolio comprises 27 new-medicine projects, 5 complex generics and biosimilars and 9 novel medical devices, with 6 under the Strategic Priority framework, and involves Sun Pharma, Zydus, Biocon and Wockhardt. A second call adds a dedicated "New Discovery" track with up to ₹50 crore per project, subject to at least 25% co-funding by institutional investors. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/news/pharma-dept-to-offer-gap-funding-for-early-stage-novel-drug-discovery-126093001526_1.html)【Source: Department of Pharmaceuticals release, 2026-09-30】

**【2026-09-30】Multiple states push price controls on medical devices and hospital consumables** — Karnataka's drug controller has proposed a National List of Essential Medical Devices with price caps across 24 categories spanning cardiology, oncology, neurology and kidney care; a Maharashtra survey documented extreme mark-ups on hospital consumables (IV sets billed at ₹325 against a ₹11.05 purchase price; nebuliser kits at ₹715 against ₹40), with the state urging NPPA to cap trade margins, a call backed by the medical device industry association (about 1,000 member companies). — Source: [India Pharma Outlook](https://www.indiapharmaoutlook.com/news/pharma-pricing-under-siege-key-interventions-of-the-month-nwid-5897.html)【Source: India Pharma Outlook monthly round-up, 2026-09-30】

**【2026-09-29】Kerala High Court: Centre may invoke Section 100 of the Patents Act to manufacture and supply patented medicines at low cost** — Ruling on a petition brought by a deceased metastatic breast cancer patient, the court held that Section 100 ("use of inventions for purposes of government") covers government manufacture of a patented medicine and its non-commercial sale or supply to a needy patient, and applies where a patented medicine is sold at an exorbitant price. The court said the government must first gather information and assess affordability, and rejected the Centre's and industry's reading that the provision is confined to government use. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/kerala-hc-calls-for-centres-intervention-on-exorbitantly-priced-patented-medicines/134553429)【Source: Kerala High Court judgment (Justice Harisankar V Menon), reported 2026-09-29】

**【2026-09-29】Pharmexcil issues Maldives health ministry medicine procurement expression of interest** — The Pharmaceuticals Export Promotion Council of India notified a requirement of medicines from the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of Maldives, inviting willingness to supply from member companies holding SRA approvals or Maldives FDA approvals, via an online form; the channel is a standard entry point for Indian firms into government-to-government procurement. — Source: [Pharmexcil](https://pharmexcil.com/circulars/viewcirculars/15903/a6fed343d73645f027446ebe1fe92751.html)【Source: Pharmexcil Circular No.15903, 2026-09-29】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-30】Biodeal acquires Biological E sterile injectable site with a carbapenem vial line** — Biodeal Pharmaceuticals has acquired Biological E's sterile injectable plant at Paonta Sahib, Himachal Pradesh (14 acres, 17 production lines, around 135 million units of annual capacity, equipped with German-made Groninger lines), which manufactures general injectables and carbapenem vial products. The company plans to add pre-filled syringe and lyophilised formats, expand capacity by about 30 million units in the near term, and pursue PIC/S and EU accreditation. Biological E said the divestment allows it to concentrate investment in its new vaccine R&D and CDMO campus in Telangana. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/biodeal-pharmaceuticals-acquires-major-sterile-injectable-facility-from-biological-e-22023)【Source: Company announcement, 2026-09-30】

**【2026-09-30】ChrysCapital and Tata Capital Healthcare Fund III invest US$70 million in Linux Laboratories** — Linux has secured US$70 million in equity funding, with ChrysCapital taking a significant minority stake and Tata Capital Healthcare Fund II exiting fully; proceeds will fund deepening of core specialties, entry into adjacent therapeutic areas, brand acquisitions and capacity and R&D investment. Linux covers more than 125 brands, led by central nervous system therapies (epilepsy, neuropathic pain, depression, autism), and is expected to post FY27 revenue of ₹450–500 crore and EBITDA of ₹85–90 crore. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/mergers-and-acquisitions/chryscapital-to-take-minority-stake-in-linux-laboratories/134588430)【Source: Company press release, 2026-09-29】

**【2026-09-29】Godavari Biorefineries secures Chinese patent for antiviral compounds** — The company announced that its patent on "Use of Compounds for Treating Viral Infections" has been granted by the China National Intellectual Property Administration, following a similar grant in Japan; the patent covers compounds inhibiting Vacuolar ATPase (V-ATPase), a host-cell pathway hijacked by multiple viruses, and their use in antiviral formulations. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/regulatory/godavari-biorefineries-bags-chinese-patent-for-antiviral-v-atpase-compounds-22013)【Source: Company announcement, 2026-09-29】

**【2026-09-29】Akums receives CDSCO approval to manufacture colchicine** — CDMO Akums Drugs and Pharmaceuticals has obtained approval from India's Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation to manufacture the anti-inflammatory drug colchicine, indicated to reduce the risk of myocardial infarction, stroke, coronary revascularisation and cardiovascular death in adults with established atherosclerotic disease or multiple cardiovascular risk factors. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/akums-gets-cdsco-nod-to-manufacture-anti-inflammatory-drug-colchicine/134568529)【Source: CDSCO approval, company statement 2026-09-29】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-09-30】MACLEODS recalls 22,032 bottles of olanzapine in the US** — According to the USFDA enforcement report, Macleods Pharmaceuticals has voluntarily recalled 22,032 bottles of the antipsychotic olanzapine in the US after the product failed impurity and degradation specifications, with organic and unspecified impurities exceeding limits; the action is classified as a Class III recall (minor defects or manufacturing-standard violations). The same report shows Amneal recalling 5,694 exenatide pre-filled pens, manufactured at its Ahmedabad facility in India, over a delivery-system defect, classified Class II. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/amneal-macleods-pharma-recall-drugs-in-us/134588403)【Source: USFDA enforcement report, 2026-09-30】
