## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-03】CDSCO mandates Bullous Pemphigoid as an ADR in teneligliptin package inserts** — The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation has directed all State Licensing Authorities to require manufacturers of the anti-diabetic drug teneligliptin to list "Bullous Pemphigoid" as an adverse drug reaction in the package insert and promotional literature of their formulations. The decision follows an ADR signal assessment by the National Coordination Centre for the Pharmacovigilance Programme of India and review by the Subject Expert Committee (Endocrinology and Metabolism); the DCGI has asked states to report action taken. — Source: [PharmaBiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191009&sid=1)【Source: CDSCO/DCGI circular, Post Marketing Drugs Safety Monitoring Division】

**【2026-10-02】DCC proposes monthly reporting and traceability regime for narcotic and psychotropic preparations by March 2027** — Minutes of the 70th Drugs Consultative Committee meeting show a plan to require every state and union territory to report monthly to CDSCO on licences, handling volumes, inspections and enforcement for such medicines, and to examine adapting the Central Bureau of Narcotics portal for end-to-end track and trace. States were also asked to report measures against spurious drugs, with CDSCO to compile a national mechanism proposal. The recommendations take effect only upon DCGI approval. — Source: [News18](https://news18.com/amp/india/centres-drug-panel-moves-to-tighten-watch-on-narcotic-medicines-seeks-states-help-against-fake-drugs-ws-bl-10363643.html)【Source: Minutes of the 70th DCC meeting (2026-09-18)】

**【2026-10-04】Kerala drugs control seeks interim curbs on inter-state direct procurement to ensure traceability** — The State Drugs Control department has written to the state government seeking a six-month restriction barring pharmaceutical firms from procuring medicines directly from other states, requiring procurement through authorised medical depots within Kerala instead. The department cites difficulty verifying the source of inter-state supplies and rising diversion of subsidised institutional-supply medicines into the retail market after repackaging, while acknowledging possible conflict with procurement freedom under the current GST regime. — Source: [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/kerala/kerala-state-drugs-control-dept-seeks-curbs-on-inter-state-drug-procurement-to-ensure-traceability/article71540395.ece)【Source: Kerala Drugs Control letter to the state government, reported 2026-10-04】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-04】Delhi's RML Hospital recalls 10 drug batches over quality failures and physical defects** — Four notices issued by the hospital's drug units between September 7 and 14 pulled eight batches that failed analytical testing: sodium valproate, spironolactone and imipramine tablets, plus adenosine, methyl ergometrine and other injections. Two further batches — isosorbide mononitrate and metoprolol tablets — were found in powder form inside the strip; the affected batches were manufactured by Glowson India Labs. The hospital said alternatives are in place and no severe adverse events have been reported. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/delhis-rml-hospital-recalls-10-drug-batches-after-quality-failures-defects/134674009)【Source: RML Hospital recall notices, 2026-09-07 to 09-14】

**【2026-09-30】Chhattisgarh announces 141-acre pharma park at Naya Raipur** — The state chief minister announced a 141-acre park with plug-and-play manufacturing facilities and common utilities, targeting formulations and API manufacturing investment to serve central India and neighbouring markets. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/chhattisgarh-cm-announces-141-acre-pharma-park-in-naya-raipur/)

**【2026-09-28】Morepen files its first US ANDA, for sitagliptin, extending from APIs into own finished dosages** — Morepen Laboratories has submitted its first abbreviated new drug application to the US FDA, for the anti-diabetic sitagliptin. The company's core business is APIs and CDMO services; the filing marks its entry into own-label finished dosages in the United States, linking to its integrated CDMO capability. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/regulatory/morepen-enters-us-finished-dosage-space-with-landmark-sitagliptin-anda-submission-22004) · [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/morepen-strengthens-integrated-cdmo-capabilities-with-first-us-anda-submission/)【Source: Company announcement 2026-09-28】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-01】ZYDUS extends Sri Lanka Sunshine Healthcare joint-venture closing to December 2026** — Zydus Lifesciences and Sri Lanka's Sunshine Healthcare Lanka have executed a letter agreement postponing the closing date of their 50:50 joint venture, Zydus Sunshine Lifesciences, to December 2026 because conditions precedent remain outstanding. The equity commitment exceeds US$20 million for a formulations plant on roughly four acres in the Horana investment zone, where the foundation stone was laid on 26 June 2026. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/zydus-lifesciences-extends-sunshine-healthcare-lanka-jv-investment-deadline-to-december-2026-22048)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-01】

**【2026-10-04】AUROBINDO receives US FDA final approval for perampanel tablets in six strengths** — The approval covers 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 mg strengths, bioequivalent to the reference drug Fycompa, to be made at wholly owned subsidiary APL Healthcare's Unit-IV and launched in Q3FY27. IQVIA puts the US market for the product at about US$67 million for the twelve months to August 2026; the company now holds 599 US FDA ANDA approvals (574 final, 25 tentative). — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/regulatory/aurobindo-pharma-secures-usfda-final-approval-for-multi-strength-perampanel-tablets-22059)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-04】
