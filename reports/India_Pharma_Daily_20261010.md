## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-09】Draft rules to ease animal experimentation; institutional ethics panels to self-approve most studies** — The Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying has issued draft Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (Control and Supervision of Experiment, Breeding and Trading of Laboratory Animals) Rules, 2026. They replace the current body-size based classification with a four-tier pain-based one (mild, moderate, severe, non-recovery); registered Institutional Animal Ethics Committees (IAECs) may approve mild and moderate experiments, leaving only the "severe" tier with the Central Control and Supervision of Experiments on Animals (CCSEA). The draft also consolidates and supersedes the 1968 and 1998 rulebooks. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/experiments-on-animals-set-to-become-easier/134822465)【Source: MoFAHD draft rules 2026, consultation stage】

**【2026-10-08】57th GST Council delivers pharma-relevant tax measures (update)** — The Council recommended allowing input tax credit (ITC) on free physician samples and on expired stock destroyed where law requires it; refund of input services under the inverted duty structure from 1 November 2026, and of plant and machinery from 1 April 2027 spread over 60 months; acknowledgement of refund claims cut from 15 to 10 days with 90% of eligible claims sanctioned automatically after risk assessment; and facilitation of exports of services including clinical research, testing and contract manufacturing. — Source: [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2320934)【Source: PIB PRID 2320934 (2026-10-08)】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-08】FDA FY25 India inspection data: OAI rate 17.9%, data integrity flags CROs** — Gregory Smith, Country Director of the US FDA India Office, told an IPA Advanced GMP Workshop that of 207 inspections in India in FY25, 37 (17.9%) were classified Official Action Indicated, in line with the ~18% global average; US-based plants had the highest OAI rate (21.6%) and China the lowest (8%). The most frequent deficiencies were inadequate written records (20%), insufficient investigations (17%) and inadequate laboratory controls (12%). The FDA flagged data integrity issues at CROs including Raptim Research and Panexcell, requiring studies to be repeated. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulatory-update/fda-watch-india-mirrors-global-oai-trends-data-integrity-keeps-cros-under-lens/134834881)【Source: IPA Advanced GMP Workshop 2026-10-08】

**【2026-10-09】CEA article: after a decade of Make in India, Penicillin G is made locally again** — India's Chief Economic Adviser Nageswaran and co-authors wrote that PLI schemes have drawn ₹2.4 lakh crore of investment, about 83% of it in five sectors including electronics, pharmaceuticals and solar; Penicillin G, once imported entirely from China, is again produced in India. The article argues the next step is lower tariffs on intermediates, components and capital goods, noting domestic value addition in electronics is still only 18%–23%. — Source: [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/opinion/make-in-india-measured-against-wrong-decade/article71564859.ece)【Source: signed op-ed by CEA Nageswaran, 2026-10-09】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-09】AUROBINDO recalls about 113,000 bottles of acetaminophen in the US** — According to the US FDA enforcement report, the company's US arm recalled 112,932 bottles of acetaminophen in three separate actions (90,456 bottles of 500-count packs, 7,836 of 1,000-count packs and 14,640 of 200-count packs), initiated voluntarily on 18 September after consumer complaints of tablet discolouration; the FDA has not yet classified severity. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/aurobindo-pharma-recalls-over-1-1-lakh-bottles-of-paracetamol-in-us/134826942)【Source: USFDA Enforcement Report 2026-10】

**【2026-10-09】DR. REDDY'S Andhra FTO-11 plant gets 2 Form 483 observations after pre-approval inspection** — In an exchange filing the company said its Formulations Technical Operations FTO-11 site at Srikakulam underwent a US FDA pre-approval inspection from 5 to 7 October and received a Form 483 with two observations, which it will address within the stipulated timeline. The site makes injectables across therapeutic segments for the US and Europe; the nature of the observations was not disclosed. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/dr-reddys-andhra-facility-gets-2-observation-from-fda/134815901)【Source: company filing 2026-10-09】

**【2026-10-08】JUBILANT PHARMOVA's radiopharma unit to invest CAD 94.5 m in Quebec plant** — Canada's Ministry of Industry announced a CAD 23.8 m Strategic Response Fund contribution to Jubilant Radiopharma supporting a CAD 94.5 m project to modernise and expand its Kirkland, Quebec site into a global centre of excellence for radiopharmaceuticals; the project will double RUBY-FILL (rubidium-82 generator) capacity and create or retain 225 jobs, with the customer base having doubled in three years. — Source: [Government of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2026/10/government-of-canada-announces-new-investment-to-strengthen-canadas-domestic-radiopharmaceutical-production-capacity.html)【Source: Government of Canada news release 2026-10-08】
