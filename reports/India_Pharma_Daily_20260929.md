## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-28】DGTR initiates anti-dumping investigation into Penicillin G and its salts from China** — Acting on an application by Lyfius Pharma Pvt. Ltd. (Aurobindo group's Penicillin G plant at Kakinada SEZ), the sole eligible domestic producer, the Directorate General of Trade Remedies has initiated an anti-dumping investigation into imports of Penicillin G and its salts from China PR, classified under customs code 2941 10 10. The applicant alleges "material retardation" to the establishment of the domestic industry and seeks normal value determination for China under the non-market-economy methodology. The POI is 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. The Authority has included Virchow Petrochemicals within the domestic industry, while the applicant claims Virchow imported significant quantities of the subject goods. — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-penicillin-g-and-its-salts-peoples)【Source: DGTR F. No. 6/56/2026-DGTR · SETU AD/OI/057/2026, initiated 2026-09-28】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-28】Ind-Ra pegs FY27 Indian pharma revenue growth at ~10% on healthy CDMO/API demand** — India Ratings and Research (Ind-Ra) expects the Indian pharmaceutical industry to grow around 10% year on year in FY27, driven by domestic momentum, complex generic launches, Europe expansion and healthy CDMO/API demand. The US market, about one-third of industry exports, remains the key watchpoint on pricing, tariffs and geopolitics, with growth expected to be company-specific; USFDA inspections are expected to increase but are unlikely to cause significant disruption. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/financial-performance/ind-ra-pegs-pharma-fy27-revenue-growth-at-10-as-domestic-demand-cdmos-cushion-us-decline/134544237)【Source: Ind-Ra report, via ET Pharma 2026-09-28】

**【2026-09-28】Morepen files first US ANDA for Sitagliptin, entering US finished-dosage space** — Morepen Laboratories has submitted its first US ANDA for Sitagliptin Tablets USP in three strengths, marking its extension from APIs into the US finished-dosage market. The same company is the Indian applicant in the anti-dumping case on Montelukast Sodium. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/regulatory)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-09-28】SUN PHARMA secures global (ex-US, ex-China) rights to LIB Therapeutics' lerodalcibep** — Sun Pharma has signed an exclusive licensing and manufacturing agreement with LIB Therapeutics for the PCSK9 inhibitor Lyrokaul (lerodalcibep), a once-monthly subcutaneous injection already approved in the US and Europe, obtaining manufacturing and commercialisation rights in all markets outside the US and China, with Sun responsible for regulatory approvals in not-yet-approved territories. IQVIA estimates a corresponding market of about US$3.7 billion, growing at a 38% CAGR over the past two years. — Source: [Sun Pharma](https://www.sunpharma.com/media/press-releases)【Source: Sun Pharma announcement 2026-09-28】

**【2026-09-28】LUPIN receives USFDA tentative approval for Apixaban Oral Suspension** — Lupin's Apixaban Oral Suspension 1.25 mg/mL — a differentiated oral liquid version of the blood thinner Eliquis — has received tentative approval from the US FDA. Final approval depends on the patent or exclusivity status of the reference product. — Source: [Lupin](https://www.lupin.com/media/press-releases/lupin-receives-tentative-approval-from-u-s-fda-for-novel-apixaban-oral-suspension)【Source: Lupin announcement 2026-09-28】

**【2026-09-28】ZYDUS: USFDA inspection at Ahmedabad SEZ II plant concludes with 1 observation** — Per an exchange filing, the USFDA conducted a cGMP inspection at the company's Ahmedabad SEZ II manufacturing site from 21 to 28 September 2026, which concluded with one observation and no data-integrity-related observations; the company said it will address the observation expeditiously. A separate inspection of its New Jersey office pharmacovigilance system on 22–25 September ended with nil observations. — Source: [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/companies/zydus-life-share-price-usfda-inspection-at-ahmedabad-plant-concludes-with-1-observation-20000239.htm)【Source: Zydus Lifesciences exchange filing 2026-09-28】
