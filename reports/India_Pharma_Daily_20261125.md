## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-18】Pharmexcil seeks industry inputs on the EU Public Procurement Act, assessing impact on Indian drug and API exports** — The European Commission has proposed COM(2026) 590 final, revising the EU public procurement framework to introduce European preference, supply-chain resilience and strategic autonomy provisions, with possible implications for Indian manufacturers supplying medicines, APIs and pharmaceutical products to EU government and institutional tenders; Pharmexcil is collecting impact cases and data from exporters to submit consolidated feedback to India's Department of Commerce. — Source: [Pharmexcil circular](https://pharmexcil.com/circulars/viewcirculars/16209/ed2d1e5149276dbfac33a4fefd9c197f.html)【Basis: Pharmexcil Circular PXL/HO/Cir-099/2026-27, 2026-11-18】

**【2026-11-24】PCI vice president calls for reviving Section 42 dispensing privilege and taking AMR outreach beyond campuses** — Speaking at National Pharmacy Week 2026 (theme: antimicrobial resistance), Pharmacy Council of India vice president Jashubhai H. Chaudhari opposed any weakening of dispensing regulation, stressing that only registered pharmacists may dispense medicines, and asked each pharmacy student to educate 10 to 20 people in family and neighbourhood on rational antibiotic use, self-medication risks and course completion; PCI has set up an Academia-Industry Advisory Committee for industrial training. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=192101&sid=2)【Basis: National Pharmacy Week 2026, 2026-11-24, Sri Ramachandra University, Chennai】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-24】PMEC India 2026 opens; Department of Pharmaceuticals urges investment in advanced manufacturing and indigenous machinery** — The 19th edition runs 24–26 November at IEML, Greater Noida; Joint Secretary Satyaprakash T L called for greater investment in advanced manufacturing technologies and domestic machinery capability, while Senior Scientific Advisor Raj Shirumala said India must move from volume-driven output to higher-value innovation including next-generation biologics; organiser Informa cited tariff pressure, margin compression and supply-chain constraints as forces reshaping global pharma manufacturing, with rising weight for automation and digitalisation. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/pmec-india-2026-opens-puts-spotlight-on-the-next-phase-of-pharma-manufacturing/)

**【2026-11-24】GSK launches Blenrep in India, the country's first BCMA-targeted antibody-drug conjugate** — GlaxoSmithKline Pharmaceuticals India launched belantamab mafodotin as combination therapy for adults with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma after at least one prior line; the approval rests on the DREAMM-7 and DREAMM-8 phase III studies, with DREAMM-7 showing median progression-free survival of 36.6 months for the Blenrep-bortezomib-dexamethasone arm versus 13.4 months for the daratumumab-based comparator. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=192114&sid=2)
