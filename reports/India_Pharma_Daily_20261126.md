## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-25】DGFT revises two operational guidelines under Niryat Disha, widening freight and testing support for pharma MSMEs** — Under LIFT, freight reimbursement moves from a notified product list to all eligible merchandise of qualified MSMEs (negative list excepted); eligible areas expand to 308 low-export-intensity districts across 21 states and union territories; the minimum distance threshold drops from 200 km to 100 km with a 400 km cap on inland legs; land customs stations and integrated check posts are added as eligible routes, and air-freight support extends to Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. Under TRACE, eligible testing, inspection and certification entries rise from 462 to 600 (about 30% more). — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=192121&sid=1) · [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=192119&sid=1)【Basis: DGFT amendments to EPM–Niryat Disha operational guidelines, reported 2026-11-25】

**【2026-11-25】Health Ministry proposes amending Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, to allow immediate suspension of manufacturing licences** — Per a pharmabiz editorial, the Union Health Ministry has proposed amendments empowering regulators to suspend a manufacturing licence immediately where a drug fails specified critical quality parameters, without waiting for the full routine process. The proposal remains at draft stage; no official gazette text has been sighted. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/ArticleDetails.aspx?aid=192111&sid=3)【Basis: pharmabiz editorial, 2026-11-25, draft stage】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-25】October pharma exports at US$2.751 bn, up 10.55% YoY; imports US$8.76 bn, up 6.26%** — Per preliminary Ministry of Commerce and Industry data, October exports reached US$2.751 bn against US$2.489 bn a year earlier, up 19.41% in rupee terms. Imports of medicinal plants and pharmaceutical products were US$876.01 mn, up 6.26%. For April–October FY2027, exports totalled US$18.89 bn (+5.7%) and imports US$5.65 bn (+3%). September export growth was 4.7%, so October roughly doubled that pace. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=192122&sid=1)【Basis: Ministry of Commerce preliminary trade data, released 2026-11-25】
