## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-08-19】CDSCO Moves 4 CNS Drugs to Schedule H1 to Curb Abuse**
India's Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, in a gazette notification dated August 19, placed four central nervous system (CNS) drugs — Flupentixol (antipsychotic), Zopiclone (insomnia), Gabapentin (nerve pain) and Carisoprodol (muscle relaxant) — under Schedule H1 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act. Retailers and pharmacists must now keep detailed records of buyer details, prescribing doctor's name/address and quantity supplied, preserved for three years for inspection. The move follows a recommendation by the CDSCO Drugs Consultative Committee (DCC) five months ago, based on an expert committee report on misuse and intoxication. The notification has a 30-day public feedback period. According to IQVIA, the neurology/CNS therapy segment logged sales of ~₹17,439 crore (≈$1.82B) on a moving annual turnover basis between August 2025 and July 2026; pregabalin was recently added to Schedule H1 on similar grounds. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/centre-puts-stricter-controls-on-sale-of-4-cns-drugs-over-abuse-concerns-126082100936_1.html)
