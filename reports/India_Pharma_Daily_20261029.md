## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-28】NPPA publishes revised MRPs for 543 anti-cancer brands, effective 9 November** — Acting on its 17 October trade margin rationalisation for 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs, the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority released revised maximum retail prices for 543 brands covering breast, prostate, colon, ovarian and cervical cancer therapies plus immunotherapy, targeted therapy and chemotherapy-support drugs; sample cuts range from 57% to 90%, e.g. anastrozole 10-tablet pack falls from ₹695.76 to ₹73 and bevacizumab 4 ml vial from ₹21,767.70 to ₹5,930.13; the list reflects data submitted up to 27 October and will be updated continuously, with retailers and hospitals to charge the new prices from 9 November. — Source: [NPPA](https://nppa.gov.in/)【Source: PIB release 2026-10-28; S.O. 5690(E) 2026-10-17】

**【2026-10-28】DPIIT identifies about 120 products for domestic manufacturing, including pharma products and inputs (update)** — The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade has shortlisted some 120 products for a three-track localisation push: short-term import protection aimed mainly at Chinese supplies, cost-disability compensation via capital or turnover-linked incentives, and R&D support where technology is unavailable domestically; the list spans chemicals such as acetic acid, pharmaceutical products and their inputs, medical technology items, machine tools and construction equipment; tunnel boring machines are handled separately after Chinese export restrictions, and consultations with other agencies continue. — Source: [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/articleshowprint/135248521.cms)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-28】PIRAMAL PHARMA cuts Chinese raw-material procurement to single digits, framed as risk management** — Chairperson Nandini Piramal said China now accounts for a single-digit percentage of consolidated procurement spend, a move aimed at diversifying geopolitical risk rather than gaining a cost advantage; the company offers customers nearshoring and offshore options through sites in India, the UK and the US, with overseas plants handling ADCs, high-potency APIs and sterile injectables at higher margins; CDMO is expected to be the main revenue and margin driver over the next two to three years. — Source: [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/healthcare/piramal-pharma-cutting-china-exposure-to-manage-supply-chain-resilience-says-nandini-piramal/articleshow/135265887.cms)

**【2026-10-28】MOREPEN LABORATORIES Q2 revenue up 81% to ₹755 crore, exports up 131%** — A second consecutive record quarter: EBITDA ₹132 crore (+273%), net profit ₹89 crore (+117%), EBITDA margin 17.45% (+897 bps); CDMO revenue ₹253 crore, or 52% of combined API and CDMO revenue; exports ₹397 crore (+131%) and medical devices ₹208 crore (+65%); combined API and CDMO capacity stands at 614 kilolitres. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/morepen-laboratories-posts-record-rs-755-cr-q2-revenue-ebitda-soars-273-to-rs-132-cr-22266)

**【2026-10-28】SUDEEP PHARMA's 51,200-tonne Gujarat plant cleared by state FDA, adds glycinate capacity** — The greenfield site at Nandesari received a manufacturing licence from the Gujarat Food and Drug Control Administration with installed capacity of 51,200 MT a year; about 60% will serve the existing pharmaceutical, food and nutrition and specialty ingredients portfolio, with the rest directed to phosphates, glycinates, gluconates and citrates; capacity for higher-value molecules such as bisglycinates was constrained at the mother plant, and commercial supplies are expected from H2 FY27 subject to customer qualification. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/sudeep-pharma-gets-fda-nod-for-new-gujarat-facility-22262)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-28】CIPLA's in-licensed Sino Biopharmaceutical dual-epitope HER2 ADC meets primary endpoint in Phase III** — In the China-run APEX-Breast01 study of 543 patients with HER2-low recurrent or metastatic breast cancer, rolditamig deuderuxtecan delivered median progression-free survival of 16.4 months versus 5.5 months on chemotherapy, a 71% reduction in risk of progression or death, with an objective response rate of 68.8% versus 31%; Cipla holds exclusive development and commercialisation rights for India, South Africa and five other markets, with milestones of up to USD 123 million plus double-digit royalties, and says further development and regulatory work is needed in India. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/research-and-development/cipla-sino-bispecific-adc-cuts-cancer-progression-by-71-in-late-stage-china-trial/135245707)
