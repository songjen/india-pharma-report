## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-30】DGTR initiates material-injury anti-dumping probe into DCC from China** — India's Directorate General of Trade Remedies has initiated an anti-dumping investigation (material-retardation type) into imports of N,N′-dicyclohexylcarbodiimide (DCC) originating in or exported from China PR. DCC is a widely used coupling agent in peptide and API synthesis and in other fine-chemical dehydration reactions; the case runs in parallel with the ongoing China-related investigations into clavulanic acid, 6-APA, amoxycillin trihydrate, penicillin G and sodium cyanide. — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-ongoing-investigation)【Source: DGTR Initiation Notification 2026-09-30】

**【2026-10-05】State mark-up surveys push TMR policy review** — Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh have carried out surveys on pharmaceutical mark-ups, and the Department of Pharmaceuticals has told a Parliamentary Standing Committee that it is examining whether to write trade margin rationalisation (TMR) into the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013. Scheduled essential medicines currently use a 16% retailer margin to compute the ceiling price, while makers of non-scheduled formulations set their own price and may raise MRP by up to 10% a year. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/news/state-scrutiny-of-pharma-price-mark-ups-may-lead-to-review-of-tmr-policy-126100501088_1.html)【Source: DoP reply to Parliamentary Standing Committee (2026-08)】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-05】Grant Thornton–FICCI report: India must move from scale to trust** — India ranks third globally in pharmaceutical volume but 11th by value, exporting USD 30.5 billion of drugs to 191 countries in FY2024-25, the report notes. APIs and key starting materials remain heavily dependent on concentrated external sources, and although the bulk-drug PLI has attracted INR 45,774 crore of investment against a INR 15,000 crore outlay, it has not removed that structural dependence. Data integrity is flagged as the biggest gap: about 60% of FDA warning letters involving Indian pharma sites in FY2025 cited data-integrity issues, versus roughly 15% globally. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/india-news/india-pharma-sector-scale-to-trust-global-edge-grant-thornton-ficci-report-126100500519_1.html)【Source: Grant Thornton Bharat–FICCI report 2026-10-05】

**【2026-10-05】Serum Institute's quadrivalent HPV vaccine Cervavac secures WHO prequalification** — The recombinant quadrivalent vaccine covers HPV types 6, 11, 16 and 18 and is indicated for the prevention of cervical, vulvar, vaginal and anal cancers in girls and young women and anal cancer in boys and young men aged 9–26, on a two-dose schedule six months apart. Trials showed non-inferior efficacy against Merck's Gardasil; types 16 and 18 together account for more than 70% of cervical cancer cases globally. Prequalification opens access to UN agency and other public procurement channels. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/serum-institutes-hpv-vaccine-secures-pre-qualification-status-from-who/134707227)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-05】

**【2026-10-05】Fake-medicine racket's international trail points to a Turkiye labelling node** — Bengaluru's Special Investigation Team has found that the alleged kingpin travelled to Turkiye several times a year to procure original labels of a New York-based pharmaceutical company, which were brought to India and affixed to counterfeit medicines to pass them off as genuine. After his Turkish visa was cancelled he routed procurement through intermediaries in third countries including Azerbaijan. Investigators also found counterfeit medicines were sometimes mixed with genuine stock before distribution; the cross-state network is still being traced. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulatory-update/fake-medicine-racket-kingpins-turkiye-link-exposed/134683668)【Source: Bengaluru SIT investigation update 2026-10-05】

**【2026-10-05】Industry leaders say Indian pharma is shifting from generics to innovation-led growth** — Speaking at the Indian Foundation for Quality Management's third annual symposium, Sun Pharma Executive Chairman Dilip Shanghvi, Biocon Executive Chairperson Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw and Glenmark Chairman Glenn Saldanha agreed the next decade is critical for India to move from "pharmacy of the world" to an innovation-led industry, urging sustained R&D investment and faster review and clinical timelines, and citing China's biopharma rise as a model. Existing schemes such as BRIDGE, RDI and Bioshakti were cited as levers. — Source: [PTI (Rediff Money)](https://money.rediff.com/news/market/indian-pharma-shifts-to-innovation-led-growth-leaders-say/55599120261005)【Source: IFQM third annual symposium 2026-10-05】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-05】ZYDUS and MSN launch the first generic riociguat (Adempas) in the US across all five strengths** — Zydus Lifesciences and MSN Laboratories announced the US launch of riociguat tablets in 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 and 2.5 mg strengths, the first approved generic equivalent of Adempas. The product was developed and is manufactured by MSN, with Zydus handling US commercialisation. According to Bayer's 2025 annual report, global Adempas sales were about EUR 745 million (approximately USD 850 million). — Source: [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/companies/zydus-msn-launch-first-generic-version-of-adempas-in-us-20004985.htm)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-05】
