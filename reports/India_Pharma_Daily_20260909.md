## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-04】Drugmakers oppose NPPA proposal to make manufacturers solely liable for medicine overpricing** — Industry bodies led by the Federation of Pharma Entrepreneurs (FOPE) have written to the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) opposing a proposed amendment that would hold manufacturers solely liable for overpriced medicine sales while exempting retail chemists, hospitals and nursing homes from prosecution. FOPE argues that Para 26 of the Drugs (Prices Control) Order (DPCO), 2013 already distributes responsibility across the chain, that liability for the final price charged to patients should sit with the party actually setting it at the point of sale, and that manufacturers' duties are limited to declaring accurate MRPs via IPDMS and complying with price notifications. The All India Organisation of Chemists and Druggists (AIOCD), which represents retailers and distributors, welcomed the retail exemption and sought no retrospective liability on existing stock, a 75–90 day transition period and clear statutory protection for retailers, stockists, distributors, C&F agents and hospital pharmacies — Source: [Economic Times](https://economictimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/pharmaceuticals/pharma-companies-reject-proposal-for-sole-accountability-in-medicine-overpricing/articleshow/133745399.cms)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-07】Pharmexcil chairman expects more large exporters to follow Sun Pharma's US MFN pricing model** — After Sun Pharma became the first Indian major to sign a Most-Favoured-Nation (MFN) pricing agreement with the US to shield itself from potential Section 232 tariffs, Pharmexcil Chairman Namit Joshi said more large exporters are likely to adopt such models to protect value amid rising bargaining power of US distributors and pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs) and compressed margins. Exporters note that annual contracts are no longer prevalent, competition forces price matching on the same molecule, and some products are already sold to the US below prices charged in Europe and Africa, pushing companies toward stronger distribution capabilities and deeper value-chain integration — Source: [The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/business/2026/Sep/07/more-indian-pharma-exporters-may-follow-sun-pharma-on-us-mfn-pricing-to-bypass-tariff)

**【2026-09-07】USFDA issues Form 483 with 7 observations to Indoco Remedies after Goa sterile plant inspection** — The USFDA inspected the company's Plant II (Sterile) facility in Goa from 27 August to 4 September 2026 and issued seven observations in Form 483; the company said it will submit its response within the stipulated timeframe. Indoco is a fully integrated formulations-and-API manufacturer that returned to profit in Q1 FY27 with a net profit of ₹64.99 crore (≈US$6.85 mn), versus a year-ago net loss — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/capital-market-news/indoco-remedies-drops-after-usfda-flags-7-observations-at-goa-plant-126090700175_1.html)

**【2026-09-08】FDA warning letter and import detention hit Gujarat API supplier Shoolin Pharma Chem** — A warning letter (signed 18 August, made public in early September) details CGMP failures found during an April 2026 inspection: unsanitary conditions (residues on walls and equipment, corroded product lines, an employee wearing open-toed sandals near open processing), inadequate cleaning of shared multi-drug equipment, missing raw QC data behind certificate-of-analysis results, and an ineffective quality unit. The company makes 14 APIs — including sildenafil citrate, tadalafil and pregabalin — sold in bulk to US compounding pharmacies; products have been subject to detention under Import Alert 66-40 since 11 August, and a US market recall was initiated — Source: [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/pharmalot/2026/09/08/fda-warns-api-supplier-residue-corroded-production-line-sandals)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-09-08】Sun Pharma earns investment-grade ratings (Moody's Baa1 / S&P BBB+) on its US$11.75 bn Organon acquisition** — Both agencies assigned investment-grade long-term issuer ratings with stable outlooks following Sun Pharma's binding bid for 100% of Organon & Co. at a US$11.75 bn enterprise value. S&P expects the deal to nearly double Sun's revenue to about ₹1.3 trillion (≈US$14 bn) by FY28, with Organon contributing an estimated 40–45% of combined group EBITDA, bringing scale comparable to Teva and Viatris. The transaction is expected to close by March 2027; Sun has secured a US$12 bn committed acquisition bridge loan and held ~US$3.6 bn in cash as of 31 March 2026 — Source: [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/pharmaceuticals/sun-pharma-gets-ratings-boost-from-moodys-sp-over-usd-11-75-bn-organon-co-acquisition-bid/articleshow/133920411.cms)

**【2026-09-08】Biocon signs 10-year Brazil PDP supply deal for Pertuzumab with Bahiafarma and Bionovis** — The consortium received 100% allocation under Brazil's 10-year Productive Development Partnership (PDP) programme for Pertuzumab, a monoclonal antibody for HER2-positive breast cancer not yet manufactured locally, gaining exclusive access to Brazil's public healthcare market (SUS), which accounts for ~70% of national demand. Biocon will receive milestone payments and a revenue share over 10 years, with phased localisation of production in the mid-to-long term — Source: [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/stocks/news/biocon-shares-rise-2-on-10-year-pertuzumab-supply-deal-for-breast-cancer-therapy-in-brazil/amp_articleshow/133910110.cms)

## 4. Risk Alerts

1. **US 100% tariff on patented pharmaceuticals takes full effect on 29 September** (Section 232, Proclamation 11020): generics and biosimilars are temporarily exempt, but importers of patented drugs and their APIs enter the final three-week shipping window; the MFN pricing model may spread from Sun Pharma to more mid-to-large exporters, forcing a trade-off between tariff exposure and pricing concessions plus US localisation commitments.
2. **India's FY26 pharma exports to the US stood at ~US$9.47 bn, down nearly 10% YoY** (Pharmexcil data): attributed to a high base, channel destocking and product cycles rather than structural demand weakness, yet rising distributor/PBM bargaining power is structurally compressing export margins, pressuring firms with high US dependence.
3. **A dense run of FDA enforcement signals on Indian sites** (Shoolin import alert on APIs + Indoco sterile-plant 483 + the earlier Cohance 483) spans hygiene, aseptic processing and data integrity; API/formulation exporters should strengthen self-inspection as individual plant findings can spill over into buyer compliance screening.
