## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-09】DGTR extends PUC/PCN comments deadline a second time to 21 Nov in the China and EU clavulanic acid anti-dumping case, ruling out further extension** — The notice moves the deadline for comments on the scope of the product under consideration and the PCN methodology from 30 Oct to 21 Nov, with submissions to be filed on the SETU portal in confidential and non-confidential versions; the case was initiated on 30 Sep under File No. 6/51/2026-DGTR against China and the EU. — Source: [DGTR](https://dgtr.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-11/Notification_2nd_Extension_Clavulanic_acid_tracked%20%281%29.pdf)【Source: DGTR notice 2026-11-09, File No. 6/51/2026-DGTR, SETU Case ID AD/OI/054/2026】

**【2026-11-14】Pharma department weighs extending trade margin cap to high-cost anti-infectives and ICU drugs; compulsory licensing a last resort** — Secretary Manoj Joshi said the government is examining widening the trade margin rationalisation from 137 anti-cancer drugs to high-cost hospital-administered medicines, focusing on anti-infectives, emergency and ICU drugs, with no intervention intended below ₹100; compulsory licensing would be used only after price negotiations, bulk purchase, voluntary licensing and patient assistance are exhausted; a decision on regulatory data protection is still pending. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/compulsory-licensing-of-drugs-only-after-exhausting-all-other-measures-pharma-secy/135820325)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-14】Eighteen patent filings on lenacapavir in India prompt patient groups to seek rejection of secondary patents** — Gilead has filed at least 18 applications in India covering the compound, salt forms, formulations, dosing regimens and methods of use, with granted patents running to 2042; patient groups have written to the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade asking for rigorous examination and rejection, calling the pattern evergreening; only six voluntary licensees, including Dr. Reddy's, can make the product, with generic injections projected at about USD 40 per patient per year across 120 low- and middle-income countries. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/breakthrough-hiv-drug-sees-18-patent-filings-may-delay-affordable-generics/135820604)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-11-09】AUROBINDO's Eugia recalls a lot of vancomycin injection nationwide in the US** — A glass particle was found in the vial; the affected lot is 3VM26026A, NDC 55150-471-01, expiring July 2028, shipped to wholesalers nationwide between 2 and 15 September and recalled to the hospital and user level; the company said no adverse events had been reported and is arranging returns and replacement. — Source: [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/eugia-us-llc-fka-auro-medics-pharma-llc-issues-voluntary-nationwide-recall-vancomycin-hydrochloride)【Source: FDA company announcement 2026-11-06; FDA publish date 2026-11-09】

**【2026-11-13】ALKEM's new CEO sets chronic-care expansion as priority, anti-infectives remain the domestic lead** — New CEO Amrut Naik said chronic therapies are only 24–25% of the portfolio against an industry split of about 65:35, and the company will focus on diabetes and cardiovascular while extending into neuropsychiatry and dermatology; chronic sales grew 22.4% in the September quarter, 640 basis points ahead of the Indian pharma market, and domestic sales rose 9.5% to ₹3,027 crore; Alkem remains first in anti-infectives and third overall in India. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/expanding-chronic-care-portfolio-key-focus-area-for-alkems-new-ceo/135789283)
