**【2026-10-22】Modi says pharma PLI has drawn over ₹5,000 crore of investment with 40-plus projects live; Penicillin-G production resumes at Kakinada (update)** — Addressing the NDTV World Summit, Prime Minister Narendra Modi cited the resumption of Penicillin-G output at Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, as evidence of falling dependence on imported key inputs, and said the pharma PLI has attracted more than ₹5,000 crore of investment with over 40 projects having commenced operations, focused on building a domestic API supply chain. — Source: [NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/india/ndtv-world-summit-2026-live-updates-pm-modi-s-jaishankar-nikhil-kamath-andrew-ng-among-key-speakers-12218267) · [Prokerala](https://www.prokerala.com/news/articles/a1827657.html)【Source: PM's address at NDTV World Summit, 2026-10-22】

**【2026-10-22】India Chem 2026 opens: BHAVYA Rasayan chemical park scheme takes shape, ₹1,000 crore central funding per park with state matching of at least ₹500 crore** — The 14th India Chem 2026 runs in Mumbai from 22 to 24 October; Chemicals and Fertilizers Minister J. P. Nadda called the scheme a key lever, with the Centre funding ₹1,000 crore per chemical park and states required to contribute at least ₹500 crore, three dedicated parks in the first tranche, and site selection anchored on port, rail and road connectivity; India targets a $300 billion chemicals market by 2030 and $1 trillion by 2040. — Source: [News On Air](https://newsonair.gov.in/india-aims-one-trillion-dollar-chemicals-market-by-2040-says-union-minister-j-p-nadda)【Source: Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals release at India Chem 2026, 2026-10-22】

**【2026-10-22】Maharashtra chemical industry policy to be finalised in first week of November; Palghar chemical park and a bulk drug park planned** — Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis said at India Chem 2026 that the state's dedicated chemical policy will be completed in the first week of November, with four pillars including domestic manufacturing of critical raw materials and APIs and import substitution; the state plans to acquire 2,500–5,000 acres at Palghar near the upcoming Vadhvan Port for a chemical park and has proposed a bulk drug park; Maharashtra accounts for about 19% of India's chemical value added and 17% of chemical exports. — Source: [Nation Press](https://www.nationpress.com/national/cm-fadnavis-unveils-chemical-policy-palghar-park-at-india-chem-2026)【Source: CM Fadnavis address at India Chem 2026, 2026-10-22】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-22】India's chemical input producer prices up 16.7% y/y in September, versus 4.0% for pharmaceuticals** — Official statistics show the producer price index for chemicals and chemical products rose 16.7% year on year in September (2.4 percentage points faster than August), while basic pharmaceuticals and preparations rose 4.0% (0.6 points faster); pharmaceutical industrial output grew 1.6% y/y in August. Input prices are outpacing output prices, intensifying the cost squeeze on formulators. — Source: [IndexBox](https://app.indexbox.io/indicators/3006/356/)【Source: MoSPI producer price and industrial production indices, 2026-09】

**【2026-10-22】Beta Drugs H1 revenue up 29.2%, API segment up 29%; own capacity to start in Q4 FY27 to cut KSM dependence** — Consolidated revenue was ₹263.04 crore with profit after tax of ₹34.3 crore, up 43.5%, and gross margin up 486 basis points to 56.21%; about ₹26 crore has been invested in API expansion and backward integration with a further ₹35 crore planned, and the new capacity starts in Q4 FY27 to reduce reliance on KSM suppliers; tight supply of platinum-group APIs pushed some oncology formulation orders into the second half. — Source: [Kalki Media](https://kalkinemedia.com/in/news/announcements/beta-drugs-nsebeta-announces-q2-fy27-financial-results-post-board-meeting)【Source: company filing (NSE: BETA) 2026-10-22】
