### [2026-08-11] Draft Drugs, Medical Devices & Cosmetics Bill 2026 Unveiled; Med-Tech Industry Opposes "Drug-Style" Regulation (MoHFW / ET HealthWorld)
On Aug 7, the Health Ministry drafted the Drugs, Medical Devices and Cosmetics Bill 2026 (draft) to replace the 1940 Drugs and Cosmetics Act. On Aug 8, multiple medical device industry associations wrote jointly to the Health Minister opposing the regulation of devices under a drug-centric framework: the draft proposes 1–7 years of imprisonment for labelling errors, documentation lapses and engineering defects (even without patient harm), and would empower the Drugs Controller General (DCGI) to issue binding directions to state drug regulators. The draft is currently in the inter-ministerial consultation stage. Covering drugs, devices, cosmetics and clinical trials, it represents the largest overhaul of India's pharma regulatory framework in 86 years.
Source: [ET HealthWorld (via Weibo repost)](https://weibo.com/ttarticle/p/show?id=2309405331480506400978)

### [2026-08-10] Kerala Drug Control Cracks Down on "Misleading Discount Boards" at Pharmacies, Enforcing DPCO Price Framework (The Hindu)
Kerala's State Drugs Control department has begun acting against medical shops displaying boards offering arbitrary discounts, acting on a circular by the State Drugs Controller based on a Kerala High Court ruling of June 19. Drug inspectors and assistant controllers have been authorised to check shops for misleading advertisements that influence purchasing decisions. The court clarified that **selling below the maximum retail price is legal and genuine discounts to consumers are not prohibited — only misleading or vague discount claims are restricted**. Under DPCO 2013, NPPA fixes wholesaler/retailer margins of 8%–16% for controlled drugs (e.g., insulin) and 10%–20% for decontrolled products.
Source: [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/kerala/display-boards-offering-arbitrary-discount-on-drugs-to-face-action-in-kerala/article71328378.ece)
