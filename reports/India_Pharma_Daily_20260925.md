## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-23】DGTR issues positive preliminary anti-dumping finding on Chinese synthetic musk, opens copolymer polyol probe the same day** — India's Ministry of Commerce and Industry issued two China-related notices on 23 September: ① a **positive preliminary finding** on tonalid (synthetic musk) originating in or exported from China, recommending a provisional anti-dumping duty of **USD 3,967 per tonne** under HS 2914 39 90; ② initiation of an anti-dumping investigation into copolymer polyol with hydroxyl value ≥23.5, covering HS 3907 2010/2090 and 3907 2910/2990, with interested parties required to file through the SETU portal within **37 days** of initiation. — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-investigation-in-india)【Source: DGTR Preliminary Findings 2026-09-23 (Ethenone NCV PF) / Initiation Notification 2026-09-23】

**【2026-09-24】PIB marks 12 years of Make in India: pharma turnover at ₹4.72 lakh crore, third globally by volume** — In its 12-year factsheet on Make in India (launched 25 September 2014), the Press Information Bureau said India's pharmaceutical industry recorded FY2024-25 turnover of **₹4,71,898 crore (about USD 53 billion)**, a **9.5% CAGR** since 2020-21, ranking **third globally by volume** and 11th by value; **domestic medical device output rose 48.2%**, from ₹28,000 crore in 2019-20 to ₹41,500 crore in 2024-25. The factsheet also cites **Miqnaf**, India's first domestically developed macrolide antibiotic in three decades. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2314447)【Source: PIB PRID 2314447】

**【2026-09-24】NHRC proposes a national central drug registry with dynamic QR-code traceability** — The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) has recommended to CDSCO a **National Drug Data Registration Certificate (NDDRC)** requiring all medicines — branded, branded generics, unbranded generics and contract-manufactured or export products — to be mandatorily registered through a central government portal; a **drug trace-and-track portal** recording manufacturer, composition, batch and distribution chain; and **dynamically and uniquely serialised** QR codes on packs to prevent duplication. CDSCO has referred the proposals to the **Drug Consultative Committee (DCC)**. The move follows **283** spurious and adulterated samples detected nationwide in FY26. — Source: [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/news/india/nhrc-proposes-central-drug-registry-real-time-tracking-to-curb-spurious-medicines/amp_articleshow/134444789.cms)【Source: NHRC recommendations (forwarded to CDSCO in August 2026)】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-24】CRISIL: Indian pharma revenue to grow 11–13% in FY27, margins to compress 150–200 bps** — Based on a review of about 190 Indian drugmakers, CRISIL Ratings expects FY27 revenue growth of **11–13%**: exports up **14–16%** in rupee terms and the domestic market up **9–11%**. Raw material, energy and freight inflation is expected to compress operating margins by **150–200 basis points to 21–21.5%**, with differentiated US launches and inventory normalisation only partly offsetting pricing pressure. Chronic-therapy price revisions are seen at 5–6% with volume growth recovering to 4–5%; debt-to-EBITDA is expected to stay near **1.2x**. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/financial-performance/crisil-pegs-pharma-fy27-revenue-growth-at-11-13-but-cost-pressures-may-squeeze-margins/134466948)【Source: CRISIL Ratings report 2026-09-24】

**【2026-09-22】Pfizer India responds to Karnataka FDA crackdown: copied batches withdrawn, probe supported** — After Karnataka FDA cancelled 16 and suspended 8 drug licences linked to the Bidadi repacking case (goods worth about ₹5.05 crore seized), Pfizer India said **patient safety and product quality are its highest priorities** and that it has worked with the authorities over the past month, including **withdrawing Pfizer batches whose numbers were copied to avoid confusion, helping differentiate genuine from counterfeit product and providing technical data on affected batches**. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/pfizer-india-speaks-up-after-karnataka-fda-crackdown-says-patient-safety-is-top-priority/134449178)【Source: Pfizer India statement 2026-09-22】

**【2026-09-23】ERS Genomics and Sai Life Sciences sign non-exclusive CRISPR/Cas9 licence** — ERS Genomics, the CRISPR licensing company, announced a non-exclusive licence agreement with Indian CRDMO **Sai Life Sciences**, granting rights to use Cas9 gene-editing technology in drug discovery and development services — extending the Indian CRDMO service frontier into gene editing and other new modalities. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/ers-genomics-and-sai-life-sciences-sign-crispr-cas9-license-agreement/)
