## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-27】Karnataka Health Minister writes to Union Health Minister: high-value drugs sold at up to 70x procurement cost** — Karnataka Health Minister U.T. Khader has written to Union Health Minister J.P. Nadda stating that high-value medicines reaching hospitals through distributors and stockists — oncology, nephrology and HIV drugs, implants and surgical consumables — show gaps between procurement price and printed MRP of "sometimes 30 times to more than 70 times", with hospitals retaining an undisclosed excess margin. He has sought a national study and proposed capping the patient-facing price at the lower of the applicable statutory price, the printed MRP, or the hospital's net acquisition cost plus a notified reasonable service margin and applicable taxes. — Source: [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/cancer-kidney-aids-drugs-sold-at-up-to-70-times-procurement-cost-karnataka-health-minister/article71516820.ece)

**【2026-09-25】Karnataka may cut C&F agents and stockists out of hospital drug procurement** — Amid the Bidadi spurious-drug investigation, Health Minister Khader said the state is considering requiring hospitals to buy medicines directly from manufacturers, removing carrying-and-forwarding agents and stockists to cut commissions and fix accountability. The plan draws on Uttar Pradesh's traceability rules for discounted institutional supplies — billing the intended institution directly and marking packs "institutional supply" — and would tighten qualification requirements for C&F agents; a final decision awaits a national conclave of drug control officials in Bengaluru in October. — Source: [Times of India](http://m.timesofindia.com/city/bengaluru/karnataka-may-cut-drug-agents-stockists-from-hospital-supply-chain/articleshow/134490577.cms)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-24】IOL Chemicals' Fenofibrate API receives EDQM Certificate of Suitability** — Indian API maker IOL Chemicals and Pharmaceuticals has obtained a Certificate of Suitability (CEP) from the European Directorate for the Quality of Medicines & HealthCare (EDQM) for its Fenofibrate API under Process II, underpinning registration and supply of the fibrate lipid-lowering API in EU regulated markets. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/briefs-biocon-malaysia-iol-chemicals-and-natco-pharma-21986)【Source: company announcement 2026-09-24】
