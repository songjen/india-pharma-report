## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-29】(Update) Supreme Court resumes drug-pricing hearing: questions uniform 16% retail margin and mandatory in-house hospital pharmacies** — A Supreme Court bench resumed hearing a batch of petitions on drug pricing and mandatory generic prescribing, asking why the 16% retailer margin set under the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013 is not applied uniformly to scheduled, non-scheduled and patented medicines. Citing an essential cancer drug supplied to retailers at about ₹3,000 but sold to patients at an MRP of ₹27,000, the bench pressed on where the near ten-fold gap goes, calling the situation "carnage". Petitioners submitted that about 82% of medicines fall outside the scheduled category; the real beneficiary is private hospitals rather than drugmakers. No order was passed; the matter was posted to 12 October 2026. — Source: [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/supreme-court-questions-steep-mark-ups-on-cancer-drugs-calls-pricing-disparity-carnage/article71523314.ece)·[CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/india/sc-questions-16-pc-retailer-margin-rule-seeks-uniform-pricing-for-drugs-devices-20000597.htm)【Source: Supreme Court hearing 2026-09-29; next hearing 2026-10-12; DPCO 2013】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-29】Symbiotec Pharmalab receives USFDA Establishment Inspection Report for Pithampur plant, classified VAI** — The company disclosed that the US FDA has issued an Establishment Inspection Report for its Pithampur SEZ manufacturing site in Madhya Pradesh, classified Voluntary Action Indicated, meaning the agency noted matters for improvement but requires no action. The inspection covered 10–14 August 2026; the report reached the exchanges on 29 September. The company is one of the leading global suppliers of corticosteroid and steroidal-hormone APIs, operating continuous-flow chemistry for progesterone. — Source: [EquityPandit](https://www.equitypandit.com/ellenbarrie-industrial-gases-shares-rise-on-rs-481-crore-bhel-contract-win)【Source: company exchange disclosure 2026-09-29】

**【2026-09-29】Anupam Rasayan completes 48.2% controlling acquisition of Bliss GVS Pharma, building a ₹4,000-crore-plus integrated platform** — The company completed the controlling stake purchase at ₹299 per share (₹1,750 crore ≈ US$182 million), funded by a ₹300-crore term loan plus about ₹1,450 crore raised through non-controlling, non-voting instruments led by Bain Capital. Combined pro-forma revenue is projected above ₹4,000 crore (≈US$416 million) with EBITDA of about ₹834 crore. The three acquisitions secure fluoride raw materials (Tanfac — hydrofluoric acid and potassium fluoride), a US manufacturing base (Jayhawk) and finished-dosage capacity (Bliss GVS's six USFDA/EU-GMP plants, currently at about 30% utilisation, targeted at 70–80%). — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/anupam-rasayan-completes-rs-1750-crore-acquisition-of-bliss-gvs-pharma-to-build-global-platform-22011)【Source: company announcement 2026-09-29】

**【2026-09-29】US Section 232 pharma tariffs take effect: India among 20 jurisdictions granted zero tariff on specialty drugs; generics already outside scope** — The US Commerce Department published the list of eligible jurisdictions in the Federal Register; India and 19 other jurisdictions receive a zero ad valorem tariff from 00:01 US Eastern on 29 September 2026 on specialty categories including rare-disease, fertility, cell and gene therapies and antibody-drug conjugates, plus associated ingredients. The 100% tariff took effect for covered companies outside the initial annex; generic pharmaceuticals and their ingredients remain outside the Section 232 pharma tariffs. Pharmexcil chairman Namit Joshi said the qualifying share of India's exports cannot yet be determined and requires product-level tariff-classification data, adding that the exemption eases pressure to localise manufacturing for the covered products. — Source: [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/us-exempts-select-indian-specialty-drugs-from-100-tariff/article71524356.ece)【Source: US Commerce Department/BIS Federal Register notice 2026-09-23; effective 2026-09-29】
