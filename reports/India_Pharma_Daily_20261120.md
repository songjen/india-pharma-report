## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**[2026-11-19] Health Ministry moves to classify antimicrobials as "new drugs", requiring central clearance before state manufacturing licences** — A gazette notification proposing amendments to the NDCT Rules would bring antimicrobials (antibiotics, antifungals, antivirals) under the new-drug framework: manufacturers must obtain Central Licensing Authority approval before applying to the State Licensing Authority, with a 30-day public comment window. The stated rationale is that some antimicrobials have been licensed without bioavailability/bioequivalence and stability studies, and substandard products fuel resistance. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=192009&sid=2) · [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/govt-plans-central-licensing-of-antimicrobials-to-tackle-drug-resistance/135990998)【Basis: NDCT Rules 2019 draft amendment, Rule 2(1)(w)(v), gazette notification 2026-11-19】

**[2026-11-19] Separate wholesale licence proposed for bulk drugs (Form 19-BC／20-BC), split from formulations** — A single licence currently covers wholesale of both formulations and bulk drugs; the draft creates dedicated application and licence forms, requires the licence to record the categories of bulk drugs handled (including NDPS-controlled substances), and mandates personal supervision by a competent person. The Drugs Consultative Committee (67th meeting, 2025-11-17) and DTAB (93rd meeting, 2026-02-16) have cleared the proposal. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=192007&sid=2)【Basis: Drugs Rules 1945 draft amendment, Form 19-BC／20-BC】

**[2026-11-19] Good Storage and Distribution Practices to be written into law; GDP mandatory for manufacturers, importers and traders** — A draft gazette notification dated 2026-11-04 would add a GSDP definition to Rule 2 and require licensees to comply with CDSCO-drafted, DTAB-adopted good distribution practice guidelines, embedding the condition in Rules 26, 33A and 65, thereby giving legal force to what are now guidelines. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=192011&sid=2)【Basis: Drugs Rules 1945 draft amendment, Gazette of India 2026-11-04】

**[2026-11-20] BioPharma SHAKTI (₹10,000 crore, ~US$1.07 bn) targeted for launch in January** — The Department of Pharmaceuticals joint secretary said the final policy will go to the cabinet and implementation will begin in January. The scheme supports CRDMOs manufacturing biopharmaceuticals and strengthens the clinical research ecosystem over FY27–FY31, with the first disbursement cycle focused on non-communicable diseases (diabetes, cancer, autoimmune); year-wise allocations are not yet fixed. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/centre-to-roll-out-biopharma-shakti-by-january-next-year/136005273)【Basis: OPPI Annual Summit 2026, remarks by Satyaprakash T L, Joint Secretary, DoP, 2026-11-20】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**[2026-11-18] Cipla searched by DRI over classification and duty exemption of an imported finished product** — The 16 November search covered the Vikhroli corporate office and the Bhiwandi depot, examining whether an imported formulation was correctly classified under customs rules and eligible for duty exemption. Cipla said there is no material impact on financials or operations; the stock closed 0.7% lower at ₹1,383 (~US$14.8) and is down more than 7% year to date. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/cipla-faces-dri-scrutiny-over-imported-finished-product/135922588)

**[2026-11-19] CDSCO begins compiling a national list of approved medicines, seeking pre-1988 approval records** — The list will cover DCGI-approved single-ingredient drugs and FDCs, products examined by the Kokate Committee, pre-1988 approvals, formulations in the Indian Pharmacopoeia and National Formulary of India, and vitamin combinations. With records scattered across approval routes, industry questioned how regulators can verify that marketed medicines are approved. BDMA, IDMA, IPA and other associations attended. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulatory-update/drug-regulator-seeks-pre-1988-approval-records-to-compile-national-list-of-medicines/135976266)

**[2026-11-19] Draft Schedule M amendment allows discretionary relaxation for medicinal gases, empty gelatin capsules and disinfectant fluids** — A note proposed at the end of Part XIII would let the licensing authority or central licence approving authority modify space and equipment requirements for these categories, with reasons recorded in writing; industry had argued the revised Schedule M does not prescribe specific requirements for such products. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=192012&sid=2)【Basis: Schedule M draft amendment, DTAB recommendation 2026-11】

**[2026-11-19] Piramal exercises call option to buy remaining 26% of Yapan Bio** — The move takes Piramal Pharma's stake from 74% to 100%, subject to completion. Yapan Bio provides process development, characterisation and Phase I/II GMP manufacturing for vaccines and biologics; deal value and share transfer date are not yet finalised. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/piramal-pharma-moves-to-take-full-ownership-of-yapan-bio/)
