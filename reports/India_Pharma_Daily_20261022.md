## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-21】Sanofi's Qfitlia (fitusiran) wins CDSCO marketing authorisation for haemophilia A and B, as few as six injections a year** — Indicated for routine prophylaxis to prevent or reduce bleeding episodes in patients aged 12 years and older with haemophilia A or B, with or without inhibitors; based on the ATLAS Phase III programme, with dosing guided by antithrombin levels; India has roughly 28,900 registered haemophilia patients, most still on on-demand therapy. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/cdsco-approves-sanofis-qfitlia-for-haemophilia-a-and-b-in-india/)【Source: CDSCO marketing authorisation 2026-10-21】

**【2026-10-21】Argentina opens direct provincial drug imports and accepts WHO/PAHO/PIC-S GMP certificates, enabling direct supplies of Indian medicines and APIs** — Under the revised framework provinces may import medicines directly, accepting GMP certificates issued by MERCOSUR authorities, PIC-S member agencies and WHO/PAHO-recognised regulators, without ANMAT on-site inspections; India's pharma exports to Argentina were USD 22.25 million in FY26 (+22.8%) and USD 10.28 million in April–July FY27 (-2.16%). — Source: [Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/news/india-argentina-push-for-direct-pharma-trade-as-tucum-n-eyes-supplies-11792578022977.html)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-21】DR. REDDY'S wins DCGI approval for Zytorvi (toripalimab) in first-line oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma, its second India indication** — Approved with cisplatin and paclitaxel for first-line treatment of unresectable advanced, recurrent or metastatic ESCC; JUPITER-06 showed a 42% reduction in risk of progression or death and median overall survival of 17.7 versus 12.9 months; the India label does not require PD-L1 expression testing, and the licence covers 21 countries. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/drug-approvals-and-launches/dr-reddys-wins-cdsco-nod-to-expand-zytorvis-use-in-gi-cancer/135086536)【Source: company announcement 2026-10-21】

**【2026-10-21】ZYDUS gets FDA final approval for estradiol/levonorgestrel transdermal system with 180-day CGT exclusivity** — The ANDA was designated a competitive generic therapy, qualifying for 180-day exclusivity upon first commercial marketing; US sales were USD 45.7 million for the 12 months to August 2026 (IQVIA); the product will be made at the Moraiya plant in Gujarat. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/drug-approvals-and-launches/zydus-gets-fda-nod-for-hormone-replacement-therapy-with-180-exclusivity/135087189)【Source: company announcement 2026-10-21】

**【2026-10-21】ZYDUS biologics CDMO Zylidac Bio partners Prolific Machines to move light-controlled protein expression to GMP** — A 50-litre tech transfer at the Emeryville site comes first, followed by scale-up of client programmes to 250 litres and GMP manufacturing; the illumination hardware retrofits existing bioreactors, targeting difficult-to-express proteins such as multi-specific antibodies, fusion proteins and cytokines. — Source: [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/lifestyle/healthcare/from-lab-to-production-line-zydilac-bio-bets-on-light-powered-protein-manufacturing-20016371.htm)【Source: company announcement 2026-10-21】
