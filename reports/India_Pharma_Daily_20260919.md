## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-17】DGTR issues affirmative final findings on Ethambutol Hydrochloride; duty on non-cooperative Chinese producers raised to US$7,234/MT** — India's Directorate General of Trade Remedies (DGTR), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, has issued affirmative final findings in the anti-dumping investigation into Ethambutol Hydrochloride (an anti-TB API) originating in or exported from China PR and Thailand, recommending a **five-year anti-dumping duty** as follows: **Wuhan Wuyao Pharmaceutical Co., Limited (China) US$5,347/MT**, **any other Chinese producer US$7,234/MT**, and **all Thai producers US$6,584/MT**; the goods fall under Indian customs HS codes 2905 14 10 and 2905 14 90. The case (AD(OI)-22/2025, File No. 6/25/2025-DGTR) was initiated on 2025-09-23 on an application by **Lupin Limited**, India's sole domestic producer; the period of investigation is 2024-04-01 to 2025-03-31. Preliminary findings of 2026-01-30 had proposed provisional duties of US$5,124 / 6,096 / 6,513 per MT, meaning the **final rate for non-cooperative Chinese producers is about 18.7% higher than the preliminary rate**. The preliminary record shows three responding Chinese producers — Wuhan Wuyao Pharmaceutical, **Sinobright Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd** and Brilliant Pharmaceutical Limited (none claimed market-economy treatment, so normal value was computed on the Indian price basis). Importer/user questionnaires were sent to 14 Indian parties including **Anuh Pharma, Aquatic Remedies, Macleods Pharmaceuticals**, Cadila Pharmaceuticals, Strides Pharma and Imex Overseas — **none of which responded** — Source: [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-ethambutol-hydrochloride)

**【2026-09-18】Biopharma SHAKTI cleared by Expenditure Finance Committee, awaits Cabinet nod; new bulk-drug support scheme also due within three months** — Pharmaceutical Secretary Manoj Joshi said the Biopharma SHAKTI scheme was approved by the Finance Ministry's Expenditure Finance Committee on Wednesday (9/16) and is **expected to receive Union Cabinet approval within three months**. Joshi also called for **parallel execution** of regulatory processes to cut approval timelines — Source: [Informist Media](https://www.informistmedia.com/EquityWire/60038/Biopharma-SHAKTI-gets-expenditure-panel-nod-awaits-Cabinet-approval)

**【2026-09-17】(Update) Health Ministry proposes extending the BA/BE prior-intimation regime to export-bound drugs; mandatory CCTV for medical stores** — A draft notification proposes replacing the licensing requirement with a **prior-intimation mechanism for bioavailability/bioequivalence (BA/BE) studies of drugs intended for export**. According to official records, CDSCO as Central Licensing Authority processes about **4,000–4,500 BA/BE study applications each year**, and the revised mechanism is expected to significantly reduce procedural delays. In a separate notification, the Ministry has proposed making **CCTV cameras mandatory at all medical stores** to strengthen oversight of the sale and distribution of medicines, saying the mechanism would "enhance transparency and accountability across the pharmaceutical supply chain". Both amendments have been issued as drafts and are open for stakeholder comments for **30 days** from publication — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/govt-mulls-expanding-ba/be-intimation-regime-cctv-mandate-for-chemist-shops/134304022)

**【2026-09-17】Supreme Court upholds Himachal HC ruling: CDSCO drugs inspectors lack independent enforcement powers under Chapter IV** — The Supreme Court on Tuesday (9/15) dismissed the Union government's challenge and upheld the Himachal Pradesh High Court's 7 May judgment, holding that **a drugs inspector of the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) cannot independently exercise enforcement powers — inspection, sampling and prosecution — under Chapter IV of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act**, as those powers rest with state authorities. The dispute arose after a CDSCO inspector collected samples of drugs manufactured by Salus Pharmaceuticals at a hospital in Jalandhar Cantt. Counsel for the petitioner noted that **CDSCO has in recent years increasingly undertaken inspections and enforcement against drug manufacturers, and the ruling could put the statutory basis of such central enforcement action under closer judicial scrutiny** — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/supreme-court-upholds-himachal-hc-ruling-on-drug-regulator-powers-impacting-cdsco-enforcement/134297434)

**【2026-09-17】Health Ministry proposes boxed warning on emergency contraceptive levonorgestrel** — A gazette notification proposes a **boxed warning** on the over-the-counter emergency contraceptive **levonorgestrel**, specifying that it should be taken **only in emergency situations and not as a regular method of contraception**, that it is not an abortion pill, and that it offers no protection against HIV or other sexually transmitted infections, with frequent use potentially causing side effects. The move follows a recommendation by the **Drugs Technical Advisory Board (DTAB) at its 93rd meeting**, which approved a proposal from a high-level expert committee and the Drugs Consultative Committee (DCC) — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/centre-proposes-boxed-warning-on-contraceptive-pill-levonorgestrel/134304611)

**【2026-09-17】US ANDA filings up 16.3% year-on-year; Indian drugmakers account for roughly half of new filings** — According to a Nomura report, global pharma companies filed **457 ANDAs** in the nine months from October 2025 to June 2026, **up 16.3%** from 393 in the same period a year earlier, with **Indian drugmakers estimated to account for roughly half** of the fresh filings. Filings had fallen to a 20-year low in FY25 (year ended September 2025) before recovering in FY26. Growth is driven mainly by **complex generics** — inhalers, peptide injectables, long-acting injectables and liposomal oncology therapies — where higher technical and regulatory barriers limit credible competitors and pricing remains more resilient. FDA approved 689 ANDAs in FY2025 while 600 were submitted, of which 100 were classified as complex products; Nomura estimates complex products made up about 15–20% of recent filings. The report flags the proposed **100–200% US tariffs on imported generics from 2028** as an overhanging risk — Source: [The Indian Express](https://www.usfinancetimes.com/us-generic-drug-filings-rise-despite-looming-tariff-threat.html)

## 4. Risk Alerts

- **Ethambutol final findings land with a steep rate for non-cooperative producers** — The rate for "any other Chinese producer" rose from US$6,096/MT in the preliminary findings to **US$7,234/MT** in the final findings, about 10% above the Thai rate (US$6,584/MT), materially squeezing non-cooperative and transhipment routes. With Lupin as India's sole domestic producer, Indian downstream procurement is likely to shift further toward domestic supply. Note that **Anuh Pharma, Aquatic Remedies and Macleods — all existing customers in our coverage — appear on the importer/user list in this case**, so cost pass-through and supplier-switching pressure from them may increase.
- **CDSCO enforcement jurisdiction curtailed by the courts; regulatory intensity may shift to the states** — Following the Supreme Court ruling, CDSCO's statutory basis for independent inspection, sampling and prosecution under Chapter IV is weakened, while states such as Karnataka and Maharashtra have recently been highly active (fake-drug crackdowns, licence suspensions). **Compliance requirements are becoming more fragmented and inter-state practice less uniform**, raising the bar on supplier audits and documentation consistency.
- **Currency and cost pressure persist** — The rupee stands at about ₹95.98 per USD (having touched 96.06 intraday), with Brent crude around US$103–104/bbl. Sentiment towards Indian pharma equities is positive (Nifty Pharma up 0.7% week-on-week), but rising input and logistics costs continue to drive **price pressure, payment-term extension and FX-hedging requests** from customers.
- **Import dependence on raw materials is being policy-ised** — With Biopharma SHAKTI and the new bulk-drug support scheme nearing approval, and senior executives at Biocon and Mankind Pharma publicly committing to reduce dependence on imported raw materials, India's domestic API substitution and capex are likely to accelerate, bringing forward head-on competition with China in **fermentation-based and commodity API** segments.
