## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-30】DGTR fixes product scope in China Amoxycillin Trihydrate anti-dumping case, questionnaire deadline extended to 16 November** — The Authority issued its PUC/PCN methodology: the scope stays as notified on 24 September with no change, and no PCN methodology is adopted at this stage; as no interested party filed comments, all Amoxycillin Trihydrate remains within scope. The questionnaire response deadline was extended from 1 November to **16 November 2026**, with no further extension allowed; the applicant is Apitoria Pharma (an Aurobindo subsidiary). The Glycine case deadline was similarly extended to 15 November. — Source: [DGTR case page](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-amoxycillin-trihydrate-originating)【Source: File No. 6/59/2026-DGTR, SETU AD/OI/060/2026】

**【2026-10-29】Gujarat approves dedicated incentive scheme for Jambusar bulk drug park, 2,015 acres for APIs and intermediates** — The park in Bharuch district is being built under the central bulk drug park programme; incentives include a 10% capital subsidy on eligible fixed capital investment, a special interest subsidy for MSMEs, full reimbursement of utility charges for up to 10 years, and full waiver of stamp duty and registration fees on first land allotment, along with a CETP, steam network, solvent recovery and TSDF. Officials said the aim is to cut import dependence for APIs, KSMs and intermediates. — Source: [PTI (Rediff)](https://money.rediff.com/news/market/gujarat-bulk-drug-park-scheme-boosts-pharma-self-reliance/57136920261029)【Source: PTI report 2026-10-29, state government release】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-27】PIRAMAL PHARMA Q2 revenue up 28%, EBITDA up 72%; FY27 guidance raised to 19–20%** — Revenue ₹2,617 crore (about US$276 mn), with CDMO at ₹1,424 crore, up 36%, and Complex Hospital Generics at ₹849 crore, up 32%; EBITDA ₹385 crore at a 14.7% margin and net profit of ₹5 crore, back in the black. **【2026-10-30】HIKAL's Bengaluru API plant wins Swissmedic GMP certificate** — The certificate was issued under the EU–Switzerland mutual recognition agreement, following audits by Japan's PMDA and Brazil's ANVISA, giving the site a third stringent-market qualification; the company said it opens European customer orders and diversifies its manufacturing footprint amid global trade shifts. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/hikal-bags-swissmedic-gmp-approval-for-bengaluru-api-facility-22279)【Source: company announcement 2026-10-30】

**【2026-10-30】ALIVUS (formerly Glenmark Life Sciences) Q2 net profit up 51.3%, EBITDA margin at record 41.8%** — Revenue ₹682 crore (about US$71.8 mn), up 16%, with non-GPL business up 20.8%; EBITDA ₹285.1 crore, up 47%, lifting the margin by 880 bps; net profit ₹196.8 crore and operating cash flow ₹270.2 crore.
