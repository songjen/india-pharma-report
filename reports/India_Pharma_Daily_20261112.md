## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-11】DGTR retains original PUC in DCC sunset review; questionnaire deadline extended to 29 Nov** — In the sunset review concerning N,N′-Dicyclohexyl Carbodiimide from China PR, no interested party commented on the product under consideration or the proposed PCNs, and the domestic industry proposed no PCN methodology, so the PUC stays as defined in the original investigation; the deadline for questionnaire responses, including the exporter questionnaire, moves from 14 Nov to **29 Nov**, with no further extension to be granted. DCC is a standard coupling agent for peptide coupling and steroid esterification. — Source: [DGTR notice](https://dgtr.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-11/Notification_DCC_PUC_PCN_EQR_Extension%20(1).pdf)【Source: DGTR F.No. 7/26/2026-DGTR, 2026-11-11; SETU AD/SSR/013/2026】

**【2026-11-11】DGTR keeps PUC/PCN unchanged in sodium cyanide mid-term review; questionnaire deadline extended to 29 Nov** — The review covers China PR and the European Union and was initiated on 30 Sep; with no comments received on product scope or the PCN methodology, the initiation-notification basis applies, and the questionnaire deadline moves from 14 Nov to 29 Nov, with no further extension. Sodium cyanide is a base feedstock for cyanide downstreams such as cyanoacetic acid. — Source: [DGTR notice](https://dgtr.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-11/Notification_Sodium_Cyanide_PUC_PCN_EQR_Extension%20(1).pdf)【Source: DGTR F.No. 7/30/2026-DGTR, 2026-11-11; AD/MTR/006/2026】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-11】US FDA requires NAION warning on all GLP-1 receptor agonist labels** — A 09 Nov drug safety communication reports a pooled analysis of 126 randomised controlled trials covering more than 134,000 patients: 6.4 cases of non-arteritic anterior ischaemic optic neuropathy per 100,000 person-years in treated patients versus 3.8 in controls; 12 approved products must add the risk to the Warnings and Precautions section. Novo Nordisk said existing studies have not established causality. — Source: [Guangming Online](https://m.gmw.cn/2026-11/10/content_1304584764.htm)

**【2026-11-11】India turns self-sufficient in penicillin G: Aurobindo capacity 15,000 t, output about 10,000 t, above domestic demand** — On its Q2 call the company said the penicillin G plant has 15,000 t of capacity and currently produces about 10,000 t against Indian demand of roughly 9,000 t; prices have already risen, the plant is EBITDA-positive at a low level, and further ramp-up depends on regulatory approvals over the next six months; 6-APA and amoxicillin capacity is being expanded in parallel. — Source: [Multibagg (call transcript)](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/aurobindo-pharma-q2-record-revenue-cmwbt8tfn001f2mnps3qgfpyg)【Source: company Q2 FY27 earnings call, 2026-11-05】

**【2026-11-11】India's dependence on Chinese APIs at 73.7%; related imports USD 4.35 bn in 2024-25** — Department of Pharmaceuticals data show China accounted for 73.7% by value of the USD 4.35 bn of API, bulk drug and intermediate imports in 2024-25; the first bulk-drug PLI has commissioned 39 projects with cumulative sales of ₹3,792 crore (about USD 404 mn), but only ₹87.7 crore (about USD 9.33 mn) of incentives had been disbursed by March. — Source: [Policy Circle](https://www.policycircle.org/industry/bulk-drug-manufacturing-scheme/)【Source: Department of Pharmaceuticals data disclosed to Parliament, 2026】

**【2026-11-11】ALIVUS (formerly GLENMARK LIFE SCIENCES) signs shareholders' agreement for 76% of IQGen-X** — Per the exchange disclosure, Alivus appoints two of three directors while the founders retain one seat and 24%, sellable to Alivus at an agreed benchmark price after five years; the target handles formulation development for oral solids, sterile injectables and ophthalmics, with FY26 revenue of ₹3.48 crore (about USD 0.37 mn) and consideration of ₹9.12 crore (about USD 0.97 mn). — Source: [Multibagg (filing analysis)](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/alivus-iqgen-x-board-option-announcement-cmwc16zyi00812rpbd7e97flt)【Source: company exchange disclosure, 2026-11-11】
