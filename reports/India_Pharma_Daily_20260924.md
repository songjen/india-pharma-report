**【2026-09-23】US Commerce defines zero-tariff specialty pharmaceuticals and eligible jurisdictions, with India on the list** — Under Proclamation 11020, orphan drugs, nuclear medicines, plasma-derived therapies, fertility drugs, cell and gene therapies, antibody-drug conjugates, CBRN medical countermeasures and animal health products, together with associated ingredients, can receive a zero tariff rate if they are products of an eligible jurisdiction (**India is listed**) or are found to meet an urgent US health need; submissions open from 23 September, the relevant HTSUS provisions take effect on 29 September, when a 100% tariff begins for most other companies; **generic pharmaceutical articles and associated ingredients currently fall outside the Section 232 tariffs**. — Source: [PolicyRisk (Federal Register summary)](https://policyrisk.com/federal-register/2026-19498)【Source: US BIS Notice, Docket No. 260918-0006, published 2026-09-23】
