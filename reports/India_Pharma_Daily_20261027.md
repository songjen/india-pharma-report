## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-23】NPPA issues implementation FAQs on the trade margin cap for non-scheduled formulations (update)** — Clarifying the 30% trade margin rationalisation (TMR) ceiling set by S.O. 5690(E) of 17 October, NPPA states that Form V and Annexure-I must be filed through the IPDMS portal within seven days of the order irrespective of any change in MRP; a newspaper advertisement showing pre-revised and revised MRP is mandatory for every product whose price is reduced; the TMR applies to all strengths, dosage forms and routes of administration containing any Table B drug; revised prices take effect from 9 November 2026, and manufacturers with no MRP change may avail the annual increase under Paragraph 20(1) of DPCO 2013 only after 12 months from the last revision. — Source: [NPPA](https://nppa.gov.in/storage/uploads/tender/frequently-asked-questions-faqs-on-tmr-23-10-2026.pdf)【Source: S.O. 5690(E) 2026-10-17; NPPA FAQs 2026-10-23】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-26】Chinese raw-material supplies dry up, 300 Madhya Pradesh drug units cut to a single shift as input prices rise 30–50%** — The Madhya Pradesh pharmaceutical association and the state unit of IDMA report that supplies from China of diclofenac, amoxicillin, azithromycin, para-aminophenol, ampicillin, penicillin and several solvents have "completely stopped"; combined with higher gas prices amid the Middle East conflict, 300 plants in the Pithampur cluster have moved from three shifts to one. Input prices are up 30–50% and material is unavailable even at higher prices; a 20–25% price relaxation has already been granted under DPCO, while irregular LPG supply is hampering ampoule sealing for injectables. — Source: [Bhaskar English](https://www.bhaskarenglish.in/amp/local/mp/indore/news/madhya-pradesh-pharma-industry-drug-price-hike-middle-east-war-impact-137592229.html)

**【2026-10-26】Cipla Q2 FY27 preview: net profit seen down 28%, US sales down 22–27% on higher raw-material costs** — A nine-brokerage average compiled by Informist puts net profit at ₹980 crore (down 28% YoY) and revenue at ₹7,681 crore (up 1%); US sales are estimated at US$170–181 million, down 22–27%, while India is seen growing 13–15%. Brokerages attribute the pressure to the absence of generic Revlimid and lanreotide, a high base, higher raw-material costs and biosimilar investment. The board meets on 27 October to approve the results. — Source: [EconoTimes](https://econotimes.com/Cipla-Profit-Seen-Falling-28-as-U-S-Sales-Weigh-on-September-Quarter-1763626)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-26】BIOCON signs Janssen licence adding commercial rights for Yesintek (ustekinumab biosimilar) in nine markets** — Under a licence agreement with Janssen Sciences Ireland, Biocon can commercialise Yesintek with immediate effect in Australia, Brazil, China, Hong Kong (China), Israel, Mexico, New Zealand, South Korea and Kazakhstan; US entry dates and those for Europe, the UK, Canada and Japan were secured earlier. Reference product Stelara recorded worldwide sales of US$6.08 billion in 2025. — Source: [Biocon](https://www.biocon.com/biocon-secures-market-entry-for-yesintek-a-proposed-biosimilar-to-stelara-in-additional-global-markets)【Source: company stock-exchange disclosure 2026-10-26】
