## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-07】Minutes of 70th DCC meeting released: red-box warning mandatory for cough syrups, public COA platform rejected** — The minutes of the 70th Drugs Consultative Committee (DCC) meeting disclose several decisions: under Rule 96 of the Drugs Rules, a red boxed warning reading "shall not be used in children below 4 years of age" is mandated on cough syrup labels and cartons, with images of babies and toys prohibited; a proposal to make public upload of manufacturers' batch test reports/Certificates of Analysis (COA) on a digital platform was rejected, with the Committee favouring risk-based inspections, independent sampling and enforcement instead; a recommendation was made to bring narcotic and psychotropic preparations under national digital tracking, targeted for completion by March 2027; licence retention moves online. — Source: [The Health Master](https://thehealthmaster.com/2026/10/07/70th-dcc-meeting-major-changes-in-pharma-policy-in-india/)·[Ingredients South Asia](http://www.ingredientssouthasia.com/News/dcc-rejects-proposal-for-creation-of-digital-platform-to-upload-quality-control--test-reports-of-pharma-cos)【Source: Minutes of 70th DCC meeting, 2026-09-18】

**【2026-10-09】Health Ministry proposes prior approval for prescription drug advertising, 30-day consultation** — The Union Health Ministry issued a draft amendment on drug advertising that would require prior approval for advertisements of prescription and restricted medicines, and would extend coverage to e-pharmacy platforms, influencer promotions, AI-generated content and paid reviews. Advertising of prescription drugs to the public is already prohibited, but online channels have left enforcement gaps. A 30-day consultation window has been opened. — Source: [ET Government](https://government.economictimes.indiatimes.com/amp/news/digital-india/from-e-pharmacies-to-influencers-india-moves-to-regulate-prescription-drug-advertising/134846235)【Source: Health Ministry draft amendment, 2026-10】

**【2026-10-08】Uttar Pradesh finds hospital pharmacy mark-ups of up to 67x, asks NPPA to review trade margins** — The UP Food Safety and Drug Administration inspected 214 hospital pharmacies attached to medical colleges and private hospitals and analysed 130 medicines; in 36 cases the printed MRP exceeded the purchase price by more than ten times, with the widest gap nearly 67-fold (polymyxin-B injection procured at ₹74.09 against an MRP of ₹4,980.94). Hospital pharmacies have been directed to display procurement and sale prices from 1 November, and the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority has been asked to review trade margins. — Source: [India Pharma Outlook](https://www.indiapharmaoutlook.com/news/this-week-on-the-pharma-regulatory-front-four-key-shifts-nwid-5943.html)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-08】India's pharma market grows 13.5% y-o-y in September, a three-year high; oncology up 21.6%** — According to pharma retail monitor Pharmarack, the Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM) grew 13.5% in value terms in September, the strongest in three years, with volume contributing 3.4%, price 6.2% and new products 3.8%. Cardiac therapies rose 17.7%, anti-diabetics 16.1% and anti-neoplastics 21.6%, while respiratory volumes fell 6.9% on a weak monsoon. Brokerages maintain a positive view on domestic leaders on the strength of specialty portfolios. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/news/eplane-noida-international-airport-evtol-vertiport-mou-e200x-126100800841_1.html)

**【2026-10-10】Maharashtra FDA extends drug enforcement to API manufacturers across the supply chain** — State FDA Commissioner Tukaram Munde said enforcement has been widened from retail outlets to the entire supply chain, including pharmacies, formulation plants, active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) manufacturers, blood products, devices and cosmetics, and that inspections of API makers had begun, revealing severely rusted manufacturing equipment in some cases. More than 2,000 drug manufacturing units in the state are under intensified surveillance; around 15,000–16,000 establishments have been inspected, nearly half with major compliance deficiencies, with about 600 suspended and 5,200 issued improvement notices. — Source: [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/amp/india/story/tukaram-munde-defends-shah-rukh-khan-ajay-devgn-tiger-shroff-notices-more-action-likely-3014139-2026-10-10)

**【2026-10-11】WHO issues first guidelines on child and adolescent obesity: no drugs or surgery for ages 0–9** — The World Health Organization released its first guidelines on integrated management of obesity in children and adolescents: for ages 0–9 it strongly recommends against pharmacological treatment, bariatric surgery or weight-management devices, centring care on structured diet, physical activity and behavioural support; for ages 10–19, approved obesity medicines may be considered only after a supervised multimodal lifestyle programme has failed. The guidance arrives as GLP-1 demand in India accelerates and global and domestic players expand into weight management. — Source: [India Pharma Outlook](https://www.indiapharmaoutlook.com/news/this-week-on-the-pharma-regulatory-front-four-key-shifts-nwid-5943.html)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-05】ZYDUS LIFESCIENCES and MSN launch first generic riociguat (Adempas) in the U.S.** — Zydus announced, with MSN Laboratories, the U.S. launch of riociguat tablets in five strengths (0.5/1/1.5/2/2.5 mg), the first FDA-approved generic equivalent of Adempas and the first to market; the product was developed and is manufactured by MSN.
