## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-21】Health Ministry approves Biovigilance Programme of India, releases 7th edition of National Formulary** — Union Minister of State for Health & Family Welfare Anupriya Patel, at the 6th National Pharmacovigilance Week, announced: (1) approval of the **Biovigilance Programme of India (BvPI)**, led by the Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission (IPC), to monitor adverse events from medicines and biological products used in organ and tissue transplantation, covering both donors and recipients; (2) the **7th edition of the National Formulary of India** (653 drugs); (3) a guidance document on medical-device adverse-event reporting and the **ADR-PvPI 2.0 app**. About **1,150 ADR monitoring centres** are operational nationwide, with plans to extend the network to primary healthcare level; India has risen to **8th globally** in contributions to the WHO patient safety database. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2312930)【Source: PIB PRID 2312930 (2026-09-21)】

**【2026-09-21】CDSCO expert panel recommends waiving local Phase III and bioequivalence trials for lenacapavir** — Minutes of the Subject Expert Committee (SEC) under CDSCO show that local Phase III clinical trials and BA/BE studies were **waived** for generic **lenacapavir** (a twice-yearly long-acting HIV prevention injectable) filed by Dr. Reddy's and Emcure, subject to a post-marketing **Phase IV** study with efficacy as the primary endpoint; the waiver was **not granted for heavily treatment-experienced, multidrug-resistant patients**. Emcure has already filed its injectable version with the DCGI and hopes for approval within this fiscal year. — Source: [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/drugmakers-move-closer-to-launch-landmark-hiv-drug-as-cdsco-eases-approval-process-14034536.html)

**【2026-09-21】CDSCO proposes mandatory red warning on cough syrup labels: not for children under 4** — The proposal seeks to amend Rule 96 of the Drugs Rules, 1945, requiring a **red, prominently displayed warning reading "Cough syrup should not be used in children below 4 years of age"**, plus a pictorial depiction of school-age children. The move follows a DGHS advisory of October 2025 that failed to stop paediatric prescribing; a CDSCO internal technical committee in April 2026 recommended consulting ICMR and holding stakeholder consultations. The proposal is before the **Drugs Consultative Committee (DCC)**. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/cough-syrup-bottles-may-soon-carry-a-warning-label-in-red/134377864)

**【2026-09-21】(Update) DCC to weigh central licensing for all sterile drugs; CDSCO quality database to open to procurement agencies** — The proposal would bring all sterile formulations (injectables, ophthalmics) under the Central Licensing Approving Authority, citing around **240 injection samples found not of standard quality in 2025-26** and international alerts on **Burkholderia cepacia contamination (metoprolol)**. The DCGI separately disclosed that CDSCO's quality surveillance database adds **about 200 products each month** and will be made **accessible to procurement agencies** for tender evaluation. — Source: [India Pharma Outlook](https://www.indiapharmaoutlook.com/news/regulatory-watch-eu-nods-fda-clears-india-rewires-nwid-5839.html) · [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/policy/lite/india-to-roll-out-digital-drug-regulatory-system-soon-dcgi-21925)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-21】Cohance Lifesciences' Jaggaiahpet API plant in Andhra Pradesh ends USFDA inspection with 5 observations** — The inspection ran **14–19 September**; the company disclosed on 21 September that it received a **Form 483 with five observations**, has initiated CAPA and will respond within the stipulated timeline (details not disclosed). The site is a major contributor to Cohance's API revenue; it is the company's third facility inspected recently (five observations at Pashamylaram, Hyderabad, in August; four at the Shamshabad formulation plant, 27 Aug–4 Sep). — Source: [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/sensex-nifty50-today-stock-market-highlights-21st-september-2026/article71488721.ece)【Source: company disclosure 2026-09-21 (SEBI Reg.30)】

**【2026-09-21】Aragen steps up complex-modality investment, plans about 500 KL of new small-molecule CDMO capacity** — Hyderabad-based CRDMO **Aragen Life Sciences** plans to add roughly **500 kilolitres of small-molecule CDMO capacity** and invest in a biologics drug-product facility including fill-finish. Focus areas are **ADCs (including proprietary linker libraries), peptides, oligonucleotides and high-potent APIs**, starting with a clinical-scale peptide plant. The company serves over 500 customers including 16 of the top 20 global pharma companies, synthesises 60,000–70,000 novel molecules a year, has filed a DRHP with SEBI, and grew 18% from FY25 to FY26. Management cites global drugmakers seeking supply-chain alternatives to China. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/aragen-steps-up-investment-in-complex-drug-modalities-plans-500-kl-new-cdmo-capacity/134377673)

**【2026-09-21】Akums launches DCGI-approved triple-therapy Combikit for H. pylori** — The combination comprises **clarithromycin + vonoprazan + amoxicillin**; H. pylori infection is associated with chronic gastritis and peptic ulcer disease and is a recognised gastric-cancer risk factor. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/akums-announces-dcgi-approved-triple-therapy-for-h-pylori-infection/)

**【2026-09-21】FDA approves Amneal's lanreotide injection with Competitive Generic Therapy exclusivity; immediate launch** — The long-acting **lanreotide injection 120 mg/0.5 mL** is indicated for acromegaly, gastro-entero-pancreatic neuroendocrine tumours and carcinoid syndrome. **CGT designation** can confer 180 days of exclusivity; in-house manufacturing allowed launch on approval. — Source: [India Pharma Outlook](https://www.indiapharmaoutlook.com/news/regulatory-watch-eu-nods-fda-clears-india-rewires-nwid-5839.html)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-09-21】BIOCON: Pertuzumab biosimilar Pebrilzo receives positive EMA CHMP opinion** — Biocon Biologics announced that the EMA's Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) recommended granting marketing authorisation for its **Pertuzumab biosimilar (420 mg concentrate for solution for infusion, brand name Pebrilzo)** for **HER2-positive breast cancer** across multiple disease stages. CEO Tambe called it the **first monoclonal antibody biosimilar to receive a positive CHMP opinion under EMA's tailored clinical development approach**. The European Commission will take the final decision; the product is not authorised in the EU until then. — Source: [Biocon](https://www.biocon.com/biocons-pertuzumab-becomes-first-biosimilar-to-secure-ema-chmp-approval-recommendation-under-new-tailored-clinical-approach/) · [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/biocon-receives-ema-chmp-approval-for-pertuzumab-biosimilar/)【Source: company announcement 2026-09-21】
