## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-08-25】Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission (IPC) launches 7 nitrosamine impurity reference standards for the first time, strengthening detection of carcinogenic impurities in medicines** — At its first-ever interactive meeting at the Central Drugs Laboratory (CDL) / Central Cosmetics Laboratory (CCL), Kolkata (theme "IP 2026: Perspectives, Suggestions & Way Forward"), IPC launched seven Indian Pharmacopoeia Nitrosamine Impurities Reference Standards: NDMA, NDEA, NEIPA, NDBA, NMPA, NDIPA and NMBA. Nitrosamines are potent genotoxic and carcinogenic compounds that may arise unintentionally during manufacturing, storage, packaging or contamination; controlling them is critical for medicines used in hypertension, gastric disorders, diabetes and tuberculosis. The launch strengthens India's capability for accurate detection and quantification of nitrosamine impurities, supports method validation, quality assurance and regulatory compliance, and promotes harmonisation with international quality standards plus self-reliance in critical reference materials. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2303150)

**【2026-08-25】Commerce Minister Goyal leads India's largest-ever 200+ business delegation to Japan (Aug 24-27), focusing on India-Japan pharma investment and certification barriers** — The delegation spans manufacturing, semiconductors, clean energy, pharma, automotive and start-ups, covering Tokyo, Nagoya and Osaka.
