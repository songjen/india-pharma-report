### 【2026-08-10】CDSCO Circular: New Drugs Not Approved Anywhere Globally Must Undergo Full Review in India; Indian Trial Subjects Cannot Be a Shortcut (CDSCO/ET Pharma)
In a circular dated August 10, CDSCO clarified that applications to import and market new drugs **not approved by any regulator worldwide** — but with Phase III global clinical trial (GCT) data involving Indian subjects — require a **comprehensive evaluation of non-clinical and clinical data** under the New Drugs and Clinical Trials (NDCT) Rules, 2019, processed by CDSCO's IND Division. Participation of Indian subjects in global trials cannot be used as a shortcut to marketing approval in India. Earlier, India allowed generics to conduct BA/BE studies via a notification route without prior approval; CDSCO has also granted case-by-case waivers for capital-intensive Phase III trials and embraced non-animal methods (NAM). The circular sets a clear boundary for innovative drugs entering India and affects filing strategies of MNCs and innovation-focused partners.
