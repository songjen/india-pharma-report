## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-17】NPPA caps trade margin at 30% for 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs, effective 9 November** — The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority, invoking Paragraph 19 of the DPCO 2013, notified a 30% cap on the trade margin (equivalent to a 42.86% mark-up on price to stockist) for 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs, including Nivolumab, Olaparib, Denosumab, Pembrolizumab and Osimertinib; MRPs are expected to fall by up to 70%, yielding annual savings of about ₹2,500 crore (≈USD 263 mn). Manufacturers must file revised prices within seven days and publish them on their websites; retailers and hospitals must apply the new prices from 9 November and may not cut output without approval. — Source: [NPPA](https://nppa.gov.in/storage/uploads/tender/277271-17-10-2026.pdf)【Source: NPPA Gazette Notification S.O. 5690(E), 2026-10-17】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-17】German and Indian firms exchange NDA on immunoglobulins, eye technology transfer to India** — The Embassy of India in Berlin said Germany's Neovii Biotech and India's VINS Bioproducts exchanged a non-disclosure agreement on the sidelines of the Indo-German Health Leadership Dialogue on 13 October, covering manufacturing, technology transfer to India and commercialisation of immunoglobulin products, including anti-thymocyte globulin (ATG) used in organ transplantation. If realised, the tie-up would strengthen domestic capability in transplant and blood-disorder biologics and reduce import reliance. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/indian-german-firms-to-collaborate-on-immunoglobulin-products-indian-embassy/135006183)【Source: Embassy of India, Berlin announcement (Indo-German Health Dialogue 2026-10-13)】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-17】ALEMBIC wins USFDA approval for ciprofloxacin/dexamethasone otic suspension, a USD 110 mn market** — The company announced final FDA approval of its ANDA for Ciprofloxacin and Dexamethasone Otic Suspension USP 0.3%/0.1%, therapeutically equivalent to Sandoz's Ciprodex, indicated for acute otitis media in children with tympanostomy tubes and acute otitis externa in children and adults. IQVIA puts the US market for the product at about USD 110 mn for the twelve months to June 2026. Alembic now holds 246 cumulative ANDA approvals (226 final, 20 tentative). — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/regulatory/alembic-pharma-secures-usfda-approval-for-ear-infection-suspension-22178)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-16】

**【2026-10-17】ANUH PHARMA buys Vanquest's Tarapur plant for ₹72.9 crore, adding 525 tpa of API capacity** — Anuh Pharma signed an asset transfer agreement to acquire Vanquest Pharma's API and intermediate manufacturing facility at MIDC Tarapur, Maharashtra, including land, buildings and plant, for ₹72.9 crore (≈USD 7.7 mn) in cash. The deal is at arm's length, involves no related parties and no share issuance, and substitutes existing capacity for greenfield construction to shorten commissioning and compliance timelines. — Source: [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/healthcarebiotech/Anuh-Pharma-Acquires-Manufacturing-Unit-for-Rs-729-Crore-to-Boost-Capacity/6ad225d5b2eb76fd08c59010)【Source: Company BSE announcement 2026-10-17】
