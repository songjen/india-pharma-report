## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-13】PIB recaps PLI outcomes across 14 sectors: pharma PLI output above ₹4.02 lakh crore, bulk drug PLI sales only USD 403 million** — As of 30 June 2026, the Pharmaceuticals PLI recorded cumulative investment of ₹46,744 crore (about USD 4.97 billion), production/sales of ₹4,02,869 crore and incentive disbursal of ₹6,662 crore; the Bulk Drugs PLI logged investment of ₹5,211 crore, sales of ₹3,792 crore and incentives of just ₹88 crore against an outlay of ₹6,940 crore. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2335931)【Source: PIB PRID 2335931, 2026-11-13】

**【2026-11-13】NPPA orders states to police the 30% trade margin cap on 137 anti-cancer drugs, action reports due 16 November (update)** — An office memorandum directs State Drug Controllers to monitor compliance by manufacturers, dealers and hospitals and to display revised price lists; based on company filings up to 11 November, prices fell in 1,031 of 1,225 formulations, 600 brands dropped more than 50%, the steepest cut was 96.45%, and annual savings are estimated at ₹3,502 crore (about USD 372 million). — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/nppa-directs-monitoring-of-trade-margin-rationalisation-on-137-anti-cancer-drugs/)【Source: NPPA office memorandum, 2026-11-13】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-13】BLD Pharmatech's Indian unit receives Bangalore Customs show-cause notice on declared value and R&D service fees** — Customs proposes to mark up the declared value of certain historical imports and to include part of the R&D service fees charged by group entities in the assessable value, recovering differential duty and interest with possible penalties; the company disputes the findings and valuation method and has appointed advisers to respond; BLDIND revenue was RMB 115 million in 2025, or 8.65% of consolidated revenue. — Source: [CFI](https://www.cfi.net.cn/p20261113001486.html)【Source: company announcement 2026-054, 2026-11-13】

**【2026-11-13】BioSpectrum whitepaper: FDA issued 513 warning letters in 2026, 25 to India and 22 to China** — Covering 13 January to 10 November, 59 letters went to Asia-Pacific; of 121 drug manufacturing quality letters, 45 went to the region; drug quality letters were posted a median eight days after issuance, often before the company's reply was due, and import alerts frequently preceded the letter. — Source: [BioSpectrum Asia](https://www.biospectrumasia.com/regulatory/29/28676/fda-warning-letters-2026-lessons-for-india-china-and-asia-pacific-pharma.html)

**【2026-11-12】Top pharma firms pin growth on a handful of launches; DRL says semaglutide API scale-up is resolved, launch this month** — Cipla targets a USD 250 million quarterly US run-rate by Q4 FY27 (USD 172 million in Q2), relying on generic Ventolin and Advair plus two respiratory products and one peptide drug; Dr. Reddy's semaglutide is approved in 10 countries and, after API scale-up constraints, is now set for a November launch; Sun Pharma leans on Leqselvi and Unloxcyt. — Source: [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/healthcare-top-pharma-firms-bet-big-on-a-slew-of-new-launches-4383852)
