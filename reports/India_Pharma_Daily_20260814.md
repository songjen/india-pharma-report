### [2026-08-10] CDSCO: Globally Unapproved New Drugs Must Undergo Full Domestic Review in India (CDSCO)
On Aug 10, CDSCO issued a directive stating that any new drug not yet approved by a foreign regulator — even if Indian patients participated in international clinical trials — must undergo a **complete domestic review process** in India (submit Form CT-18 for commercial import application, reviewed under Rule 75, released via CT-19/CT-20 upon approval). Participation in global Phase III trials alone does not constitute a shortcut to marketing approval. This tightens the entry threshold for new drugs into India and may lengthen global pharma companies' launch timelines in India.
