## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-15】CDSCO waives separate export NOC for approved r-DNA drugs exported under export-only brand names** — In a notice to State Drug Controllers dated 12 October, CDSCO clarified that a firm holding a valid Form CT-23 marketing authorisation for an r-DNA drug and seeking Form 28-D export product permission does not need a separate CDSCO Export No Objection Certificate, provided approved particulars — composition, specifications, dosage form, presentation, manufacturing site and process — remain unchanged and the export-only brand name is the sole difference. Eligible applicants may file directly with the concerned State Licensing Authority via the online licensing system; all other r-DNA cases still require a quantity-specific Export NOC. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/cdsco-waives-export-noc-for-approved-r-dna-drugs-sold-under-new-brand-names-126101501306_1.html)【Source: CDSCO notice dated 2026-10-12, published on the CDSCO website 2026-10-14】

**【2026-10-13】Pharmexcil Chairman: MIP curbs yet to deliver, extension crucial for inventory and import dependence** — Pharmexcil Chairman Namit Joshi said India's effort to shield domestic manufacturers from low-cost Chinese imports through Minimum Import Price curbs has not yet produced the desired gains; an extension would give companies room to clear inventory overhang and reduce import dependence for critical raw materials. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/videos/mip-curbs-yet-to-deliver-desired-gains-extension-crucial-pharmexcil-chairman/134913088)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-15】Novo Nordisk India cuts Norditropin FlexPro prices by 33%–35%** — Novo Nordisk India reduced prices for its growth hormone product Norditropin FlexPro in the Indian market: the 10 mg device falls from ₹16,836 to ₹11,300 (down 33%) and the 15 mg device from ₹25,254 to ₹16,450 (down 35%), a move the company said is aimed at improving affordability for children with growth disorders. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/novo-slashes-growth-hormone-drug-market-price-by-35-in-india/134963316) · [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/novo-india-slashes-norditropin-flexpro-cost-by-35-for-growth-hormone-therapy-22164)

**【2026-10-09】FDA India Office: 207 Indian sites inspected in FY25, 17.9% classified OAI, in line with global average** — Gregory Smith, Country Director of the US FDA's India Office, told an IPA virtual GMP workshop that of India's 610 FDA-approved plants, 207 were inspected in FY25 and 37 (17.9%) received Official Action Indicated (OAI) classifications, broadly in line with the 18% global average, while 22% were No Action Indicated and 60% Voluntary Action Indicated. The most frequent deficiencies were inadequate written records (20%), insufficient investigations (17%) and inadequate laboratory controls (12%); the FDA also flagged data integrity concerns at CROs including Raptim Research and Panexcell. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulatory-update/fda-watch-india-mirrors-global-oai-trends-data-integrity-keeps-cros-under-lens/134834881)【Source: US FDA India Office FY25 inspection data (IPA workshop) 2026-10-09】
