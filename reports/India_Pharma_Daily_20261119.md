## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**[2026-11-16] DGTR extends questionnaire deadline in the China-origin Amoxycillin Trihydrate anti-dumping case to 26 Nov, rules out further extension** — The notice moves the response deadline from 16 Nov to 26 Nov and states that trade remedy investigations are time-bound, so no further extension will be granted. The investigation was initiated on 24 Sep 2026 against Chinese-origin material; interested parties must file confidential and non-confidential versions through the SETU portal. — Source: [DGTR](https://dgtr.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-11/Amoxy%20Exten.pdf)【Basis: DGTR notice dated 2026-11-16, File No. 6/59/2026-DGTR, SETU Case ID AD/OI/060/2026】

**[2026-11-18] Health Ministry launches World AMR Awareness Week 2026, targeting both prescribing and dispensing** — Participants cited data showing more than half of antimicrobial sales in India occur without a prescription and nearly half of outpatient antibiotic prescriptions are inappropriate. Measures include warning displays at pharmacies, curbing substandard and spurious medicines, restricting antimicrobial use in food-producing animals, under NAP AMR 2.0 (2025–2029). — Source: [pib.gov.in](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2338081)【Basis: PIB PRID 2338081, 2026-11-18】

**[2026-11-19] Supreme Court reserves verdict on TMR; arguments point to hospital markups, not retail chemists, as the core distortion** — In Kishan Chand Jain v EMRB, trade bodies argued that high MRPs originate in private hospital supply chains. Hospitals' cross-subsidisation plea citing emergency care and CGHS, ESIS and Ayushman Bharat was rejected; the court held that viability should come from clinical services rather than markups on medicines and consumables, and noted that inflated printed prices burden retail patients. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191992&sid=2)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**[2026-11-19] Sun Pharma supply-chain director: FY2024-25 pharma exports USD 30.5 bn, API and intermediate imports USD 4.35 bn, China 73.7%** — Using the example of four months of usable cover against a three-month replenishment lead time from an alternative source, he argued the effective response window is only one month. Under the bulk-drug PLI scheme, 39 projects covering 28 APIs and key starting materials had been commissioned by June 2026. — Source: [pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191988&sid=2)

**[2026-11-17] PLI 2.0 debate intensifies: only 20 of 55 selected applicants are MSMEs** — The scheme carries an outlay of ₹15,000 crore (about USD 1.61 bn). Cumulative investment reached ₹40,294 crore (about USD 4.31 bn, against a target of ₹17,275 crore) and cumulative sales ₹3,08,409 crore (about USD 33.0 bn), including exports of ₹1,98,509 crore, with employment of about 97,000 as of September 2025. Industry bodies seek credit guarantees, interest subvention and shared testing facilities. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/pli-2-0-can-msmes-catch-up/)
