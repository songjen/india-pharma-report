## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-18】Venus Remedies wins five marketing authorisations in four countries, including meropenem in Argentina** — The Indian critical-care injectables maker secured approvals for vancomycin and fluorouracil in Kazakhstan, fulvestrant in Peru, methotrexate in Kuwait and meropenem in Argentina, spanning the CIS, Latin America and the Middle East. Its products reach more than 100 countries, and the company said it will keep expanding exports through registrations and differentiated products. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/venus-remedies-receives-five-marketing-approvals-in-kazakhstan-peru-kuwait-argentina-22181)【Source: company announcement 2026-10-18】

**【2026-10-15】India's API market projected at USD 22.18 billion by 2031, with import substitution as the main theme** — Brokerage research puts India's API market at USD 22.18 billion by 2031. Laurus reported FY26 API revenue of ₹2,746 crore (up 13% YoY) and small-molecule CDMO revenue of ₹1,896 crore (up 38%), with 92 cumulative DMFs and FY26 capex of ₹1,070 crore; Divi's holds 30 commercial generic APIs. — Source: [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/market/stock-insights/indias-api-market-could-hit-22-18-billion-by-2031-3-pharma-stocks-to-watch/4363281)

**【2026-10-17】AstraZeneca opens new Mumbai corporate office, with about 80 clinical trials running in India** — The 25,600 sq ft office was inaugurated by Maharashtra's Chief Minister. The company has introduced more than 25 new medicines and indications in India in three years, runs around 80 global clinical trials in the country and employs nearly 5,000 people there, focused on oncology, biopharmaceuticals and rare diseases. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/astrazeneca-opens-new-mumbai-corporate-office-22176)【Source: company announcement 2026-10-17】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-18】LUPIN receives CDSCO approval for nivolumab biosimilar, covering both finished formulation and bulk drug** — Lupin may manufacture and market a biosimilar of BMS's Opdivo across all 12 originator indications, with a Phase IV study required. The approval covers both the finished formulation and the bulk drug, manufactured in partnership with Hyderabad-based CDMO Virchow Biotech. Zydus (Tishtha, January) and Dr. Reddy's (Nivorz, September) launched earlier; India's immuno-oncology market is estimated at around ₹3,900 crore. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/drug-approvals-and-launches/lupin-gets-cdsco-nod-for-nivolumab-biosimilar/135025868)【Source: CDSCO approval, reported 2026-10-18】
