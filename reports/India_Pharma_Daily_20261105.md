**【2026-11-04】Health Ministry drafts two Schedule M amendments: sterile contamination limits aligned with WHO, batch release assigned to authorised person** — Microbial limits are proposed at no growth for Grade A, ≤10 CFU/m³ for Grade B, ≤100 for Grade C and ≤200 for Grade D, following a Drugs Consultative Committee review and consensus at the 93rd DTAB meeting; batch release must be assigned to a qualified and experienced person, with batch records under Schedule U retaining equipment, environmental monitoring, IPQC, CoA and stability data. Both drafts are open for comment for 30 days. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/centre-moves-to-tighten-sterile-drugs-contamination-norms-in-line-with-who-standards/135533519)【Source: Health Ministry gazette drafts 2026-11-04, 30-day comment period】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-04】WOCKHARDT seeks 12 to 14 years of data protection for India-developed drugs** — Chairman Habil Khorakiwala said the absence of data exclusivity and slow approvals leave domestic innovators with three to four years less effective patent life than in the US and Europe, citing an IQVIA study that innovative drugs reach India about 3.95 years late; he urged CDSCO to link with the patent office and verify India-granted patents at approval. First public support for data protection from a large Indian drugmaker, limited to domestically developed innovations. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/wockhardt-seeks-14-year-data-shield-for-india-made-drugs/135517340)
