## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-01】Commerce Ministry holds India-US trade consultation with industry; exporters urge early conclusion of BTA to cut tariff uncertainty** — India's Commerce Ministry on September 1 chaired a consultation with 11 export sectors including pharmaceuticals, engineering, textiles and chemicals, along with industry chambers CII, FICCI, Assocham and FIEO, chaired by Additional Secretary Darpan Jain, who is also India's chief negotiator for the India-US bilateral trade agreement (BTA). Exporters reported that US order volumes have declined, buyers are shortening order cycles and diversifying sourcing, and urged early conclusion of the BTA to remove tariff uncertainty. The meeting sets the stage for Commerce Minister Goyal's late-September US visit for the G20 Trade Ministerial in Milwaukee (starting Sep 30), bilateral talks with USTR Jamieson Greer, and a business delegation to Chicago in October. Data: in FY2025-26 India's exports to the US were $87.31B (marginal growth), imports $53.45B (+17.16%), trade surplus $33.9B (narrowed from $40.9B the prior year); in Apr-Jul 2026-27 exports rose 3% to $34.5B. The US has imposed an additional 10% tariff on India and other countries since July 24. ## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-01】Sun Pharma signs White House MFN pricing deal: over 2-year reprieve from Section 232 tariffs on innovative drugs, commits MFN pricing for Medicaid** — India's largest drugmaker Sun Pharma on September 1 joined eight other pharma companies at the White House to sign pricing agreements: extending Most Favoured Nation (MFN) pricing to all US state Medicaid programmes and to future innovative medicine launches, in exchange for a delay of Section 232 tariffs on its innovative pharmaceutical products for more than two years. Sun also committed to donate 71.4 tonnes of clindamycin and 6.75 tonnes of doxycycline to the US Strategic Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients Reserve (SAPIR). The White House said the nine companies have committed to invest at least $19.6B collectively in US manufacturing. The US is Sun's largest innovative-medicine market, accounting for ~27% of global revenue; Sun is advancing its $11.75B acquisition of Organon. Other terms remain confidential; the stock closed ~2% lower on Sep 1 (Nifty Pharma -1.4% that day). With the US having announced 100% tariffs on generics from Aug 2028 and 200% from Aug 2029, the MFN route is seen as a new template for Indian drugmakers to mitigate tariff risk. Source: [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/industry/pharma/story/how-sun-pharma-is-avoiding-a-tariff-hit-by-agreeing-to-mfn-pricing-for-us-medicaid-552560-2026-09-01), [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/sun-pharma-share-price-white-house-pricing-deal-investment-expansion-tariff-reprieve-drugs-organon-19980933.htm), [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/industry-sun-pharma-secures-over-two-year-relief-from-us-pharma-tariffs-pact-signed-with-us-govt-to-provide-affordable-drugs-4329881)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-08-31】Client Aurobindo: US arm Lannett launches generic Advair Diskus — company's first commercialized inhalation product in the US** — Aurobindo's US subsidiary Lannett Company has launched a generic version of GSK's Advair Diskus (fluticasone propionate/salmeterol inhalation powder, 100/50 and 250/50 mcg) for asthma and COPD, marking the first product from Aurobindo's inhalation pipeline to be commercialized in the US. The US market for this category is approximately $908M (IQVIA). Cipla has also received FDA approval for the same generic (all three strengths), setting the stage for competition in the US respiratory segment. Source: [Economic Times Pharma](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/pharmaceuticals/aurobindo-pharma-launches-generic-advair-diskus-for-asthma-and-copd-in-us/printarticle/133653548.cms)

## 4. Risk Alerts

1. **US tariff roadmap looming**: The US has announced 100% tariffs on generics from Aug 2028 and 200% from Aug 2029; the US accounted for ~30.89% ($2.5B) of India's pharma exports in Q1 FY27. Sun Pharma has hedged innovative-drug tariffs via the MFN deal, but clients such as Aurobindo and Lupin have not yet followed, leaving medium-term tariff exposure on generic businesses.
2. **India-US BTA first tranche still pending**: Exporters report shorter/smaller US orders; the 10% additional tariff (effective Jul 24) plus BTA uncertainty may persist until the Sep 30 G20 Trade Ministerial window.
3. **Elevated Indian pharma valuations**: Nifty Pharma fell 1.4% on Sep 1 (Sun -2.8% leading), with the sector at ~43x PE vs historical average of 37-38x; brokers flag earnings-delivery pressure.
4. **Structural API dependence on China unchanged**: India still relies heavily on Chinese KSMs/APIs (e.g., ~98% import dependence for erythromycin); China suppliers should monitor competitive capacity from PLI/PRIP-driven domestic substitution.
