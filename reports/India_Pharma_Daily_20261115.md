## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-14】PIB: exports up year-on-year for seven straight months; RoDTEP and RoSCTL remission extended to 31 Dec** — Current export instruments: 26,283 Advance Authorisations, 3,579 DFIAs and 21,838 EPCGs issued this fiscal; RoDTEP and RoSCTL both extended to 2026-12-31, with RoDTEP refunds totalling ₹77,262.60 crore from April 2021 to December 2025; the Export Promotion Mission carries an outlay of ₹25,060 crore, including a ₹497 crore RELIEF component covering freight and war-risk premiums on West Asia routes. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2336328)【Source: PIB PRID 2336328, 2026-11-14】

**【2026-11-13】DGFT brings India-New Zealand FTA into the origin-rules framework; preferential CoO goes electronic** — Public Notice 40/2026-27 inserts serial xvii under Para 2.88(a) of HOP 2023 listing the India-NZ FTA that entered into force on 20 Oct, and adds the authorised agencies for preferential Certificates of Origin in Appendix 2B (DGFT and regional offices, Export Inspection Council, SEZs, all products); the accompanying trade notice provides for electronic issuance and QR verification via the Trade Connect platform. — Source: [DGFT notice](https://content.dgft.gov.in/Website/dgftprod/768cd243-7a87-4e5b-a78a-691d64512816/PN_0001.pdf)【Source: DGFT Public Notice 40/2026-27, 2026-11-13; Trade Notice 34/2026-27】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-14】Indian pharma shifts to high-value drugs and advanced manufacturing; September domestic market up 17.1%, strongest in three years** — IQVIA puts September domestic market growth at 17.1% year-on-year; Pharmarack data show GLP-1 therapies up 72% in value and 13% of the diabetes market; FY25 bulk drug and API imports were about USD 4.35 bn with China at 73.71%, and NITI Aayog assesses Chinese APIs at 35–40% cheaper than domestic output; FY25 pharma exports were USD 30.47 bn and April–June FY27 shipments USD 8.10 bn, up 6.8%; Bulk Drugs PLI has drawn ₹5,210.74 crore of investment with 39 projects commissioned; CPHI India runs 23–25 November in New Delhi. — Source: [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/industry/pharma/story/indias-pharma-industry-shifts-to-high-value-drugs-advanced-manufacturing-to-cut-china-dependence-report-567492-2026-11-14)

**【2026-11-14】Nutraplus India's assets auctioned by its bank, API operations halted, going concern void** — The board approved September-quarter results on 14 Nov: revenue nil for a third consecutive quarter, property, plant and equipment and capital work-in-progress both at zero, negative equity of ₹4.91 crore and ₹8.54 crore payable to creditors other than micro and small enterprises; the company said all plant and equipment were disposed of through a bank auction and key finance and production staff had left, with no restructuring plan. — Source: [Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/nutraplus-india-limited-business-closure-announcement-cmwgedu2d001y2rpbtrc2u9mq)【Source: board resolution 2026-11-14 (exchange filing)】
