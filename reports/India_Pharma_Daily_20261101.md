## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-31】Residual shelf-life threshold for imported drugs standardised at 12 months, replacing the 60% rule (updated)** — The Health Ministry amended Section 31 of the Drugs Rules: a minimum residual shelf life of 12 months is now required at the time of import, and the earlier "60% of labelled shelf life" threshold is withdrawn; biologicals and radiopharmaceuticals remain at 60% given their stability profile. The amendment was finalised after a Drugs Technical Advisory Board recommendation, following the June draft. The stated rationale is to cut supply-chain medicine wastage and improve inventory usability. The same 60% rule also applies to drugs exported from India, and industry is lobbying for a downward revision. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy-and-regulations/centre-eases-shelf-life-norms-for-imported-drugs/135397354)【Source: Rule 31 amendment, gazette 2026-10-28; draft G.S.R. 505(E) 2026-06-22】

**【2026-10-31】NPPA trade-margin list takes effect: 484 of 643 packs cut, originator prices unchanged (updated)** — Based on company submissions up to 23 October, 484 of 643 revised MRP entries were reduced and 159 left unchanged; the average cut across all entries was 38.22% and 50.78% among those actually reduced. All 17 AstraZeneca, 14 Novartis, 12 Roche and 10 Eli Lilly entries were unchanged, while Samarth cut 40 entries by 70.5% on average, RPG 23 by 70.9%, Reliance 25 by 69.6%, and Hetero 57 of 59 by 57.4%. New prices apply from 9 November. — Source: [Pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191648&sid=2)【Source: NPPA TMR notification 2026-10-17; revised list 2026-10-28】

**【2026-10-29】Chief Economic Adviser says "2026 resembles 1991", lists APIs among critical inputs to be de-imported** — Speaking at an ISID-IDRA conference, he set three parallel goals — import substitution, strategic resilience and strategic indispensability — naming crude oil, industrial metals, fertilisers, APIs, rare earths and battery chemicals as categories needing lower import dependence, and urging strategic buffers and diversified sourcing. Industries receiving policy support should also demonstrate export competitiveness or price parity with imports. — Source: [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/economy/2026-resembles-1991-why-cea-v-anantha-nageswaran-wants-india-to-rethink-its-growth-strategy-what-he-said-11793372293114.html)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-31】SEC recommends import and marketing approval for Boehringer Ingelheim's nerandomilast, with Phase IV condition** — The pulmonary subject expert committee on 14 October found an unmet medical need in progressive pulmonary fibrosis and recommended import and marketing of the 9 mg and 18 mg film-coated tablets with a local Phase III waiver, on condition that a Phase IV protocol be filed with CDSCO within three months of approval. The molecule is the only approved preferential PDE4B inhibitor and is already approved in the US, EU, UK and Japan. — Source: [Pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191628&sid=1)【Source: SEC minutes 2026-10-14】

**【2026-10-30】STRIDES PHARMA Q2 revenue up 17.1%; ex-US business up 31% and overtakes the US for the first time** — Revenue was ₹1,430 crore (about USD 151 million), with ex-US markets at ₹696 crore, up 31% and ahead of the US at ₹686 crore (+9%). EBITDA rose 8.4% to ₹251 crore at a 17.6% margin (19% a year earlier) and gross margin fell 180 bps. Management cited higher raw material, freight and operating costs arising from geopolitical disruption; net debt rose ₹211 crore in H1 on an elongated cash cycle and higher inventory. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/strides-pharmas-ex-us-business-surges-31-overtakes-us-for-first-time-22275)【Source: company results release 2026-10-30】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-31】SUN PHARMA wins CDSCO approval for oral semaglutide, API sourced from China's Huadong group** — The once-daily tablet is therapeutically equivalent to Novo Nordisk's Rybelsus and covers 3, 7 and 14 mg for type-2 diabetes; it is an r-DNA product to be made at the Kamrup plant in Assam, with the API supplied by Hangzhou Zhongmeihuadong Pharmaceutical. India's GLP-1 market (injectables plus orals) has grown from ₹950 crore in 2025 to ₹2,700 crore, with Torrent holding nearly 90% of the oral segment. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/drug-approvals-and-launches/sun-pharma-wins-cdsco-nod-for-semaglutide-pill/135385080)【Source: CDSCO marketing approval 2026-10-31】

**【2026-10-31】ALEMBIC wins final FDA approval for venetoclax tablets as first Paragraph IV filer with 180-day exclusivity** — The 10, 50 and 100 mg strengths are therapeutically equivalent to AbbVie's Venclexta and are indicated for chronic lymphocytic leukaemia, small lymphocytic lymphoma and older or comorbidity-burdened AML patients ineligible for intensive induction. As a substantially complete first Paragraph IV applicant, Alembic may qualify for 180-day generic exclusivity. IQVIA puts the US market at USD 1,584 million for the 12 months to June 2026; cumulative ANDA approvals now total 248 (228 final, 20 tentative). — Source: [Alembic Pharmaceuticals](https://alembicpharmaceuticals.com/webfiles/media/2026-2027/press-release-venetoclax-tablets-10-mg-50-mg-and-100-mg.pdf)【Source: company release 2026-10-31】
