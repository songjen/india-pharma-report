## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-19】Pharma PLI enables domestic output of 1,931 products, incl. 191 bulk drugs made in India for the first time** — Official data: cumulative sales under the pharma PLI exceed ₹3.64 lakh crore; 41 products approved under the bulk drugs PLI, with production started for 18 APIs including Penicillin G, clavulanic acid, dexamethasone, prednisolone, levofloxacin, ofloxacin, norfloxacin, atorvastatin, telmisartan, carbamazepine and diclofenac sodium; ~55,000 tpa capacity created across 26 critical APIs; 48 projects approved, cumulative investment ₹5,070.45 crore, incentives disbursed ₹87.70 crore. — Source: [Pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191332&sid=1)

**【2026-10-19】DoP: RPTUAS upgrade subsidy nearly exhausted, 301 units approved for ₹300.48 crore** — Under the Revamped Pharmaceutical Technology Upgradation Assistance Scheme (outlay ₹300.10 crore) for revised Schedule M / WHO-GMP upgrades, 301 applications worth ₹300.48 crore have been approved, close to the ceiling; 157 units have completed upgrades and obtained compliance certificates, 110 projects are under implementation and 4 sanctions were cancelled. DoP asked industry bodies to ensure complete documentation for faster disbursal. — Source: [Pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191336&sid=1)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-19】India drug exports up 4.9% to $16.13 bn in H1 FY27; pharma imports $4.773 bn, up 2.48%** — Preliminary commerce ministry data: April–September drug exports $16.13 bn vs $15.38 bn a year earlier; Q2 up 3.04% to $8.035 bn and September up 4.7% to $2.744 bn. Pharma imports $4.773 bn, with Q2 down 4.31% and September down 0.19%. FY26 exports were $31.12 bn, up only 2.13% versus 9.39% in FY25. — Source: [Pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191333&sid=1)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-20】ANUH PHARMA commissions Tarapur Unit F-10 API plant (update)** — The company announced commissioning of its API facility at Plot F-10/1, MIDC Tarapur; it had earlier acquired the site from Vanquest for ₹72.9 crore, adding about 525 tonnes per year of capacity — its third API production block since 2024. — Source: [Anuh Pharma](https://www.anuhpharma.com/)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-20】

**【2026-10-20】SUN PHARMA to raise ₹10,000 crore via five-year bonds to refinance Organon bridge loan** — Two five-year tranches totalling ₹10,000 crore (about $1.05 bn), including a ₹3,000 crore greenshoe: first tranche of ₹3,000 crore with a put option at the end of 17 months; second tranche of at least ₹5,000 crore plus ₹2,000 crore greenshoe on a bullet structure; coupons of 8.05% and 8.20%, with book-building on Thursday. Proceeds part-refinance the dollar bridge loan taken for the Organon acquisition. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/sun-pharma-to-issue-over-five-year-bonds-bankers-say/135059850)

**【2026-10-20】SUN PHARMA's UNLOXCYT gains NCCN Category 2A preferred designation** — The updated US NCCN guidelines list cosibelimab-ipdl (UNLOXCYT) as a Category 2A preferred option for locally advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma in patients who are not candidates for curative surgery or radiation, supporting US market access for the product. — Source: [Sun Pharma](https://www.sunpharma.com/media/press-releases)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-20】
