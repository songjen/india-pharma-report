## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-04】Health Ministry proposes immediate licence suspension for NSQ drugs on five critical parameters** — Current rules require a show-cause notice before suspension or cancellation, leaving a gap in which the drug stays in circulation; the draft lets the Central Licensing Approving Authority or State Licensing Authority suspend the manufacturing licence immediately on a Government Analyst's report, with revocation only after an effective root cause analysis and verified CAPA; scope covers assay, dissolution, related substances, impurities and sterility, and Form 13 reports go to DCGI and the state authority. Follows the 65th DCC meeting (2024-12-20) and 92nd DTAB meeting (2025-04-24); comments open for 30 days. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2332796)【Source: PIB PRID 2332796, 2026-11-04 20:57 IST】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-05】FPME urges DCGI to ease export NOC delays, says DCC decision pending over six months** — The 69th Drugs Consultative Committee (June 24) proposed a sub-committee on exporters' concerns but no follow-up has occurred, and manufacturing permissions for export-only products remain stalled; FPME asks that formulations listed in the Indian Pharmacopoeia and National Formulary of India be treated as sanctioned for export and exempted from the NOC, with the central NOC confined to unapproved drugs, non-approved FDCs and banned products; it also flags omissions and wrong names/strengths in CDSCO's online approved-drug repository. — Source: [Pharmabiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=191734&sid=1)

**【2026-11-04】Delhi High Court issues notice over delay in ranitidine safety review** — The court sought replies from the Centre, CDSCO, DTAB and ICMR on a petition seeking a time-bound review; the plea notes the US, EU, Australia, Canada, UK and Singapore all acted after the 2019 NDMA alerts, while India referred the matter to a committee and an ICMR study in April 2025, the three-month deadline lapsed, and as of October 7, 2026 CDSCO still said the report was awaited; next hearing in February. — Source: [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/hc-seeks-replies-on-plea-to-speed-up-ranitidine-safety-review-101793816650065.html)
