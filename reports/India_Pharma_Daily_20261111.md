## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-09】Clavulanic Acid anti-dumping case: PUC/PCN comment window extended a second time to 21 Nov; DGTR states no further extension (update)** — DGTR issued a second extension for comments on the scope of the Product Under Consideration and Product Control Number methodology in the anti-dumping investigation on Clavulanic Acid (potassium clavulanate and its amine salt) from China PR and the European Union: the deadline moves from 30 Oct to **21 Nov**, with an explicit statement that trade-remedy investigations are time-bound and **no further extension will be granted**; filings must go through the SETU portal. The case was initiated on 30 Sep, Case No. **AD/OI/054/2026**, covering a key β-lactam intermediate. — Source: [DGTR case page](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/en/anti-dumping-cases/initiation-anti-dumping-investigation-concerning-imports-clavulanic-acid-also)【Source: DGTR notice F.No. 6/51/2026-DGTR, 2026-11-09】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-10】Shengnuo Biotech refunds US customer USD 24.96 mn after FDA import alert 66-40, pivots peptide APIs to India and other markets** — Chengdu Shengnuo Biotechnology disclosed that, after its subsidiary Shengnuo Pharmaceutical was placed on FDA Import Alert 66-40 on 19 Sep, it signed a return-and-refund agreement with US customer A: partial peptide API from two 2025 orders is being returned and **USD 24,959,600** refunded in three instalments, with an estimated hit of **RMB 163 mn** to revenue and **RMB 84.25 mn** to net profit attributable to shareholders for the year. The company said it will accelerate registrations in Europe, India, Russia, Korea and Southeast Asia to offset the US standstill. — Source: [Beijing News](https://www.163.com/dy/article/LBCUCR4C0512D3VJ.html)【Source: company announcement 2026-11-09】

**【2026-11-09】MOREPEN enters US prescription generics with desloratadine, extending API strengths downstream** — Per its press release, the company launched its first US prescription generic, desloratadine tablets USP 5 mg, in 100- and 500-count packs through its wholly owned US subsidiary; it has supplied desloratadine API to the US for years. It has also filed its first ANDA, for sitagliptin phosphate tablets 25/50/100 mg, currently under review. The company plans to extend from API and CDMO capabilities into formulation development and US distribution, and is evaluating HPAPIs and oncology. — Source: [Morepen press release](https://morepen.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/investor/uploads/1794198953502-SEinPressrelease91126.pdf)【Source: company announcement 2026-11-09, NSE/BSE filing】

**【2026-11-10】Vector white paper: India's pharma "compliance spiral" — 80% say open investigations delay batch release** — A survey of several FDA-approved Indian formulation plants found every plant had a backlog of incidents awaiting closure, with some investigations running **six months**; nearly **80%** of respondents said open investigations directly delayed batch releases; senior leaders spend up to **27%** of their time on backlogs; human error accounted for **56%** of reported incidents; operators make an average of **120 entries** per shift in batch production records, and **80%** of supervisors oversee five or more lines per shift. Targeted interventions could cut new incidents by 30–50% and closure timelines by 20–40%. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/indias-130-billion-pharma-dream-faces-a-hidden-bottleneck-study-22358)

**【2026-11-10】BDR PHARMA buys Acme's oral solid formulations business, adding hormone and thyroid CDMO capacity** — BDR acquired a controlling stake in Acme Formulation's oral solid formulations business from shareholders led by PAG; terms were not disclosed. Founded in 2005, Acme provides contract development and manufacturing for thyroid products, female sex hormones and complex oral solids, exporting to more than 40 countries; PAG took control in 2021 for **₹1,054 crore (about USD 112 mn)**. PAG and the founder retain the sterile injectables platform Immacule, which runs two Nalagarh plants with capacity above 200 million units a year. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/mergers-and-acquisitions/bdr-pharma-buys-acmes-oral-solid-formulations-biz/135696891)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-11-10】LAURUS takes 60% of Canada's Galenvs for USD 3.75 mn, plus 52% of SI&C Resins** — The company disclosed it will acquire **60%** of Galenvs Science for **USD 3.75 mn**; the target specialises in custom oligonucleotide synthesis and functional magnetic nanoparticles and reported USD 1.6 mn revenue in calendar 2025. It will also buy **52%** of Hyderabad-based SI&C Resins for **₹32 crore** in cash, bringing peptide solid-phase synthesis resins and chromatography stationary phases in-house; SI&C will build a large-scale plant for peptide resins and chromatography gels. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/mergers-and-acquisitions/bdr-pharma-buys-acmes-oral-solid-formulations-biz/135696891)
