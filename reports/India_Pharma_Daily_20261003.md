## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-30】DGFT extends RoDTEP export incentive scheme until 31 December** — DGFT Notification No. 41/2026-27 extends the validity of the Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products (RoDTEP) scheme to 31 December 2026. The scheme grants transferable duty credit scrips for embedded domestic taxes on exported goods and covers a wide range of export products including pharmaceuticals; the notification does not alter existing rates or filing procedures. — Source: [DGFT](https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=notification)【Source: DGFT Notification No. 41/2026-27, 2026-09-30】

**【2026-10-01】DGFT seeks pharma export compliance cost inputs to expand TRACE Annexure-I** — DGFT, through Pharmexcil, has asked member exporters to submit details of product registration, certification, inspection and testing charges not currently covered under Annexure-I of the TRACE framework (Trade Regulations, Accreditation & Compliance Enablement, under the Export Promotion Mission). Pharmexcil urged priority submission and identified three uncovered cost heads: overseas inspection fees, testing charges, and product registration and certification fees. — Source: [Pharmexcil](https://pharmexcil.com/circulars/viewcirculars/15878/0bca3e9a30e13bed306b929e021c2c9c.html)·[PharmaBiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=190976&sid=1)【Source: Pharmexcil Circular No.15878; DGFT/TRACE notice 2026-10-01】

**【2026-10-01】DTAB recommends amending Second Schedule of Medical Devices Rules on Free Sale Certificate fees** — The Drugs Technical Advisory Board held that the existing provision requiring ₹1,000 per "category" of medical device for a Free Sale Certificate is ambiguous and generates repeated queries, and recommended charging the fee per medical device under each manufacturing licence instead. The recommendation appears in the minutes of the 94th DTAB meeting and is intended to end fee disputes facing exporters. — Source: [PharmaBiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=190979&sid=1)【Source: Minutes of the 94th DTAB meeting】

**【2026-10-01】Two DCC items: Merbromin exemption review and pharma machinery disposal oversight** — The Drugs Consultative Committee (meeting of 18 September) reached two positions: first, on the Schedule K exemption for the mercury-based topical antiseptic Merbromin (Mercurochrome), given the Minamata Convention's phase-out of mercury-added consumer products and the availability of alternatives, the opinion and manufacturing/usage data of the Directorate General of Health Services have been sought before deciding whether to withdraw the exemption; second, Rajasthan's drug regulator has been directed to submit a full-chain framework covering supply, ownership transfer, decommissioning and post-disposal scrap monitoring of pharmaceutical machinery, to block equipment from closed plants from entering spurious drug production. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/drug-regulator-may-review-machinery-disposal-at-non-compliant-pharma-units-126100101060_1.html)【Source: DCC meeting minutes (2026-09-18)】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-01】India's August pharma exports up 3.8% to $2.606 billion, imports down 7.9%** — Preliminary Ministry of Commerce data: August exports of drugs and pharmaceuticals reached $2.606 billion (vs $2.510 billion a year earlier); cumulative exports for the first five months of FY27 stood at $13.39 billion, up 4.94% year on year. August imports of medicinal and pharmaceutical products fell 7.9% to $719.65 million; five-month imports rose 3.02% to $4.005 billion. In rupee terms, five-month exports grew 15.94% to ₹1,27,328 crore and imports 13.81%. — Source: [PharmaBiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=190975&sid=1)【Source: Ministry of Commerce & Industry preliminary data, 2026-10-01】

**【2026-10-02】Zydus chairman Pankaj Patel urges India to join ICH and restore weighted R&D deduction** — Speaking at the inaugural Bharat Life Sciences Awards, Zydus Lifesciences chairman Pankaj Patel said India should become a full member of the ICH to benchmark product quality and clinical data and win the trust of global companies, and called for restoring the weighted deduction of up to 200% on R&D expenditure, noting Indian companies spend only 4–5% of revenue on R&D and need to double it. He also argued the government should become the "first customer" for local innovation, including reimbursement for biosimilars. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/india-should-join-ich-restore-weighted-rd-tax-deduction-to-drive-innovation-zydus-pankaj-patel/134629474)【Source: Bharat Life Sciences Awards address, 2026-10-01】

**【2026-10-01】Zenex Animal Health completes acquisition of SAVA Healthcare's companion animal business** — Multiples-backed Zenex Animal Health announced completion of its acquisition of the SAVAVET-branded companion animal business from SAVA Healthcare. SAVAVET has around 70 brands spanning parasiticides, antibiotics, cardiac care, pain management, dermatology and wellness, distributed through about 165 stockists and more than 6,000 veterinarians; it is Zenex's third acquisition since partnering with Multiples (after Ayurvet and VievePharm). SAVA Healthcare retains its farm animal business and will continue contract manufacturing agreed products for Zenex. — Source: [PharmaBiz](https://www.pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=190973&sid=2)·[Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/zenex-animal-health-complete-acquisition-of-sava-healthcare/)【Source: Company announcement 2026-10-01】

## 3. Key Client Updates
