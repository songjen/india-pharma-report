## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-10-23】Department of Pharmaceuticals sets up three-member panel to review pharma marketing rules and assess whether UCPMP needs a statutory framework** — The DoP has constituted a three-member committee to examine the effectiveness of the current framework governing pharmaceutical marketing practices and to assess whether stronger regulatory or statutory provisions are needed; chaired by NITI Aayog Member M. Srinivas, with former Health Secretary Preeti Sudan and Medanta Chairman Naresh Trehan as members; the panel must report to the DoP within two months of its first meeting, covering the enforcement, monitoring and penalty adequacy of UCPMP 2024 as well as transparency and accountability mechanisms. — Source: [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/centre-sets-up-panel-to-review-pharma-marketing-rules-examine-need-for-stronger-legal-framework/article71616369.ece)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-23】Biodeal enters CDMO space via Biological E sterile injectables plant; carbapenem line already operational** — The Paonta Sahib site in Himachal Pradesh spans 14 acres with about 39,000 sq m of built-up area and roughly 135 million units of annual capacity; **the carbapenem line has been validated and is operational under Biodeal's ownership**, with pre-filled syringes and lyophilised formats to be added and expansion into ferric carboxymaltose and heparin, alongside EU GMP filings; the company serves over 600 clients in 75 countries, with exports at about 25% of revenue. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/pharmaceuticals/from-nasal-delivery-to-sterile-injectables-biodeal-pharmaceuticals-expansion-plans/articleshow/135137663.cms)【Source: Biodeal announcement 2026-09 (acquisition of Paonta Sahib plant)】

**【2026-10-23】Indian CDMOs pivot to peptides, ADCs and sterile injectables; Sai Life to triple capacity in five years** — Sai Life Sciences plans to expand from about 700 cubic metres to more than 2,000 cubic metres, adding roughly 500 cubic metres in the next 12 months and building a new site scalable to about 1,300 cubic metres, prioritising peptides, ADCs and oligonucleotides; OneSource Specialty Pharma has committed more than USD 75 million of a USD 100 million capex programme to drug-device combinations and sterile fill-finish, including GLP-1 support; McKinsey estimates India could capture 8–10% of global CDMO outsourcing by 2033. — Source: [India Pharma Outlook](https://www.indiapharmaoutlook.com/news/indian-cdmos-bet-big-on-complex-products-for-growth-nwid-6002.html)

**【2026-10-23】CDSCO flags 330 NSQ drug samples and one spurious drug in September; central labs account for 42** — In routine regulatory surveillance, Central Drugs Laboratories declared 42 samples and State Drugs Testing Laboratories 288 samples Not of Standard Quality, while one sample from Delhi was found spurious (brand name of another company used by unauthorised manufacturers) and is under investigation; the August figure was 220 (29 central, 191 state). — Source: [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/cdsco-flags-330-substandard-drug-samples-one-spurious-drug-in-september-alert/article71614905.ece)
