## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-11-07】Anti-cancer trade margin cap finalised: new prices for 137 drugs from 9 Nov, ₹3,502 crore annual saving (updated)** — The Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers said 76 companies have filed revised MRPs for 1,225 formulations covering 121 drugs; 1,031 formulations were cut, 600 brands by more than 50%, with the steepest cut at 96.45%. Of the 137 drugs, 10 to 15 are not yet launched in India and will be covered on launch. Under S.O. 5690(E) the trade margin is capped at 30%; companies may still take annual increases under Paragraph 20(1) of DPCO 2013, with state drug controllers enforcing compliance. — Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2333949)【Source: S.O. 5690(E), 2026-10-17; PIB PRID 2333949】

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-07】India pushes API self-reliance: FY25 bulk drug and API imports at USD 4.35 bn, China 73.71%** — Pharma exports were USD 30.47 bn in FY2024-25 and USD 8.10 bn in Q1 FY27, up 6.8%; the pharma PLI scheme generated about 115,000 jobs by March 2026; Jubilant Ingrevia invested roughly ₹2,000 crore over three years across 35 technology platforms; the domestic market grew 17.1% in September, the strongest in three years. CPHI India runs 23-25 November in New Delhi. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/healthcare/pharma-industry-steps-up-api-investments-to-cut-import-dependence-move-up-value-chain/articleshow/135636196.cms)

**【2026-11-06】US FDA issued 39 OAI actions to Indian sites in FY2025; data integrity and hygiene still top findings** — The agency's deputy country director told a Mumbai industry meeting that inspections still find truckloads of analytical, manufacturing and storage records discarded, some shredded, forcing studies to be repeated; companies were urged to scrutinise suppliers, contractors and third parties. In FY2025, 149 inspections required voluntary action and 53 drew no action. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/us-fda-flags-data-integrity-lapses-poor-conditions-at-india-drug-units/135591083)【Source: FDA official remarks, 2026-11-06】

**【2026-11-06】Maharashtra FDA audited 411 allopathic plants from April to October, suspending 63 licences and cancelling 31** — Inspections also covered 354 Ayurvedic units, 125 cosmetics firms and 52 Class A/B high-risk device makers; in October the agency seized drugs worth ₹26.8 lakh for unlicensed storage and spurious drugs above ₹1 lakh. The drive applies the revised Schedule M as its benchmark, while the Centre has stepped up joint inspections of sterile and cough syrup plants. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulatory-update/maha-fda-suspends-63-drug-manufacturing-licenses-seizes-stock-worth-over-26-lakh/135592097)

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-11-04】ZYDUS completes acquisition of Sterling Biotech's API business, taking over fermentation-based assets for ₹84 crore** — The slump sale on a cash-free, debt-free basis transfers all API assets for a lump sum of ₹84 crore, including lovastatin and fermentation-derived daunorubicin, doxorubicin and epirubicin, plus the Masar village site in Gujarat, with synergies to the existing Dabhasa plant. DifGen leads development, regulatory, manufacturing and supply; Alembic will make selected products and commercialise in the US, with first launches expected from FY28 and reserved capacity at DifGen's US sites. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/alembic-pharma-expands-us-complex-generics-portfolio-via-difgen-partnership-22328)【Source: company disclosure 2026-11-05】
