## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-11-02】India's medicine sales grow in double digits for a fourth straight quarter; September quarter up 12% to ₹70,180 crore** — Value growth of 12% (about USD 7.39 bn), after 11% in each of the two preceding quarters and 10% in Oct–Dec 2025; growth is concentrated in oncology, cardiology and diabetology, while anti-infectives showed almost no growth. Over the past two years CDSCO ordered shutdowns of hundreds of non-compliant small and medium units, and more than 60% of roughly 8,500 SME pharma units were feared to face closure under the revised Schedule M; share shifted to larger players. The GST cut on medicines from 12% to 5% also contributed. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/healthcare/biotech/pharmaceuticals/indias-medicine-sales-grow-in-double-digits-for-four-straight-quarters-led-by-chronic-care-drugs/articleshow/135435486.cms)【Source: PharmaTrac data for the September 2026 quarter】

**【2026-11-01】RPG LIFE SCIENCES to deploy another ₹500 crore on API acquisitions; API business targeted to grow 25% a year over five years** — Q2 revenue ₹211.6 crore (about USD 22.28 mn), up 16.5% and above ₹200 crore for the first time; adjusted EBITDA ₹50.1 crore, up 15%, margin 23.7%, gross margin up 260 bps, and the Naprosyn brand crossed ₹100 crore in sales. The API business has been transferred to wholly owned RPG Active Pharma with InvAscent as partner; the company plans to deploy about ₹500 crore from a ₹700 crore pool within 12 months on another API asset, having already used about ₹200 crore for Actis and Raghav, which expanded capacity fivefold and doubled the customer base. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/people/rpg-life-sciences-eyes-another-acquisition-with-500-crore-capital-pool-126110100615_1.html)【Source: company results release 2026-10-31】

**【2026-10-30】India pharma M&A value falls to USD 500 mn in the September quarter, down 80% year on year; private equity shifts to CDMO and injectable capacity** — 11 deals in the quarter versus 17 in the previous one, with average deal size USD 13.5 mn versus about USD 42 mn; the largest was Evonile (formerly Novartis India) buying Pfizer's Minipress trademarks for USD 132 mn, while Biophore acquired Par Health's sterile injectable plant for USD 43 mn. PE deals rose to 15, led by RMB Capital's ₹385 crore (about USD 40.55 mn) investment in Biodeal to fund the purchase of Biological E's injectable facility. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/mergers-and-acquisitions/pharma-ma-deal-value-volumes-declines-in-sep-quarter-pe-buyouts-rebound/135340143)【Source: Grant Thornton Bharat deal tracker, Q3 2026】

**【2026-10-31】IND-SWIFT Q2 revenue up 50% and operating EBITDA nearly fivefold as the API-to-formulations shift pays off** — Revenue ₹234.89 crore (about USD 24.74 mn), up 50.15%; operating EBITDA ₹42.33 crore with margin rising to 18.02% from 5.46%; net profit ₹31.03 crore, up 3.5 times. Raw material cost fell to 45.15% of revenue from 49.38%, with formulations and CDMO contracts contributing incremental business. — Source: [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/ind-swift-laboratories-q2-fy27-revenue-jumps-50-operating-ebitda-fivefold-22286)【Source: company results release 2026-10-31】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-31】GRANULES' Gagillapur formulations plant in Hyderabad receives FDA Form 483 with 11 observations** — The cGMP inspection ran from 19 to 30 October and was the site's first FDA inspection since the warning letter of 26 February 2025, which followed an August–September 2024 inspection. The company said the observations concern investigations and change control, process and equipment qualification, visual inspection controls and documentation practices, with no data-integrity or cross-contamination findings, no impact on manufacturing or distribution, and a response to be filed within 15 days; Q2 results were released the same day. — Source: [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/granules-india-share-price-gagilapur-unit-11-usfda-observations-newsbreak-confirmed-impact-conclusion-reaction-20023880.htm)【Source: company exchange disclosure 2026-10-31】

**【2026-10-31】SUN PHARMA calls eye-drug production halt and recall "voluntary", expects supplies to resume shortly (update)** — Managing Director Kirti Ganorkar told analysts on the Q2 earnings call that 11 ophthalmology products were voluntarily recalled and the manufacturing stop was a voluntary temporary cessation, with the regulator kept informed, and that supplies would resume "shortly" after remediation. Reuters reported the same day that central and state drug regulators inspected the Assam sterile facility, flagged several quality-management lapses, ordered a production halt on 20 August and sought details on 332 batches of ophthalmic suspensions. The company began a voluntary recall in July after a 19 June safety alert from the All India Ophthalmological Society. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/sun-pharma-terms-eye-drugs-production-halt-a-voluntary-call/135381508)
