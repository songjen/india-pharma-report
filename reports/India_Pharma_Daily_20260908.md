## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-07】Regulatory data exclusivity debate heats up: Goyal pushes regulatory convergence; OPPI seeks 10-yr NCE / 12-yr biologics exclusivity** — Commerce Minister Goyal, inaugurating Bharat Health Global Expo 2026, called for India's pharma regulation to "converge" with developed markets to attract clinical trials, R&D and investment; MNCs are lobbying for regulatory data exclusivity, with OPPI seeking 10 years for new chemical entities (NCEs) and 12 years for biologics. India currently lets generic firms cite innovator trial data for bioequivalence. Innovators say lack of protection erodes R&D returns; the health ministry reportedly fears delayed generics and higher prices. Goyal reiterated India's need to rebuild supply-chain resilience in APIs and key starting materials (KSMs); 100 new industrial parks are underway. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/need-to-increase-regulatory-convergence-to-unlock-investments-innovation-in-pharma-goyal/133862894)

**【2026-09-07】CDSCO alerts states over counterfeit Abhayrab rabies vaccine (batch KE25012): seized product misbranded, substandard** — In a public notice dated September 5, CDSCO asked state drug regulators to stay vigilant over counterfeit Abhayrab rabies vaccine batch KE25012: Delhi Police seized the batch on April 22 from an unlicensed premises (involving rabies vaccine, insulin, hepatitis B vaccine etc., value ≈ ₹6 crore ≈ US$0.63M; 4 arrested); the Central Drugs Laboratory (CDL, Kasauli) found the sample "Not of Standard Quality" and misbranded, failing the potency test. Manufacturer Indian Immunologicals (IIL) says the seized vials were not made by it — 17 differences vs. retention samples (labels, QR codes, cap colour) — and confirmed the batch was never exported. — Source: [Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/health-wellness/watch-out-for-counterfeit-batch-of-rabies-vaccine-drug-regulator-to-states-10865044/) · [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/news/industry/pharma/dcgi-alerts-states-after-misbranded-abhayrab-rabies-vaccine-batch-fails-potency-test-178673)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-08】Sun Pharma joins US MFN drug-pricing pact — first Indian drugmaker to sign; pledges API donations & US manufacturing** — The White House announced on Aug 31 that nine more companies (Alcon/Astellas/BeOne/BridgeBio/CSL/Kyowa Kirin/Sun Pharma/Teva/UCB) signed Most-Favoured-Nation (MFN) pricing agreements, bringing signatories to 26 covering ~89% of the US branded-drug market, with a combined commitment of at least US$19.6 bn in near-term US manufacturing investment. Sun Pharma is the **first Indian drugmaker to sign**: besides tying US prices to the lowest among 8 comparator countries, it pledged to donate APIs to the US Strategic API Reserve (SAPIR) — 71.4 t clindamycin and 6.75 t doxycycline. Pharmexcil Chairman Namit Joshi calls the deal a "win-win template"; analysts flag Dr Reddy's, Aurobindo, Lupin and Zydus as companies to watch — "localised US manufacturing in exchange for tariff relief and policy certainty" may become the new template for Indian pharma's US business. — Source: [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/business/market/story/sun-pharma-us-deal-tariff-relief-lower-drug-prices-american-manufacturing-2989690-2026-09-08) · [PharmExec](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/your-questions-answered-biggest-biotech-stories-week)

**【2026-09-07】Pharmarack August data: India pharma market +10.7% YoY; Mounjaro becomes India's #1 selling brand** — IPM sales in Aug 2026 were ₹23,272 crore (≈US$2.45 bn), +10.7% YoY, but prescription volumes rose only 1.1% (3-month low); rolling annual sales ≈ ₹2.59 lakh crore (≈US$27.3 bn). Cardiovascular +15.1%, anti-diabetes +15.0%; anti-infectives only +2.7% and respiratory +2.2% (weak). Lilly's Mounjaro (tirzepatide) posted rolling-12-month sales of ₹1,256 crore (≈US$132M, +502% YoY), becoming India's best-selling brand for the first time; India's GLP-1 market totals ₹2,333 crore (≈US$246M) — tirzepatide 62%, semaglutide 34%, with injectables displacing orals. — Source: [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/mounjaro-becomes-indias-top-selling-drug-as-patients-shift-to-glp-1-injections/article71438811.ece)

**【2026-09-06】Fosun Pharma sells 6% of Gland Pharma: raises ≈US$295M, remains controlling shareholder** — Fosun Pharma announced its Singapore subsidiary sold 9.897M Gland Pharma shares (~6.00% of equity) via block trade on Sept 4 for pre-tax consideration of ₹279.96 bn (≈US$295M), at ₹2,828.78/share (~2.72% discount to prior close). ## 4. Risk Alerts

- **Softening India anti-infective demand signal**: Aug 2026 IPM anti-infective sales grew only +2.7% and respiratory +2.2%, with overall prescription growth at 1.1% (3-month low). Suppliers of anti-infective APIs/intermediates to India should track order momentum (Source: [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/mounjaro-becomes-indias-top-selling-drug-as-patients-shift-to-glp-1-injections/article71438811.ece)).
- **"Localisation-for-tariff-relief" model spillover risk**: Sun Pharma's US MFN deal (incl. SAPIR donations of clindamycin/doxycycline APIs) — if Dr Reddy's/Aurobindo/Lupin follow, India's top-tier exports to the US shift toward "US-made + API reserve donations", squeezing traditional "made-in-India → US" generic/API trade volumes and indirectly affecting Chinese API suppliers' order shares (Source: [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/business/market/story/sun-pharma-us-deal-tariff-relief-lower-drug-prices-american-manufacturing-2989690-2026-09-08)).
- **US trade-remedy enforcement against Chinese chemicals/additives tightening**: USITC signed the final affirmative L-lysine CVD/AD determinations vs China on Sept 4 (AD 73.55%–139.83%, CVD 48.21%–82.11%); the final notice published in the Federal Register Sept 8 with Commerce issuing the orders. On Sept 1 Commerce also initiated an anti-circumvention inquiry into alkyl phosphate esters ("assembled in Thailand, Chinese intermediates"). Transshipment-evasion routes for Chinese chemicals are closing; export compliance review for Chinese-origin APIs/intermediates/additives is increasingly important (Source: [Federal Register](https://thefederalregister.org/documents/2026-18195/l-lysine-from-china)).
