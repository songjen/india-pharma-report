## 1. Government Pharma Policy

**【2026-09-07】Regulatory data-exclusivity battle heats up: Goyal pushes regulatory convergence; OPPI seeks 10-yr NCE / 12-yr biologics exclusivity** — Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal, speaking at the inaugural session of Bharat Health Global Expo 2026, called on India's pharma regulators to pursue "regulatory convergence" with developed markets to attract investment in clinical trials, R&D and new product introductions. Meanwhile, multinational innovators are lobbying for exclusivity over regulatory dossiers (regulatory data exclusivity): the Organisation of Pharmaceutical Producers of India (OPPI) has sought 10 years of exclusivity for new chemical entities (NCEs) and 12 years for biologics. India currently lets generic companies rely on new-drug clinical-trial data to establish bioequivalence (the US grants only 5 years for an NCE); innovators argue the lack of protection erodes R&D returns, while the Health Ministry is reported to oppose the move over concerns it would delay generic entry and raise drug costs. Goyal reiterated the need to rebuild supply-chain resilience in APIs and key starting materials (KSMs), noted that 100 new industrial parks are being prepared, that nine FTAs signed over the past four years cover 38 developed countries (combined GDP ≈ US$60 trillion), and that another 8–9 FTAs are under negotiation. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/need-to-increase-regulatory-convergence-to-unlock-investments-innovation-in-pharma-goyal/133862894)

**【2026-09-07】CDSCO alerts all states over counterfeit Abhayrab rabies vaccine (batch KE25012): seized vials found misbranded and Not of Standard Quality** — India's Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) has asked state drug controllers to keep strict vigilance over counterfeit Abhayrab rabies vaccine batch KE25012. Delhi Police seized the batch from an unlicensed premises on 22 April (seized products included rabies vaccine, insulin, hepatitis-B vaccine, human albumin and snake antivenom, worth about ₹6 crore ≈ US$0.63 million; four persons arrested); the Central Drugs Laboratory (CDL, Kasauli) found the sample "Not of Standard Quality" (NSQ) on potency and classified it as misbranded. Manufacturer Indian Immunologicals Ltd (IIL) stated on 4 September that the vials were not made by the company, citing 17 differences versus its genuine retention samples (labelling, artwork, QR-code verification, vial-cap colour, etc.), and confirmed the batch was never exported. — Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/health/misbranded-rabies-vaccines-seized-in-delhi-what-was-found-why-it-matters-126090700769_1.html)

## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-09-07】Pharmarack August data: Indian pharma market value +10.7%; Mounjaro becomes India's No. 1 brand** — According to Pharmarack, the Indian pharmaceutical market (IPM) posted August 2026 sales of ₹23,272 crore (≈US$2.46 billion), up 10.7% year on year, but prescription volume grew only 1.1% (a three-month low); growth was driven mainly by new launches and higher-priced products, with trailing-12-month sales of about ₹2.59 lakh crore (≈US$27.4 billion). By therapy, cardiac drugs rose 15.1% (₹3,229 crore ≈ US$341 million), anti-diabetes 15.0% (₹2,197 crore ≈ US$232 million) and pain/analgesics 11.3% (₹1,675 crore ≈ US$177 million), while anti-infectives (+2.7%) and respiratory drugs (+2.2%) stayed notably soft. Eli Lilly's Mounjaro (tirzepatide) became India's best-selling pharmaceutical brand for the first time, with trailing-12-month sales (to August) of ₹1,256 crore (≈US$133 million, +502% YoY), ahead of GSK's Augmentin (₹957 crore ≈ US$101 million) and USV's Glycomet GP (₹950 crore ≈ US$100 million). India's total GLP-1 market reached ₹2,333 crore (≈US$247 million), of which tirzepatide accounts for 62% and semaglutide 34%, with injectables increasingly displacing oral products. — Source: [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/mounjaro-becomes-indias-top-selling-drug-as-patients-shift-to-glp-1-injections/article71438811.ece)

**【2026-09-06】Fosun Pharma sells 6% of Gland Pharma for ≈US$296 million; remains controlling shareholder** — Fosun Pharma announced that its Singapore subsidiary sold 9.897 million Gland Pharma shares (≈6.00% of total share capital) on 4 September (India time) via block and open-market trades, for total consideration (pre-tax) of ₹27.996 billion (≈US$296 million), at an average of ₹2,828.78 per share — a 2.72% discount to the prior close; transfer is expected to complete on 7 September. Hyderabad-based Gland Pharma manufactures sterile injectables (with the US and Europe as its main revenue markets); Fosun took control in October 2017, and Gland listed on Indian exchanges in November 2019. — Source: [HKEX announcement (Fosun Pharma overseas regulatory announcement)](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0906/2026090600035_c.pdf)

## 4. Risk Alerts

- **Softening signal in Indian anti-infective demand**: In August, India's anti-infective sales grew only 2.7% and respiratory drugs 2.2%, while overall prescription volume growth slowed to 1.1% (three-month low). Suppliers exporting anti-infective APIs/intermediates to India should track near-term order momentum (Source: [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/mounjaro-becomes-indias-top-selling-drug-as-patients-shift-to-glp-1-injections/article71438811.ece)).
- **Tightening US trade-remedy enforcement on Chinese chemicals/additives**: The USITC signed final affirmative AD/CVD determinations on L-lysine from China on 4 September (AD 73.55%–139.83%, CVD 48.21%–82.11%); the final determinations publish in the Federal Register on 8 September, followed by Commerce's duty orders. Separately, on 1 September the US Commerce Department initiated a country-wide circumvention inquiry into alkyl phosphate esters assembled in Thailand from Chinese intermediates. This signals that trans-shipment routes for Chinese-origin chemicals are being closed off — exporters of Chinese-origin APIs/intermediates/additives should strengthen origin and trans-shipment compliance reviews (Sources: [Federal Register](https://thefederalregister.org/documents/2026-18195/l-lysine-from-china), [POTUS Watch Daily](https://www.potuswatchdaily.com/article/commerce-china-alkyl-ester-circumvention-inquiry)).
