## 2. Pharma Industry News

**【2026-10-12】Indian pharma shifts export mix away from a single US market; Europe and emerging markets become the incremental growth** — An Express Pharma feature reports that Indian drugmakers are re-weighting export portfolios by region. Pharmexcil Chairman Namit Joshi acknowledges the US ecosystem still leads by scale, while Shilpa Biologicals CEO Uday Harle says no single market replaces the US and the bigger opportunity is a diversified global portfolio as the value pool shifts toward biologics and advanced therapies. Registration fees, dossier licensing, samples and other market-entry costs accumulate before revenue begins, while distributor payment cycles can stretch over months, making market and partner selection as important as product selection. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/beyond-us-new-shores-new-bets/)

**【2026-10-14】NIPER Raebareli and AstraZeneca India partner to build out 25 clinical trial sites** — The National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research (NIPER), Raebareli, and AstraZeneca India signed an MoU, witnessed by Manoj Joshi, Secretary, Department of Pharmaceuticals, to assess and support 25 clinical trial sites in emerging research geographies with capability assessment, training and mentoring, and inspection readiness. The collaboration supports the Biopharma SHAKTI initiative's national target of 1,000 clinical trial sites, starting with oncology as proof of concept before extending to other therapeutic areas. — Source: [Express Pharma](https://www.expresspharma.in/niper-raebareli-astrazeneca-india-partner-to-expand-clinical-trial-sites-across-india/)

**【2026-10-13】Roche strikes licensing deal worth up to $1.16 bn with China's Rona Therapeutics** — Roche secured rights to an investigational RNAi obesity candidate targeting activin receptor-like kinase 7 (ALK7) from Chinese biotech Rona Therapeutics. Rona receives a $100 million upfront payment and is eligible for up to $1.06 billion in milestone payments plus tiered royalties on global net sales. — Source: [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/roche-strikes-obesity-drug-licensing-deal-worth-up-to-1-16-bln-with-chinese-rona-therapeutics/134919153)【Source: Rona Therapeutics announcement 2026-10-13】

## 3. Key Client Updates

**【2026-10-13】ZYDUS LIFESCIENCES Moraiya formulation plant in Ahmedabad closes USFDA inspection with 5 observations** — Zydus informed exchanges that the US FDA inspected its Moraiya (Ahmedabad) formulation plant from 5 to 13 October 2026 and the inspection closed with five observations; the company said it will work closely with the regulator to address them immediately and respond within stipulated timelines. The site supplies the US market and the outcome was disclosed under Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR rules. — Source: [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/zydus-lifesciences-share-price-usfda-moraiya-observations-bla-biosimilar-bernstein-buy-target-upside-factors-20011319.htm)【Source: company exchange disclosure 2026-10-13 (SEBI LODR Reg. 30)】

**【2026-10-14】BIOCON reaches global patent settlement with Genentech, securing earliest licensed launch dates for Pebrilzo (bPertuzumab)** — Biocon entered a global settlement and licence agreement with Genentech resolving all pending disputes in the US, Canada and other jurisdictions, enabling Pebrilzo (a Perjeta biosimilar) to launch in Q1 CY2027 in the EU and UK, Q3 CY2028 in the US, and during CY2026–2028 in other rest-of-world markets. Other terms remain confidential. Innovator Perjeta recorded over $3.6 billion in worldwide sales in 2025. — Source: [Biocon](https://www.biocon.com/biocon-secures-earliest-licensed-launch-dates-for-pebrilzo-bpertuzumab-following-global-patent-settlement-with-genentech/) · [ET Pharma](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/biocon-settles-perjeta-biosimilar-dispute-with-roche-rollout-to-start-from-q1-cy27/134937119)【Source: company announcement 2026-10-14】
